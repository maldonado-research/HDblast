# Targeted primary-source audit: fixed-geometry causal variance response

Prepared 2 October 2026 (America/Los_Angeles); retrieval timestamps in the
companion metadata are explicitly UTC. This is a bounded methods review of
five primary papers, based on the passages below. It is not an exhaustive
search, an external peer review, a priority claim, or a numerical result.
Article bodies, extracted text, and rendered pages are excluded from this
public payload.

The proposed calibration has substantial overlap with established methods:
retarded composite-operator response, one-sided finite-part logarithmic
kernels, local contact matching, and independent state-preparation terms.
The useful project-specific task is to verify their combination in the
declared fixed-r prescription at the exact minimally coupled reference,
with independent implementations and controls. The generic memory kernel
and the existence of a causal response are not new physical mechanisms.

## Closest primary passages

**Guillem Pérez-Nadal, Albert Roura and Enric Verdaguer, Stability of de
Sitter spacetime under isotropic perturbations in semiclassical gravity,
[0712.2282v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.2282v1), 14 December 2007;
Phys. Rev. D 77 (2008) 124033,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.77.124033).**
Printed pp.3–4, Eqs.2–7, rescale a massless nonconformally coupled field
to a canonical Minkowski field with the quadratic perturbation
nu a² R phi². Their nu is the deviation from conformal coupling, not a
de Sitter Hankel index. State preparation is represented by past evolution
before the finite initial time. Printed pp.6–7, Eqs.20–27, construct the
closed-time-path influence action through second order in nu and cancel
the scale dependence of a nonlocal logarithm against a local R² coefficient.

The closest explicit match is printed p.8, Eqs.32–33: the retarded kernel
is a one-sided Hadamard finite part of minus theta(Delta)/Delta plus an
explicit local term containing Euler's constant and the renormalization
scale. The limit is defined after the singular integral and local term are
combined. This page was also visually inspected. Printed pp.8–9,
Eqs.34–40, split the response into evolution after the initial time and a
term encoding the earlier state preparation. Mismatched preparation can
produce initial-time singularities; their stress calculation requires
matching the preparation geometry through fourth derivatives.

These are direct precedents for the proposed finite-part memory and
fixed-state discipline. Their massless theory, expansion in nonconformal
coupling, metric perturbation, and isotropic semiclassical solution are
different from an external mass-squared pulse around x=2H² at fixed metric.
The paper's stability conclusion does not transfer to the HDblast shell,
and its local coefficient cannot replace the inherited fixed-r matching.
This source was added to the directly inspected set for this round.

**Paul R. Anderson, Carmen Molina-París and Emil Mottola, Cosmological
Horizon Modes and Linear Response in de Sitter Spacetime,
[0907.0823v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.0823v2), 22 October 2009;
Phys. Rev. D 80 (2009) 084005,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.80.084005).**
Printed pp.4–6, Eqs.1.2–1.3, were read again. They distinguish a retarded
stress-tensor commutator driven by a metric perturbation from independent
state variations, and require the response counterterms to come from the
same renormalized effective action as the background. A background state
is fixed before evaluating the response. Their dynamical validity test
concerns solutions of the linearized equations and bounded gauge-invariant
amplitudes.

Appendix A, printed pp.51–53, Eqs.A1–A13, supplies additional passages
beyond those recorded in the stationary checkpoint. Its separated-time
kernel is minus 2 cos(k Delta) theta(Delta)/Delta. The k here is an
external spatial Fourier momentum; it is not the loop momentum in the
proposed variance integral. Eqs.A7–A9 add a local logarithm and Euler's
constant, and take the regulator limit only after combination. Eqs.A10–A13
justify the finite limit and integrate by parts to an integrable memory
expression. Printed p.52 was visually inspected.

At zero external momentum this has the same one-sided logarithmic
structure as the proposed homogeneous susceptibility, after normalization
and local prescription are matched. That agreement does not determine the
proposal's additional finite '+1': it comes from the specified positive-r
mode subtraction and requires its own calibration. The paper's explicit
de Sitter matter model is conformally invariant; plane-wave canonical
modes at one massive minimal reference do not justify importing its stress
tensor, state modes, or stability result.

**Stefan Hollands and Robert M. Wald, Local Wick Polynomials and Time
Ordered Products of Quantum Fields in Curved Spacetime,
[gr-qc/0103074v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0103074v2), 22 June 2001;
Commun. Math. Phys. 223 (2001) 289–326,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s002200100540).**
Printed pp.31–32, Theorem 5.1 and its following remark, constrain the
ambiguity in the Wick square to Z1 R + Z2 m² under their local covariance,
scaling, and regularity assumptions. Printed pp.37–38, Theorem 5.2,
Eqs.73–75, constrain ambiguities of time-ordered products to local terms
supported on coincident points, with dimensional restrictions on their
coefficients. In particular the two-Wick-square case admits a local
c-number contact term; separated points alone do not fix its extension.

This is a structural reason to keep the matched local term visible and to
avoid treating arbitrary state-dependent nonlocal memory as a removable
counterterm. The theorem is formulated for a free field with constant
mass parameter; it is not, by citation alone, a proof of every identity
for an arbitrary spacetime-dependent mass spurion or the proposal's
specific finite constant. Those remain direct common-action calculations.
This source was added to the directly inspected set for this round.

**Antonio Ferreiro and Francisco Torrenti, Ultraviolet-regularized power
spectrum without infrared distortions in cosmological spacetimes,
[2212.01078v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.01078v2), 29 March 2023;
Phys. Lett. B 840 (2023) 137868,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physletb.2023.137868).**
Printed p.3, Eqs.16–18, introduce a positive auxiliary mass frequency
and display the order-two subtraction for the field square. Printed p.5,
Eqs.33–34, give the minimally coupled massive de Sitter canonical equation
and Bunch–Davies Hankel modes, with index sqrt(9/4−m²/H²). The exact
specialization m²=2H² therefore has index 1/2 and plane waves, up to an
irrelevant constant phase. This specialization is elementary algebra from
the displayed equation, not a newly discovered de Sitter state.

The equation still has xi=0. Its special canonical frequency does not
change the action to the conformally improved one or force the renormalized
variance and stress to vanish. The paper's eventual infrared-oriented
subtraction differs from the inherited fixed-r prescription; numerical
finite constants cannot be copied across them. The de Sitter checkpoint
already read this source. This round reread the passages just identified;
its earlier trace and finite-stress discussion remains inherited evidence.

**Will Cerne and Teruaki Suyama, Nonlocal Corrections to Scalar Field
Effective Action in de Sitter spacetime,
[2601.22644v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22644v1), 30 January 2026.**
This recent source was already present in the de Sitter review and was
retrieved and read again, not newly discovered here. Printed p.5,
Eqs.26–28, express a causal memory contribution through the imaginary
part of a squared Wightman function. Printed pp.7–8, Eqs.43–51, subtract
the coincident source value inside the memory integral and retain a local

at one loop through second order in self-interaction. Eqs.52–55 make the

by a local drift term.

The source subtraction is a useful contemporary methodological comparison.
It does not supply the free variance susceptibility, its positive-r contact
constant, a shell stress-current pair, or evidence that a post-pulse signal
is heat. The slow-variation approximation is unnecessary for the proposed
compact-pulse calibration and must not silently replace its history.

checks, and five pinned primary PDFs. Four requests were broader topical
screens and five were targeted bibliographic lookups. One 2026 paper was
useful and primary-checked; nearby recent gravitational-memory, nonlocal
gravity, black-hole-response, and holographic-stability records were
screened only in metadata. These title/keyword queries can miss relevant
work. INSPIRE date filters can also match publication dates of older
preprints. Neither a zero-hit query nor a successful calibration supports
an exhaustive novelty or priority claim.

There was no new owner-repository audit. The inherited de Sitter review
identifies UTOE as adjacent prescribed changing-mass work, with its own
limits; that remains inherited context, not newly inspected evidence or
independent replication. No private repository bodies were used here.
All requested primary downloads succeeded; there is no access blocker for
the five selected sources. Earlier blocked classic publisher PDFs were
not retried and were not promoted to primary evidence in this round.
