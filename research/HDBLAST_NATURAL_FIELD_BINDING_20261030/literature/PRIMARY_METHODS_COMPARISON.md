# Bounded primary comparison: natural equations and timelike boundary data

30 October 2026. Two focused web queries, followed by primary abstract/version
records and the main theorem passages of two papers. This is a methods
comparison, not an exhaustive novelty assessment or an independent audit of
those papers' proofs. It does not change HDBLAST's equations or boundary law.

## Current primary statements

1. Zhongshan An and Michael T. Anderson, *Well-posed geometric boundary data
   in General Relativity, I: Dirichlet boundary data*,
   [arXiv:2505.07128v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07128v2), revised
   30 May 2026. Primary Theorem 1.1 establishes a local smooth open embedding
   for corner-compatible Cauchy/Dirichlet data under the Brown–York tensor
   signature assumption, including its time-direction sign after the overall
   sign choice. The introduction explicitly allows any dimension
   and any cosmological constant, and explicitly excludes coupling to other
   matter equations from its treatment. Its version comments say some proofs
   were corrected with stronger use of energy estimates while retaining the
   results. Dimension alone is therefore not the mismatch with the present
   problem: matter, boundary operator and the energy hypotheses are.

2. The same authors, *Well-posed geometric boundary data in General
   Relativity, III: Conformal-mean curvature boundary data*,
   [arXiv:2503.12599v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.12599v3), revised
   18 June 2026. The primary record explicitly identifies a gap in the earlier
   linearized existence proof and a weakened statement. Theorems 1.1–1.2 and
   Corollary 1.3 give injectivity and dense range under the stated restricted
   background geometry hypotheses, with an added corner-angle datum. The
   discussion after Corollary 1.3 distinguishes this from surjectivity on all
   smooth target data, and records failure of general energy estimates,
   including at a standard flat round cylinder. This correction was already
   recorded in the frozen 15 October interior-master capsule; this check
   reconfirms its current primary version. It is not a newly discovered
   objection to the different HDBLAST shell problem.

3. Jennifer Seiler, Béla Szilágyi, Denis Pollney and Luciano Rezzolla,
   *Constraint-preserving boundary treatment for a harmonic formulation of
   the Einstein equations*,
   [arXiv:0802.3341v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/0802.3341v2), 2008. Its primary
   introduction, harmonic evolution system and boundary sections describe
   constraint-preserving harmonic boundary algorithms and numerical tests.
   A date printed by a rendered page is not a new publication date. This
   work concerns a different vacuum numerical boundary problem and supplies
   no physical scalar-dependent Israel-shell theorem here.

## Consequence for this checkpoint

Exact coefficient identities are useful for matching a reconstructed master
field to the natural Einstein–scalar operator, its physical shell residuals
and the source identities used in the retained analytic argument. They do
not establish the energy estimates, corner regularity or existence needed
for a different boundary problem. A homogeneous residual must follow from
the same local differential equations and their needed derivatives; zero
residual values at a point do not replace those identities.

The practical lessons are to keep the natural and reduced equations
distinct, state the precise boundary data, retain the correct corner and
regularity assumptions, and distinguish an exact implementation check from
a PDE existence theorem. No vacuum theorem above is imported to close the
Einstein–scalar shell construction. None provides observational evidence
for a higher-dimensional origin of the Big Bang.
