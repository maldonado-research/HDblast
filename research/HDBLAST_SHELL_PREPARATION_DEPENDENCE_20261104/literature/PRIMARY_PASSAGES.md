# Inspected primary passages and scope

Checked 4 November 2026. These are focused passage summaries, not a claim to
have read every line or a determination of originality.

- Minamitsuji, Himemoto and Sasaki, [gr-qc/0303108](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0303108),
  section II equation (19), writes the second-order Weyl term as
  `E_tt=-kappa5^2*phi_dot^2/4+C/a^4` and explicitly makes C depend on the initial
  condition. Section IV.3 equation (89) retains a related integration term before
  a late-inflation limit removes it. The scalar boundary condition is Neumann
  without scalar-dependent tension. This is prior initial-data freedom in a
  different model, rather than this compact-preparation volume certificate.
- Deffayet, [hep-th/0409302](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0409302), section 3,
  discusses compatible master-field data on noncharacteristic and characteristic
  bulk initial curves together with brane conditions. Differential order two
  in the master PDE is distinct from a second parameter variation.
- Hiramatsu and Koyama, [hep-th/0607068](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0607068),
  sections 4.1, 4.2 and 5, use a special initial bulk state and explicitly warn
  that the curvature spectrum depends on bulk and inflaton initial conditions.
- Fujiki et al., [2501.05036](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.05036), section 5.5,
  counts the initial-data freedom for constant-potential brane trajectories.
  Fujiki et al., [2609.11643](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11643), discusses smooth
  Euclidean closure as a state prescription within its effective AdS3 model.
  These brane-local scalars and dimensions differ from the retained bulk-scalar
  system. They provide context for selection rules, not one for this preparation.

The particular-plus-homogeneous decomposition is established perturbation
machinery. The present result requires its own constraint-compatible compact
construction, anchored observables and quantitative nonzero-slope bounds.
