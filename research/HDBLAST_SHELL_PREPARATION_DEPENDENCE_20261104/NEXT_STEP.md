# Next scientific targets

The sign-reversal example establishes a limitation of the current specification:
the retained first packet and constraint equations admit physically distinct
second preparations with different intrinsic responses. The next step is to
close that preparation freedom with a stated physical model.

1. Define the action, state or generating process that selects the second
   initial data. Its energy and junction conditions must be derived within the
   retained five-dimensional model. A state prescription from a different
   gravity theory or dimension cannot be imported by analogy.
2. Alternatively, define a physically justified bounded class of preparations
   and certify which observables remain robust over it. Distinguish that class
   from the unrestricted affine space used in the counterexample. A sign at
   one chosen preparation does not supply this robustness.
3. Bound a numerical nonlinear remainder and perturbation-amplitude interval,
   then extend the calculation to a registered proper-time history. The current
   qualitative small-parameter statements provide no usable finite amplitude.
4. For cosmology, specify matter interactions, an energy ledger and a physical
   scale before testing thermalization, sustained expansion or observations.

The working paper needs author assessment and independent specialist scrutiny
of the analytic premises and contribution relative to the closest literature.
The counterexample neither disproves every higher-dimensional blast model nor
establishes an origin mechanism. No journal submission is made here.
