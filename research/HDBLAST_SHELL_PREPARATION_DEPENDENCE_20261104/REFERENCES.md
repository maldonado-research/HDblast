# Primary-source comparison and contribution boundary

The general particular-plus-homogeneous decomposition of second variations and
the importance of bulk initial conditions are established. The contribution here
is an explicit compact constraint-compatible direction and a quantitative,
conditional opposite-sign example for the specified anchored shell observables.
This is a focused comparison, not an exhaustive search or proof of priority.

- M. Minamitsuji, Y. Himemoto and M. Sasaki, *Geometry and cosmological
  perturbations in the bulk inflaton model*, Phys. Rev. D **68**, 024016 (2003),
  [gr-qc/0303108](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0303108), section II equation (19)
  and section IV.3 equation (89). Their second-order Weyl contribution retains
  a `C/a^4` integration term depending on initial conditions. Their Neumann
  scalar condition does not couple the scalar to tension. This supplies prior
  initial-data freedom in a different model, rather than the present compact
  preparation or proper-volume certificate.
- C. Deffayet, *A note on the wellposedness of scalar brane world cosmological
  perturbations*, Phys. Rev. D **71**, 023520 (2005),
  [hep-th/0409302](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0409302), section 3. Compatible
  brane boundary conditions accompany bulk initial master-field data. This is
  linear perturbation well-posedness; the differential order of its master PDE
  must not be confused with a second parameter variation.
- T. Hiramatsu and K. Koyama, *Numerical study of curvature perturbations in a
  brane-world inflation at high-energies*, JCAP **12** (2006) 009,
  [hep-th/0607068](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0607068), sections 4–5. The
  authors explicitly limit their spectrum to a special initial bulk state.
  Their observable, matter localization and approximation differ from this
  shell-volume calculation.
- M. Bruni et al., [gr-qc/9609040](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9609040), and
  M. Mars, [gr-qc/0507005](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0507005), supply standard
  second-order gauge and hypersurface-perturbation machinery. T. Buchert,
  [gr-qc/9906015](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9906015) and
  [gr-qc/0102049](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0102049), supplies the averaging
  context. No new general gauge-invariance or averaging theorem is claimed.
- The [preserved prepared-family checkpoint](../HDBLAST_PREPARED_SHELL_VOLUME_20261101/)
  supplies the original negative response, exact mean preparation, full forced
  five-mean estimate and proper-volume equation under its stated premises.
  The current comparison retains these absolute terms and cancels them only
  in the difference between two families sharing the first tangent.

Recent relevant context includes Fujiki et al.,
[2501.05036](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.05036), section 5.5, which counts
classical initial-data freedom, and [2609.11643](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11643),
which proposes Euclidean closure as a state prescription. Both concern AdS3
with a brane-local scalar; neither selects the present five-dimensional
bulk-scalar preparation. The [recent-source note](literature/RECENT_CHECKS.md)
separates inspected passages from abstract-level screening and records primary
submission dates rather than paper-index update dates.

The inspected sources motivate specifying the preparation. They do not establish
the present nonzero slopes or opposite signs; those require the separate
certificates and theorem. Internal comparison and arithmetic checks do not
establish journal significance or external peer-review acceptance.
