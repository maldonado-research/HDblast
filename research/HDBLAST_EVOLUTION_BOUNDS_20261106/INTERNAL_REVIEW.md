6 November 2026. Separate derivation, source-audit and cross-review agents
checked the three contributions. This is internal review, not external
peer review, a priority finding or a journal recommendation.

than treating generic gauge freedom as unresolved. It reconciled the

conversion costs a factor four.

frame, independent temporal/spatial positivity, all sixteen fibers and
the exact four-Dirichlet/twelve-Neumann split. The outer flux was retained
until an outer condition is imposed. The propagated second-wave source
contains the coefficient-variation term with its required factor two.
Nonzero Neumann lifting and the high-order residuals were not treated as
free or already bounded.
