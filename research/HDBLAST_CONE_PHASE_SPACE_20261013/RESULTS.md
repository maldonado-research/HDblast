# Results and limits of the cone-current checkpoint

The prior scalar/tensor radial spectral theorem is preserved. This checkpoint
derives additional local current identities and tests the inference from
radial closure to a global physical boundary condition. It does not complete
that inference.

| Result | Status and assumptions | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Einstein–scalar Lee–Wald current on the generic longitudinal slice | Exact local identity from the action, including its metric–scalar cross term; excludes the separately treated exceptional harmonic subspaces from the generic derivation | Existence of a global admissible longitudinal representative for every physical perturbation |
| Unequal-spectral current and doubled-shell balance | Exact bulk conservation and even shell cancellation; odd moving-shell current agrees with the completed quadratic action | Permission to replace a general packet by an equal-spectral Green current |
| Characteristic cone limit | Conditional on actual solutions with the uniform differentiated expansions stated in the note; yields the scalar null symplectic bilinear | Construction of registered radiative solutions, a positive energy flux, or zero flux for every admitted state |
| Fixed-time versus finite-null-cut counterexample | Exact massless test-scalar solutions in flat 5D spacetime, realized locally by compact smooth finite-energy initial data; the two integrated limits are zero and `(b^4-a^4)/64` | An exact coupled HDBLAST gravitational wave packet |
| Radial versus inertial energy counterexample | Exact flat test-scalar datum with `f~y^-nu`, `1<=nu<3/2`; finite radial norm/form but infinite inertial gradient energy | Equivalence or inequivalence of the reconstructed full gravitational phase-space completions |
| Tensor zero-profile regularity | Exact warped Weyl identity; nonzero slice Weyl obstructs a C2 extension at the vertex; direct Garriga–Sasaki precedent credited | Removal of the massless profile from an energy completion; smooth cutoff estimates retain it there |
| Smooth exceptional translations and shrinking-vertex charges | Explicit local residual-vector classification; bounded required Cartesian jets give charges of order the shrinking cut area | A charge classification on finite null cuts, or a global complete gauge quotient |

The algebra checkers exercise the potential curl, mixed-spectral conservation,
shell signs, density Jacobian, exact comparator limits, weighted-energy powers,
warped curvature and gauge-vector identities. Deliberately wrong formulas are
rejected in normal and optimized Python. Functional-analytic and physical
hypotheses are carried by the derivations and review, not certified by these
finite algebra inventories.

The literature review selectively reads nine originals, including a 2026
null-phase-space preprint; a tenth canonical paper is identified from metadata
only. Queries and caps are recorded. The underlying methods, horizon
regularity warning and radial thresholds have established precedents. This
bounded review does not certify global novelty of the exact application.

The following remain unestablished: complete physical-state reconstruction,
global gauge coverage and finite-null charges, registered radiative-solution
existence with shell matching, full vector-sector completeness, nonlinear
stability, transition from the original unstable branch, reheating,
discriminating cosmological observations, and a higher-dimensional origin of
the Big Bang. The approved APS files are unchanged.
