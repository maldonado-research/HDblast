# Next scientific targets

The current certificate concerns three second derivatives at one registered
proper time for a declared compact mean preparation. It does not close a hot
cosmological history.

1. Test dependence on other admissible compact mean preparations. The initial
   constraint fixes a moment but not every radial profile. A physical generator
   must select the relevant data, or useful predictions must be shown insensitive
   to the remaining freedom. Do not call a chosen preparation a unique model
   prediction.
2. Extend the bounded response to a registered time history and establish a
   quantitative nonlinear remainder/amplitude window. Preserve the actual
   five-mean couplings, bulk/shell forcing, clocks and measure. A larger signed
   coefficient alone does not provide an admissible finite perturbation.
3. For cosmology, specify a physical scale and matter action/state, derive their
   modified junctions and close the scalar/tension/electric-Weyl energy ledger.
   The current scalar-on-shell response is not created radiation or a thermal
   fluid. Sustained expansion, thermalization and observational comparison remain
   separate calculations.

The working manuscript needs the author's assessment and independent specialist
scrutiny, especially of the retained analytic inputs and contribution relative
to the closest literature. Internal AI review and reproducibility do not
establish journal significance or acceptance. No submission is made here.
