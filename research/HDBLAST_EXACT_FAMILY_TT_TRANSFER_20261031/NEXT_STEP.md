# Next scientific targets

The selected exact family and its anchored wave-map reference now have a
conditional coefficient identification and a qualitative small-parameter
sign. Retain the initial-data, mixed-boundary existence and actual-field
premises; the reference choices do not remove them.

The next observation calculation should choose a different
physical prescription, such as a proper-time or geodesic congruence anchored
on the initial shell section. Derive its background and first observer,
radial-normal, event and frame changes before evaluating its second
curvature projection. First-order Weyl curvature can combine with observer
changes at second order. The current interval cannot simply be relabeled as
that observer's value. Declare the prescription, point, norms, budget and
acceptance test before numerical execution.

A separate quantitative target is an effective parameter window. This needs
explicit weighted-inverse/nonlinear-map and finite-slab state estimates,
plus a continuity modulus or remainder bound for the chosen functional.
The current C2 argument supplies only the existence of a sufficiently small
interval; it does not certify a usable numerical amplitude.

External specialist review should assess the inherited analytic proofs and
the new application independently. A submission-quality manuscript must
compare its actual contribution with the closest primary literature and
separate the established generic tensor machinery from the model-specific
family and shell calculation. Additional algebra checks alone do not settle
originality, significance or journal acceptance.

Connecting this mathematical model to a cosmological mechanism additionally
requires specified generation and energy-transfer dynamics, a viable
cosmological history and observational predictions. The present curvature
projection supplies none of those by itself.
