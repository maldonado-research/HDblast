# What prior work implies for this calculation

Derivative couplings have produced both inefficient and highly efficient energy transfer in published calculations. Armendáriz-Picón, Trodden and West found weak resonance for heavy scalar matter and a long-wavelength instability for light scalar matter in the effective models they considered. Simulations of kinetically coupled dilaton and axion fields in specific inflationary $\alpha$-attractor models instead found efficient tachyonic preheating in a restricted parameter range. Those comparisons motivate computing the actual kinetic weight, initial state, growth and energy balance in HDblast. The rates and gravitational-wave amplitudes of the other models depend on their own couplings and histories. [Derivative couplings](https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.2177) · [Kinetic preheating in an $\alpha$-attractor](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0370269324004866) · [Lattice spectra and gravitational waves](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.16152)

A July 2026 fully relativistic study found that derivative inflaton couplings and sufficiently large nonlinear fluctuations in its $\alpha$-attractor models can lead to gravitational collapse and black holes. Whether energy goes into coherent fields, relativistic waves, or collapsing regions is therefore a dynamical question. The HDblast study tracks weak standing-wave seeds up to first passage. Establishing black holes would require a trapped-surface calculation and appropriate further evolution. [Primordial black holes from kinetic preheating](https://doi.org/10.1103/pygg-bb7d)

The direct energy exchange here is proportional to the stress-energy trace of the massless minimally coupled daughter. In four dimensions the classical source-free Maxwell action is invariant under a Weyl rescaling of the metric. The two powers of the inverse metric cancel the volume factor. An electromagnetic field with only that universal metric coupling receives zero *direct* energy from varying the conformal factor. The gravitational response to an evolving, uneven metric is a separate calculation. A massless scalar with a kinetic weight can have a nonzero trace. Its excitations are distinct from a gas of photons in thermal equilibrium. [Conformal invariance of Maxwell theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.09968)

That result explains why the four-dimensional evolution here still requires independent higher-dimensional matching.
