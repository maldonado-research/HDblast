# Crossed elastic channels of the massive neutral vector

**Preliminary fixed-energy check.** The archived collision study uses a single timelike spin-one resonance in $f\bar f\to f\bar f$. Its originating calculation explicitly states that a literal field theory has crossed diagrams and that it retains only the pole. The project’s older screened spacelike-vector estimate concerns the weak wall gauge field with a Debye scale. Its separate scalar calculation concerns a neutral spin-zero parent or Yukawa resonance. The recent same-sign box uses a prescribed elastic cross section. The archived Møller flux factor is a relative-flux convention for two incoming four-momenta. None of these calculations supplies the elastic rates for spacelike exchange of the heavy $M/m=8300$ vector. ## Minimal dedicated-flavor model

Impose one diagonal, pointlike coupling $gV_\mu\bar f\gamma^\mu f$ and one massive neutral vector for a single Dirac flavor. This permits both $s+t$ scattering of opposite charges and the antisymmetrized $t-u$ amplitude for two identical charges. Charge conjugation gives the same answer for two antiparticles. A phenomenological isolated resonance need not fix its spacelike exchange uniquely. The following is one definite local completion of its vertex, with a specified approximation for the propagator.

Write the CM angle cosine $z$ along the tagged incoming particle's momentum, $s+t+u=4m^2$ and $t=-(s-4m^2)(1-z)/2$. Let

$$
D_s=[s-M^2+i(g^2/12\pi)(s+2m^2)\sqrt{1-4m^2/s}]^{-1},\quad
D_t=(t-M^2)^{-1},\quad D_u=(u-M^2)^{-1},
$$

which uses the archived timelike running width and a tree-level, spacelike propagator with real mass. Equal-mass conserved vector currents remove longitudinal propagator contributions. For generic permutation $(x,y,v)$ of $(s,t,u)$ define

$$
A(x;y,v)=2[(y-2m^2)^2+(v-2m^2)^2+4m^2x],\quad
C(v)=4(v^2-8m^2 v+12m^4).
$$

The spin-averaged squared amplitude divided by $g^4$ is

$$
A(x;y,v)|D_x|^2+A(y;x,v)|D_y|^2+C(v)\operatorname{Re}(D_xD_y^*).
$$

Use $(x,y,v)=(s,t,u)$ for unlike-sign Bhabha scattering and $(t,u,s)$ for same-sign Møller scattering. The interference includes the Fermi exchange sign. At zero mass the Bhabha result becomes $2[(t^2+u^2)|D_s|^2+(s^2+u^2)|D_t|^2+2u^2\mathrm{Re}(D_sD_t^*)]$. Exchanging $(s,t,u)\to(t,u,s)$ gives the analogous Møller expression. Independent Dirac spinor contractions of both amplitudes checked all six diagram and interference terms at five massive and massless points, including threshold and the pole, within $1.6\times10^{-15}$ relative to the stated scales. The total isolated cross section separately reproduces the archived vector-pole kernel using $v_{\rm M}=2\beta$ in the CM.

The integrated differential cross section for distinguishable outgoing labels is $d\sigma/dz=\overline{|\mathcal M|^2}/(32\pi s)$. Identical final charges require a factor $1/2$ when integrating the whole sphere. For transfer of a tagged opposite-charge stream use $1-z$. For a charge-blind pressure quadrupole use $1-P_2(z)=3(1-z^2)/2$, an exchange-symmetric weight valid for both sign pairings. Scattering of two equal-sign equal-mass daughters conserves their total momentum, so a same-sign angular transport cross section does not by itself represent drag between charge streams.

A different kernel changes which pairs actually collide. The same-sign pair distribution can have very different invariant masses, particularly for collimated parallel particles. A wall-integrated rate requires that distribution, its own pair density, relative flux and a consistent symmetry factor. The table shows where the additional diagrams could alter transport. It provides no wall-integrated rate or relaxation measurement.

The benchmark has $g^2=12\pi(0.30)$, $g^2/(4\pi)=0.90$ and $g^2/(16\pi^2)\simeq0.0716$. Its on-shell width fraction is 0.30 up to the daughter-mass correction. The dressed timelike line reproduces the existing benchmark. The width alone does not fix a crossing-symmetric real self-energy, spacelike form factors, additional vector-sector degrees of freedom or a next-order uncertainty. A literal pointlike model also admits other reactions when kinematically open. The weak electromagnetic coupling $\alpha\simeq1.44\times10^{-16}$ used to count emitted photons applies to the wall photon and has no role in these massive-vector elastic rates. ## What the recorded particles contribute

An equal-time census computes $K_2=\sigma_2 v_{\rm M}$ for every eligible pair in the archived dedicated populations. Here $\sigma_2=\int(1-P_2)\,d\sigma$ measures deflection of the pair's CM quadrupole and $v_{\rm M}=\sqrt{(u_i\cdot u_j)^2-1}/(E_i E_j)$ in daughter-mass units. Unlike and like pairs use their own momenta. For $N$ particles of each sign the census contains $N^2$ unlike pairs and $N(N-1)$ unordered like pairs across both signs. Each interacting pair contributes two particle participations. For the weighted rate per particle per unit wall time the all-pair sum is multiplied by $S_0D/(6\mu^3a^3N^2)$. The identical-final-state factor is already included in the integrated like-sign cross section. Each finer snapshot has $N=16{,}384$ and 536,854,528 unordered incident pairs in total.
