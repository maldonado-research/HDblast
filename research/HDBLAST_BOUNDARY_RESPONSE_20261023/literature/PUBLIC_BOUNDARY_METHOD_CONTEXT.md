# Focused primary context for a shell-inclusive point enclosure

Checked 23 October 2026 UTC. This is a bounded method comparison, not an exhaustive search, originality assessment or external peer review. The calculation under review is a model-specific application of retarded integrals, interval source enclosures and a contraction bound. It establishes no general new wave theorem or evidence for a higher-dimensional Big Bang origin.

Two primary papers were newly inspected in relevant full-text sections. Both predate this checkpoint; neither is a newly announced discovery. They clarify the established methods surrounding the proposed shell-reaching point calculation.

## Reflections, Robin propagation and causal support

B. Costeri, C. Dappiaggi, B. A. Juárez-Aubry and R. D. Singh, [arXiv:2509.26035v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.26035v1), submitted 30 September 2025, *The Hadamard parametrix on half-Minkowski with Robin boundary conditions: Fundamental solutions and Hadamard states*.

Section 4.1 writes the scalar Klein–Gordon Robin condition as `(partial_z+kappa)Phi=0` in its coordinate-normal convention. Proposition 4.4 gives the Dirichlet/Neumann reflected construction. Theorem 4.12 uses the Robin-to-Dirichlet transform, with its inverse convolution kernel in equation (4.15). Remark 4.13 explicitly separates solving the equation and boundary condition from proving causal support; Propositions 4.18 and 4.19 then address support and uniqueness. The construction develops the nonnegative Robin-parameter case, with the transform defined for positive parameter.

**Use here:** explicitly retain the normal orientation, direct and reflected domains, and boundary lift. Check causal support in addition to boundary algebra. These are established ideas; the elementary folding identity used in this model is not claimed as a new general reflection method.

**Transfer gap:** this paper treats a scalar linear Klein–Gordon operator on half-Minkowski with a constant Robin parameter. It does not provide the registered 23-amplitude metric/scalar matrix coupling, varying material-shell map, affine shell Hessians, physical subsidiary constraints, or a numerical interval for the HDBLAST component. Its inverse-transform kernel is not substituted for the retained model's negative retarded boundary lift and Robin feedback.

## Geometry-aware retarded integration

Johannes Tausch, [arXiv:2608.15292v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.15292v1), submitted 15 August 2026, *Space-Time Galerkin Boundary Element Method for the Wave Equation*.

Sections 3.3–4 derive polytope integration transformations and disjoint pyramidal decompositions, including truncated kernels across the hyperbolicity cone. This is directly relevant context for respecting causal integration geometry. Section 6.1 reports quadrature convergence for orders 1–19 against a numerically computed order-21 reference. Section 2 explicitly sets aside the additional geometry error caused by replacing a curved surface with flat patches; Section 6.2 says the full Galerkin numerical analysis is not completely known.

**Use here:** partition the actual causal domain and keep geometrical weights and Jacobians explicit. A mesh count or observed quadrature convergence is insufficient to certify the source integral. The present proposed approach uses rational polygon areas and whole-cell source intervals, rather than importing a convergence curve as a guaranteed error bound.

**Transfer gap:** the paper concerns retarded layer-potential Galerkin integrals for wave scattering, with singular kernels and spatial boundary meshes. It supplies no checked interval constants or pointwise first-derivative estimate for the material Einstein–scalar shell system. Neither its numerical reference nor its treatment of geometry approximation is adopted as a certificate for this model.
