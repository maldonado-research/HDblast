# An explicit source in the earlier conformal-vacuum calculation

The earlier study evolved a free real scalar with changing mass on a specified closed wall that already contained the fluid precursor. Its measured vacuum production at the opening was 1.6882 parents in the first 129 harmonics. The certified full-mode upper bound was below 525 at the same time. The mechanism in this calculation changes the Hamiltonian. It adds a source linear in the neutral field through a displaced mass minimum. The archived geometry, prescribed driver trajectory, conformal curvature coupling $\xi=1/6$, daughter mass $mL=1$, mass schedule and instantaneous particle basis here all agree with that earlier test. [Earlier calculation](https://github.com/maldonado-research/HDblast/pull/189)

Let $\chi$ be the spatially homogeneous neutral field in wall-action units and $v(\psi)=g b(\psi)$ the moving minimum. Take the positive potential $M^2(\chi-v)^2/2$, where $M$ is the dimensionless parent mass. The reference pulse uses $g=0.01$ and $b=S[(\psi-0.03)/0.075]-S[(\psi-0.105)/0.075]$, with the compact scalar step of the parent model. Put $y=a\chi$, $p=y'$ and $d\eta=d\tau/a$. Conformal curvature on the unit three-sphere gives the **homogeneous** oscillator

$$
y''+\Omega^2 y=a^3 M^2v,\qquad \Omega^2=1+a^2M^2.
$$

The initial state has zero mean field and mean momentum. A linear source displaces the state while the quadratic Hamiltonian still evolves its covariance. After the source ends, the increase in the expected number above the original free-field production is $N_{\rm coh}=2\pi^2 S_0 (p^2+\Omega^2y^2)/(2\Omega)$, confined to the homogeneous harmonic. The total in the specified instantaneous basis is the coherent contribution plus the number from vacuum squeezing. Here $S_0=\sigma L^4$ in natural units. The target inherited from a cold parent density $10^{-4}$ at $a_i=2$ is $N_{\rm target}/(2\pi^2 S_0)=a_i^3D_i/M_i=0.0004210526$. The unknown normalization cancels in their ratio.

On the pinned geometry this explicit source at $g=0.01$ gives $N_{\rm coh}/N_{\rm target}=1.41655$ at the earlier decay threshold $\psi=0.77$. The ratio at half this amplitude is 0.35414. Linearity of the prescribed oscillator makes the ratio proportional to $g^2$ and gives a threshold amplitude of **0.0084020** for this chosen source shape. Narrowing the half-width to 0.035 at fixed amplitude reduces the ratio to 0.36865. Translating the original pulse to start at $\psi=0.15$ gives 2.28418. A zero-amplitude control leaves the coherent increment exactly zero. These are ratios for a prescribed background. The result does not infer the coupling from the vacuum-only study, and it makes no claim of a cold particle ensemble.

The full conformal Hamiltonian of the mean field, divided by $2\pi^2 S_0$, is $(p^2+y^2)/2+a^2M^2(y-av)^2/2$. At fixed canonical coordinates its derivative splits into the work of the moving minimum, the changing mass and the geometry. At the opening those contributions are respectively 0.00228992, 0.00050731 and 0.00857036. They sum to the final canonical energy 0.01136759 within $6\times10^{-12}$. The scale-factor work depends on using a conformal Hamiltonian. These terms cannot be interpreted as fractions of the incoming pulse's proper energy. Here the scalar trajectory is imposed, so its depletion is not evolved. The separate live calculation varies the scalar action with respect to both fields and checks the corresponding proper-volume ledger. A necessary amplitude can also be bounded without solving for the drive phase. For the free-coordinate action $I=(p^2+\Omega^2y^2)/(2\Omega)$ the forced equation implies $|d\sqrt I/d\eta|\le |(\log\Omega)'|\sqrt I/2+|a^3M^2v|/\sqrt{2\Omega}$. The earlier all-shell calculation verifies one minimum in $aM$ up to opening with $a_i M_i=3.8$, $aM>3.5$ and $a_fM_f<19.2$. During the chosen source interval $a<2$, $M=1.9$, $|v|\le |g|$, and $\Delta\tau<1.2$. Integrating the inequality gives $\sqrt{I_f}\le 15.34|g|$. Thus $|g|\ge 0.001338$ is a conservative necessary condition for the coherent increment alone to supply the target. Following the phase gives the stronger required amplitude quoted above.
