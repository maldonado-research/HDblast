# Intrinsic angular first-derivative consequence of the full23 X error

conditional response certificate, not a new PDE regularity theorem.

Let e=U−V be the error between the retained exact formal response and the
registered full23 candidate, with ||e||_X≤E. The retained zero wedge gives
sup_D+ ||e(x,r)||_H≤3E/4. Here H uses probability-normalized integration on the
unit S3 and the positive Frobenius norm on the metric/scalar angular slots.
Let D_kappa denote the intrinsic covariant derivative on the unit S3, acting
on each angular slot. It is not the full five-dimensional frame connection.

Then the pointwise norm, summed over derivative directions and tensor slots,

    sup_(x,r,n) |D_kappa e(x,r,n)|_F
       ≤ (3/4) sqrt(287*24) E < (255/4) E       if E>0.

The weak bound with ≤255E/4 also covers E=0.

## Proof

The SO(4)-invariant section space generated by the retained harmonic types is

    W = 5 R00 + 8 R11 + 8 R22 + R20 + R02,
    dim W = 5*1 + 8*9 + 8*25 + 5 + 5 = 287.

The selected 23-feature space is a subspace of W; W rather than the generally
non-invariant selected space is used for the homogeneous evaluation argument.
The derivative D_kappa is equivariant under SO(4). Consequently W_D=D_kappa W
is a finite-dimensional SO(4)-invariant space of sections of the appropriate
tensor bundles, with d=dim W_D≤287. Equip W_D with its ordinary induced L2
inner product, not a quotient norm copied from W. This norm is invariant.
Possible constant sections in the kernel of D_kappa cause no problem.

For an L2-orthonormal basis s_1,...,s_d of W_D, the evaluation-kernel trace
K(n)=sum_j |s_j(n)|_F^2 is constant: SO(4) acts transitively on S3, preserves
the fibre metric and maps one orthonormal basis to another. Its integral in
probability measure is d, so K(n)=d. Cauchy–Schwarz, or the bound of the largest
positive kernel eigenvalue by its trace, gives

    |s(n)|_F ≤ sqrt(d) ||s||_L2 ≤ sqrt(287) ||s||_L2.

This argument applies to the derivative image even though it has one more
covector slot. It uses only equivariance, finite dimension, transitivity and
the positive invariant fibre metric.

Apply the evaluation bound in W_D, then the zero-wedge estimate. Finally,
287<17^2 and 24<5^2 imply `(3/4)sqrt(287*24)<(3/4)*17*5=255/4`.

needed: angular dependence remains in a fixed finite smooth basis. The bound
