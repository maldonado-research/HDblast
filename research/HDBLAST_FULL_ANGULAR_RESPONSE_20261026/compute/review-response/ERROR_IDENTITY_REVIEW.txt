Independent full23 response error identity review

The retained T/analytic/LOCAL_TOTAL_REVIEW.md explicitly gives the full-space
zero-wedge contraction q=3409/8000000 on D+ and

  X(V)=max(sup|V|_H/3,sup|V_x|_H,sup|V_r|_H).

This norm applies to the full retained Hilbert space.  H is the exact
probability-normalized positive fibre Gram norm in the23-amplitude basis.
For compatible V vanishing on x+r<1, its value is at most3X(V)/4 and its
Robin boundary trace at mostX(V)/4 on the retained causal domain.

Let b be the exact free-plus-source datum, T the actual coefficient feedback,
and U=b+TU.  Let B0 be the candidate free wave, using the expression-defined
nominal displacement and a continuous P1 velocity, and B1 its first frozen
coefficient feedback evaluated using independently P1-approximated nominal
displacement and nominal radial derivative.

The exact algebraic identity is

  U-(B0+B1)=T^2 U+(I+T)(b-B0)+(TB0-B1).

Given ||b-B0||_X<=e0 and ||U||_X<=Ucap, this yields

  ||U-(B0+B1)||_X <= q^2 Ucap+(1+q)e0+e1.

The actual-minus-nominal initial errors deltaU,deltaUr,deltaV yield

  efree=max((deltaU+(3/2)deltaV)/3,deltaUr+deltaV).

If epsV is a uniform H error for replacing nominal velocity by its P1 table,
then ||free(nomU,nomV)-B0||_X<=epsV.  Hence the complete datum defect is

  e0=efree+epsV+S,
  S=(3/2)*17387513156573+3851400=52162547172519/2.

The retained source omission S is a full-space H/X bound. It need not be
evaluated as a waveform to charge its norm to this error ledger.

If nomU,nomUr,nomV bound the three continuous nominal initial H profiles,

  beta_nom=max((nomU+(3/2)nomV)/3,nomUr+nomV)
  X0=max((nomU+(3/2)(nomV+epsV))/3,nomUr+nomV+epsV)

bound the exact nominal and candidate free waves respectively.  Therefore

  Ucap=(X0+e0)/(1-q)

is a valid conditional full23 total-response bound, independent of the old
five-mean cap.  It is acceptable to retain the same epsV contribution both
inside this conservative X0 and in the datum-error e0.

Let nA,nB,nD,nM be Gram operator upper bounds for the frozen candidate
matrices, dA,dB,dD,dM for their differences from the actual matrices, and
epsU,epsUr for the independent P1 profile errors used only in feedback.
Then

  df_coeff=(dA+dB+(3/4)dD)*X0,
  dg_coeff=(1/4)dM*X0,
  df_profile=(nA+nB)*epsUr+nD*epsU,
  dg_profile=nM*epsU,
  e1=(3/2)(df_coeff+df_profile)+dg_coeff+dg_profile.

No additional profile epsV appears in df_profile: B0 itself is defined with
that same P1 velocity, so there is no velocity replacement within B1.

For matrices block diagonal in orthogonal angular sectors of sizes5,8,8,1,1,
the operator norm is the maximum of the block operator norms.  A valid
computable bound is the maximum Gram-weighted block Frobenius norm, each

  sqrt(sum_{i,j in block}(g_i/g_j)*m_ij^2).

This applies equally to nonnegative componentwise coefficient-error caps,
provided all off-block actual/candidate entries are exactly zero. Merely
discarding off-block entries without checking exact zero would be invalid.

This note reviews the mathematical identity.  The accompanying independent
checker must still authenticate inputs and recompute the concrete saved
matrix bounds, sample data and arithmetic before accepting an output.
