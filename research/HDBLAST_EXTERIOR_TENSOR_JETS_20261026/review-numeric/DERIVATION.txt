1. Retarded geometry and orientation

Set T=1/4 and R=5/4. The closed backward cone is
  0 <= s <= T, R-T+s <= z <= R+T-s.
It has exact area T^2=1/16 and the zero-data wave inverse has kernel 1/2:
  (Kf)(T,R) = (1/2) integral_0^T integral_[R-T+s,R+T-s] f(s,z) dz ds.
Consequently the integral of the constant source 1 is T^2/2=1/32.

The reviewer builds the full N by 2N bounding grid and clips every rectangle
against the two cone half-planes. Exact rational polygon edge intersections
and the shoelace area formula recover all 20 cells for N=4 and 72 for N=8.
The corner cells have area h^2/2; other retained cells have area h^2. Zero-area
intersections are discarded. The retained cells have disjoint interiors and
their exact total area is T^2. This algorithm does not use the producer's
retained-cell loop or its boundary-cell area rule.

Write r_plus(s)=R+T-s, r_minus(s)=R-T+s. Differentiating the wave inverse
under the integral on the retained smooth actual solution gives
  (Kf)_x  = (1/2) integral [f(s,r_plus)+f(s,r_minus)] ds,
  (Kf)_r  = (1/2) integral [f(s,r_plus)-f(s,r_minus)] ds.
For v=u_r and its free source F_r the same formulas give the free u_xr
through the sum of the F_r traces, and the free u_rr through their difference.
Every characteristic segment is checked against these exact affine curves;
its integration weight is its time length divided by two. The reported whole

2. Complete scalar and differentiated feedback

The retained equation is
  u_xx-u_rr = A u_x+B u_r+D u+F.
For X(w)=max(2 sup|w|/T, sup|w_x|, sup|w_r|), the wave inverse satisfies
  X(Kf) <= T sup|f|.
Let sigma=cap(A)+cap(B)+(T/2)cap(D), q=T sigma. Then
  X(u) <= T Fcap + q X(u).
The differentiated equation, with v=u_r, is
  v_xx-v_rr = A v_x+B v_r+D v+F_r+A_r u_x+B_r u_r+D_r u.
Both Cauchy traces of v vanish because both retained TT Cauchy traces of u
vanish identically as functions of r. This does not assert that u_x has zero
initial velocity: u_xx at x=0 generally contains F(0,r).

With sigma_r=cap(A_r)+cap(B_r)+(T/2)cap(D_r), q_r=T sigma_r, the simultaneous
upper-bound system has matrix
  [ 1-q       0   ] [ M_u ] = [ T Fcap  ]
  [ -q_r      1-q ] [ M_v ]   [ T Frcap ].
The reviewer solves this matrix by generic rational Gaussian elimination.
It obtains M_u=T Fcap/(1-q), M_v=(T Frcap+q_r M_u)/(1-q).
The norm tails, computed independently as solution minus right-hand-side,
are E_1=M_u-T Fcap=q M_u and
  E_2=M_v-T Frcap=q M_v+q_r M_u.
The second q_r M_u contribution is retained separately from its propagation
inside M_v. Equivalently E_2=q T Frcap/(1-q)+q_r T Fcap/(1-q)^2.
The point-value tails are E_0=T E_1/2 and E_v_value=T E_2/2.
This argument sums the complete scalar resolvent and every differentiated
coefficient insertion. It never differentiates a C1 error enclosure.

The six complete scaled apex intervals are reconstructed as follows:
  u:     K F +/- E_0;
  u_x:   the F trace sum +/- E_1;
  u_r:   intersection of F trace difference +/- E_1
         with K F_r +/- T E_2/2;
  u_xr:  F_r trace sum +/- E_2;
  u_rr:  F_r trace difference +/- E_2;
  u_xx:  u_rr+A u_x+B u_r+D u+F at the apex.
The last line is an exact PDE identity. It requires no source x derivative
and does not claim that a residual formed from six independent intervals is
the point interval zero or an Einstein residual bound.
