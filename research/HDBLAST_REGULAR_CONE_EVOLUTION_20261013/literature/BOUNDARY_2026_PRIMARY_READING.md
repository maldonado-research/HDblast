## Stevens and Valiente Kroon: 2604.24931v1

Chris Stevens and Juan A. Valiente Kroon, *Wellposedness of the initial boundary value problem for the conformal field equations*, arXiv header 27 April 2026; body date 29 April. Read abstract, geometric setting p. 4, boundary-gauge §§3.1–3.2 pp. 11–13, §§5.1–7 pp. 19–25, and conclusions p. 26; selected passages, not a complete proof audit.

- **Setting:** four-dimensional vacuum physical metric with `Ric(g_tilde)=0`, p. 4 §2.1; signature `(+---)`, p. 2. The conformal Einstein field equations and conformal Gaußian gauge here are distinct from prescribing conformal-class/mean-curvature boundary data in the other sources. Assumption 1, p. 11, requires the finite timelike boundary to be ruled by a congruence of timelike conformal geodesics extending to a neighborhood. Orthogonality to the initial surface at its corner and a Weyl-propagated adapted frame are also assumed. Lemma 1, p. 13, gives the geometric conditions (16),(18) for this adaptation.
- **Reduced local existence:** Proposition 2, p. 21, applies to evolution system (28a)–(28b), with smooth initial data and boundary data `phi_0−c*conj(phi_4)=q`, `|c|≤1`. Smooth initial/boundary compatibility is assumed **to every order**. It gives a unique smooth solution on `[0,t_bullet)×S`. The paragraph immediately following explicitly says this alone does not establish the full conformal equations or consistency with the geometric boundary assumption.
- **Constraints and the narrower full consistency argument:** Proposition 3, p. 22, derives propagation from the homogeneous subsidiary system (35), with no transverse boundary derivatives in the adapted gauge. §7, pp. 23–25, checks compatibility of the geometry with the special boundary condition (34), `phi_0−conj(phi_4)=q` (`q=0` is fully reflective). Proposition 4, p. 23, reconstructs magnetic Weyl data from this condition and corner data. Lemma 5, p. 24, states that the boundary is maximal, `K=0`, in the **conformal** geometry used there; it is not an Israel condition on the current physical shell. The remaining boundary reconstruction equations are (36)–(39), pp. 24–25. Some intrinsic constraint-propagation details are explicitly omitted at the conclusion of §7.
- **A material algebra caveat:** the general two-coefficient estimate (32), p. 19, is printed in the PDF as `|c1*z+c2*conj(z)|²−|z|² ≤ (|c1|²+|c2|²−1)|z|²`. It misses the cross term: `c1=c2=1/sqrt(2), z=1` gives left side 1 and right side 0. Therefore do not transplant its general sum-of-squares criterion as a verified dissipativity lemma. The selected one-coefficient condition (33) and `c=1` consistency argument avoid this particular counterexample. This isolated check does not adjudicate the entire narrower proof.

**Transfer boundary:** a four-dimensional spinorial vacuum conformal formulation with a restricted geometric boundary does not supply arbitrary five-dimensional scalar-potential matter, two interacting interiors, moving-shell embeddings, Israel transmission, scalar matching, or Wentzell boundary dynamics. Its useful precedent is the need to verify reduction, boundary stability, subsidiary propagation, gauge consistency, and corner compatibility separately.

## An and Anderson: 2507.15567v3

Zhongshan An and Michael T. Anderson, *Well-posed geometric boundary data in General Relativity, II: Twisted Dirichlet boundary data*, arXiv v3 header 30 May 2026. The thesis bibliography identifies its publication as *Classical and Quantum Gravity* 43 (2026), 115020. Read introduction pp. 1–4; selected constraint/gauge passages pp. 9,12; Theorem 3.2 p. 14 and Theorem 4.1 p. 19; §5 pp. 20–22. This is a reading of stated results and their selected proof interfaces, not an independent proof audit.

- **Dimension is not the exclusion:** §1 p. 1 assumes `M≃I×S` with compact spatial `S` of dimension `n≥2`, compact boundary, and a finite timelike cylinder `C=I×boundary(S)`. Theorem 1.1 applies in all these dimensions, so five-dimensional vacuum spacetime is within its stated dimensional range.
- **Its data are specific and gauge-sensitive:** boundary data (1.4)–(1.5), p. 1, are `([g_C],mu_g)`, with `mu_g=dv_g*(dv_gC)^(-2/n)` interpreted as a density against a background volume form. These contain no metric derivatives, p. 2; they are not prescribed extrinsic curvature or a shell stress law. The authors explicitly say the twisted volume datum is not fully gauge-invariant/geometric, p. 3, Eqs. (1.10)–(1.12).
- **Theorem 1.1, p. 2:** for smooth vacuum constraint data, the initial normal/gauge framing and smoothly compatible boundary data, harmonic-gauge solutions and target data form smooth tame Fréchet manifolds; map (1.9) is a smooth tame diffeomorphism with smooth tame inverse for a data-dependent short existence interval. The theorem does not state an ordinary derivative-loss-free Sobolev estimate. Theorem 3.2, p. 14, gives tame estimate (3.10), with target regularities in (3.9); Theorem 4.1, p. 19, patches the linear analysis globally in space for short time, Eq. (4.2).
- **Constraints and nonlinear passage:** Eqs. (2.18)–(2.23), p. 9, determine initial gauge velocity using the constraints; Remark 2.9, p. 12, combines vanishing gauge data on initial surface and boundary with vacuum constraints to recover `V=0`. Theorem 5.1, p. 20, is the general off-shell reduced-metric mapping; §5 uses Nash–Moser and restricts to the constraint-compatible vacuum subspace, pp. 20–21. Remark 5.2, p. 22, discusses finite differentiability with derivative loss `gamma=3`, but explicitly does not carry out all finite-derivative theorem hypotheses.
- **Matter statement must be bounded:** Remark 5.3, p. 22, says the results hold for any cosmological constant and KK matter obtained by symmetry reduction of higher-dimensional vacuum, with matter boundary data induced from the higher-dimensional boundary data. This does not establish arbitrary minimally coupled Einstein–scalar potentials or independently chosen Israel/scalar shell laws.

**Transfer boundary:** this is strong nonlinear local vacuum-IBVP precedent in the relevant dimension range. The current shell imposes coupled derivative/transmission conditions, while both induced geometry and embeddings evolve. Replacing those laws with freely specified twisted Dirichlet data would change the problem. No Wentzell or doubled-shell theorem is supplied by the passages read.

## Maneerat thesis: 2609.31928v1

Chawakorn Maneerat, *General Relativity with Finite Boundaries*, thesis dated July 2026, arXiv v1 header 25 September 2026. **Selective reading only**: abstract and contents; §1.3 printed pp. 13–17; §2.1 relevant scope pp. 21–22; §2.2 pp. 22–23 and §2.3.1 p. 24; selected discussion p. 57, §3.2 p. 67, §4.1 footnote p. 101; bibliography pp. 187–188. Here PDF index is printed page plus one.

The work mainly concerns vacuum finite-boundary gravity, linearized dynamics and thermodynamics. Its conformal boundary conditions fix `([h],K)`, Eq. (1.3.9), p. 16, rather than An–Anderson's twisted density. It separates elliptic Euclidean results from Lorentzian existence/uniqueness and continuous dependence: p. 17 explicitly records loss of continuous dependence at certain backgrounds through growing large tangential-momentum modes. Such growth is relevant to boundary stability, not a universal claim that every finite-boundary Einstein problem is ill-posed.

For the four-dimensional vacuum example, timelike Gauss–Codazzi constraints are (2.2.5), p. 23; its fixed conformal-class/mean-curvature data and corresponding boundary action are (2.2.3),(2.2.6), pp. 23–24. Linearized conditions (2.3.3)–(2.3.6), p. 24, are trace-free induced-metric variation and `delta K=0`. These are not an Israel shell action or jump law. The spherical Dirichlet nonlinear well-posedness sentence on p. 57 is explicitly a conjecture. The nonlinear corner-mode completion on p. 101 is explicitly unresolved. The discussion on p. 17 also distinguishes diffeomorphisms preserving a boundary as a set from ambient transformations moving its embedding—useful caution for shell gauge classification.

Worth later original reading, without fetching here: Fournodavlos–Smulevici's umbilic case **2104.08851** and totally geodesic boundary **2006.01498**; An–Anderson conformal-mean-curvature/corner work **2503.12599**; and Liu–Reall–Santos–Wiseman's cavity ill-posedness result **2505.20410**. Their titles/identities are located in thesis references [44],[45],[59],[60], pp. 187–188. These are pointers, not newly read theorems.

## Consequence for the current checkpoint

The recent sources establish or analyze carefully chosen vacuum boundary problems. They strengthen the methodological precedent for constraint-preserving boundary evolution and show why boundary conditions cannot be inferred from bulk hyperbolicity or a positive radial form. None of the selected originals supplies the current minimally coupled five-dimensional Einstein–scalar, doubled moving-shell Israel/Wentzell IBVP. Its constraint propagation, characteristic/transmission symbol, boundary energy and compatible shell/Cauchy data remain a separate derivation. No priority or novelty claim follows from this bounded reading.
