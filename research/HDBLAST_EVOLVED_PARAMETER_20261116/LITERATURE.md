# Parameter variation: source and method notes

16 November 2026. This focused review concerns differentiating a selected C2 curve of compatible initial data after finite-Sobolev quasilinear evolution. The intended conclusions use lower Sobolev norms than the bounded base solution. No source below is used as an already applicable C2 theorem for the present fixed mixed Dirichlet/Neumann system.

The direct method suggested by these distinctions starts with exact difference equations and lower-norm stability. Uniform quotient bounds can then give compactness of the field and velocity, provided their time equicontinuity is also controlled. The actual PDE recovers higher temporal jets; the fixed boundary rows and compatible initial jets must pass in the available trace spaces. Linearized uniqueness identifies the limit as the first derivative.

An actual second derivative requires differentiating the constructed first derivative, identifying the complete second-variation source, and proving parameter continuity. Bounded centered second differences or a formal two-jet alone do not establish C2 dependence. Strong convergence one order below a uniform quotient bound is sufficient for this strategy; strong convergence at the highest bounded order is a separate claim.

The source review used Firecrawl paper discovery, metadata, primary-page retrieval and focused body passages, supplemented by retained boundary references. Retrieved full documents were inspected at the stated passages; no complete-proof reading is implied. A bounded search through 16 November 2026 supplied no fresh, directly transferable mixed-boundary theorem. This is not an exhaustive literature or novelty claim, and it makes no empirical discovery or physical-validation claim.
