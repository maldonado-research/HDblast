# Internal mathematical review

6 November 2026. Separate derivation and critical-review agents checked the
boundary vector, coordinate conventions, analytic flow argument and
conditional metric energy. This is internal review, not external peer
review, a novelty determination or journal endorsement.

The critic independently contracted the five-dimensional connection for
four exact rational full symmetric Lorentzian tangential metrics. The
calculation retained nonzero normal mixed-metric jets and checked the
normal relative-harmonic row, ten Israel components and scalar row. It
confirmed the full inverse-metric trace and the factor dy/dz=−rho. Two
injected errors, replacing that trace by 4 and using the reciprocal
coordinate factor, were rejected. A separate coordinate calculation
checked the raw conformal row and the transformation of the relative
harmonic vector.

The main review correction concerned domains. A radius-.01 auxiliary
state box does not imply a radius-1/40 physical matrix box after all
components are reconstructed. The final proof restricts that implication
to the .00101 inner flow image and includes an exact Rayleigh-quotient
counterexample for the larger box. The energy derivation also explicitly
requires spatial inverse-block positivity and boundary orthogonality for
every frozen iterate. Timelikeness of dt alone and bulk orthogonality were
not substituted for those requirements.

The critic's 487 exact arithmetic/connection checks passed in normal and
optimized Python modes with identical report bytes. Sixteen further
coordinate checks were performed separately. These counts include source
and algebra checks; repeated checks are not independent physical evidence.
The public closed replay has its own authenticated inputs and checks,
described in REPRODUCE.md. Its rational inequalities substantiate the
arithmetic in the proofs; they do not mechanically prove the universal
analytic arguments.

No unresolved defect was identified within the bounded auxiliary-chart
and conditional principal-energy scope. Application to a nonlinear family
requires additional estimates. In particular, the review does not turn
field-chart jets into physical observer jets, prove a quantitative
evolution/parameter theorem, or produce an amplitude interval for the
proper-time shell quadrupole remainder.
