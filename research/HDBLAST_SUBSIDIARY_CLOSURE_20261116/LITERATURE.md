## Boundary methods and their limits

The relevant strategy is to derive a homogeneous subsidiary equation from the reduced equations,
derive its boundary rows from the geometric junction law, and apply uniqueness with zero Cauchy data.
Each join must hold for the actual gauge, matter normalization, boundary law and attained regularity.

### 1. Fournodavlos–Smulevici: umbilic vacuum boundary

[The initial boundary value problem in General Relativity: the umbilic case, arXiv:2104.08851v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.08851v1), posted **2021-04-18 13:25:57 UTC**.
The retrieved record listed no later version or journal reference.
Coverage: pinned v1 PDF, Theorem 1, §4.1 Lemma 1, §4.2 Proposition 2 and Appendix A Proposition 3;
fresh indexed body passages were also inspected.
For smooth 3+1 vacuum data with constant umbilicity and compatible corner jets, Codazzi induces
homogeneous subsidiary boundary rows without adding further primal boundary conditions.
Their coordinate Neumann row is not our covariant relative-harmonic Robin row.
Their scalar Robin energy estimate supplies no evaluated constant for the present problem.

### 2. Ruiz–Rinne–Sarbach: harmonic absorbing boundary hierarchy

[Outer boundary conditions for Einstein's field equations in harmonic coordinates, arXiv:0707.2797v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.2797v2), revised **2007-11-16 16:41:10 UTC**.
Published in Class. Quant. Grav. 24 (2007) 6349–6378, [DOI:10.1088/0264-9381/24/24/012](https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/24/24/012).
Coverage: indexed body, §IV and §IV.1.
The frozen-coefficient, small-amplitude/high-frequency analysis establishes generalized estimates
for an absorbing vacuum harmonic hierarchy; a full nonlinear extension is discussed prospectively.
The initial-data reduction can introduce extra derivatives.
This is not a finite-Sobolev Einstein–scalar Israel-boundary theorem.

### 3. An–Anderson II: geometric vacuum data and finite-regularity qualification

[Well-posed geometric boundary data in General Relativity, II: twisted Dirichlet boundary data, arXiv:2507.15567v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.15567v3), revised **2026-05-30 08:36:29 UTC**.
No journal reference appeared in the retrieved record.
Coverage: pinned v3 PDF, introduction, Theorem 1.1 and Remarks 5.2–5.3, especially PDF p.22.
The smooth vacuum setup allows spatial dimension n≥2; it is not intrinsically restricted to 3+1.
The relevant differences are the matter model, twisted conformal/volume boundary data,
gauge implementation and regularity theorem.
Remark 5.2 sketches a finite-Sobolev Nash–Moser extension with derivative loss γ=3,
but explicitly leaves verification of the needed inverse-theorem hypotheses undone.
Remark 5.3's Kaluza–Klein extension has induced boundary data; it does not identify our scalar
potential and tension with those data or provide our finite-H14 theorem.

### 4. Ginoux–Murro: admissibility is a hypothesis to verify

[On the Cauchy problem for Friedrichs systems on globally hyperbolic manifolds with timelike boundary, arXiv:2007.02544v6](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.02544v6), revised **2024-07-08 09:04:23 UTC**.
Published in Advances in Differential Equations 27 (7–8), 497–542, [DOI:10.57262/ade027-0708-497](https://doi.org/10.57262/ade027-0708-497).
Coverage: direct versioned HTML, Theorems 1.1–1.2, Definition 2.13, §4.3 and §7.1.
The Friedrichs-system result requires constant characteristic and an admissible boundary subbundle,
including a semidefinite boundary form and the correct eigenvalue-count rank.
The smooth theorem requires all-order compatibility.
The §7.1 Klein–Gordon Robin example has a∇_n u−bu=0 with ab≥0 in its own normal convention.
That sign-specific example does not replace our arbitrary-sign covector Robin energy argument.

### 5. Costeri–Dappiaggi–Juárez-Aubry: recent scalar Robin propagators

[The Hadamard parametrix on globally hyperbolic spacetimes with Robin boundary conditions: Fundamental solutions and Hadamard states, arXiv:2609.24465v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24465v1),
posted **2026-09-21 12:09:08 UTC**; preprint, no journal reference retrieved.
Coverage: direct versioned HTML, §3 Eq.(3.7), Propositions 3.4–3.5, Theorem 3.6 and Appendix B.
The displayed classical problem is smooth scalar Klein–Gordon with constant real κ,
inward-normal Robin condition and compactly supported interior smooth initial data.
Classical energy/Galerkin existence does not require the later microlocal convexity and
locally finite reflected-ray hypotheses.
Smooth variable κ is discussed in Remark 3.1; no finite-H14 mixed covector theorem is supplied.

For the present four-dimensional shell, the scalar Codazzi source and the covector connection
must both be retained. The adapted-coordinate subsidiary rows are
C_y=0 and ∂_y C_a−(Nσ/3)C_a=0.
The full Robin coefficient includes the connection contribution; a vacuum coordinate Neumann
formula cannot be copied into it.
The current five-dimensional matter-boundary closure therefore rests on its own Bianchi,
physical initial-constraint, junction and finite-energy arguments, including arbitrary Robin sign.
These references neither replace those proofs nor establish global shell formation or cosmogenesis.

## Recent cosmological models and testable predictions

These sources calculate different mechanisms. Shared words such as “brane” or “bubble”
do not identify their theories, cosmological histories or observables with this shell construction.

### 6. Sengupta: effective cyclic braneworld

[Cycles Without End: An Ekpyrotic Braneworld Bounce with a Kinetically Coupled Entropic Mechanism, arXiv:2609.06485v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06485v2), revised **2026-11-02 22:11:03 UTC**.
The arXiv comments report acceptance in JCAP; no journal DOI was retrieved.
Coverage: indexed body, §2 Eq.(1), §2.3, §8 and final limitations.
A negative-tension brane with a timelike extra dimension and an added entropy sector gives
conditional tiny tensor ratio r≈7.6×10^-26; scalar tilt and amplitude are matched through parameters.
The paper leaves bulk viability, physical-hierarchy reintegration and a full five-dimensional
tensor/junction calculation unresolved. Its two-time setup differs from our one-time model.
An accessible primordial tensor detection would test this specific tiny-r construction, not all branes.

### 7. Cai–Zhang–Guan: remote-CMB collision templates

[Eternal inflation bubble collision signature on CMB remote dipole and quadrupole fields, arXiv:2510.12134v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.12134v2), revised **2026-03-20 05:48:28 UTC**.
The arXiv comments report publication in PRD; an exact journal citation/DOI was not retrieved.
Coverage: indexed body, §§III–IV, especially IV.2.1–IV.2.2.
Analytic templates and Fisher forecasts propose remote dipole/quadrupole tests of specified
collision profiles under assumed surveys. The profile coefficients are not our perturbation amplitude.
Ideal removal of ΛCDM variance is a best-case assumption; optical-depth bias and reconstruction
systematics remain. These are sensitivity forecasts, not a collision detection.

### 8. Ghoderao–Rajantie–Steer: super-Hubble bubble gravitational waves

[Gravitational waves from super-Hubble bubbles, arXiv:2607.24734v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24734v2),
revised **2026-11-03 15:38:32 UTC**; JCAP 10 (2026) 075, [DOI:10.1088/1475-7516/2026/10/075](https://doi.org/10.1088/1475-7516/2026/10/075).
Coverage: indexed body, §§2–5, Eqs.(49),(54),(57),(61)–(64) and Fig.8.
Bubbles nucleated during ordinary four-dimensional inflation collide in radiation domination.
Expansion changes the frequency/amplitude mapping; linearized gravity and an envelope approximation
limit the result. One illustrative set is described as likely disfavoured by LIGO's null result,
while another illustrates LISA reach. This is not an observed signal or spacetime-brane origin model.

### 9. Huangfu et al.: D6-brane-inspired particle-physics transition

[The first-order phase transitions and gravitational waves from an effective theory inspired by four-stack intersecting D6-brane models, arXiv:2611.11211v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11211v1),
posted **2026-11-08 07:59:34 UTC**; preprint, no journal reference retrieved.
Coverage: indexed body, §§2–4, §7 Eqs.(137)–(138) and Appendix B.
A gauge-theory EFT yields a conditional reference peak near 0.611 Hz for assumed wall speed v_w=1.
Scalar masses/quartics are phenomenological inputs; finite acoustic lifetime suppresses the signal,
and wall friction needs a separate calculation. Detector-curve overlap is not a discovery likelihood.
This is a particle-physics phase transition, not a spacetime brane-collision origin calculation.

### 10. LVK: observational null result with a specified spectral likelihood

[Updated Upper Limits on the Isotropic Gravitational-Wave Background from LIGO, Virgo, and KAGRA Data through April 2025, arXiv:2608.23477v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23477v1),
posted **2026-08-24 16:42:08 UTC**; report LIGO-P2600217-v10, no journal reference retrieved.
Coverage: actual primary HTML body, §III and Tables 3–4.
The O1–O4c1 analysis finds no evidence for an isotropic stochastic background.
Its upper limits depend on a specified power-law family, reference frequency and amplitude prior.
A proposed peaked spectrum must be tested with its applicable likelihood; a power-law number
cannot automatically exclude a sub-Hz benchmark or an arbitrary cosmological origin.

The next empirical gate is an observable derived from an authenticated solution and proper observer,
with a physical energy scale, source history, transfer to measured units and nuisance assumptions.
The present mathematical closure and these related forecasts do not supply that calibration or a detection.
