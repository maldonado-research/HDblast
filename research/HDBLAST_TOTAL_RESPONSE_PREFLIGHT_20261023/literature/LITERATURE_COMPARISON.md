# Focused literature comparison for the total-response / shell-observable step

Retrieved 23 October 2026 (America/Los_Angeles). This is a bounded primary-source comparison, not an exhaustive review or a priority/novelty finding. Six complete primary PDFs were retrieved and read in the indicated relevant sections. The scratch PDFs and extracted texts are evidence for this audit; they should not be copied wholesale into a public HDBLAST supplement. A public supplement can include this original comparison and retrieval metadata.

The literature supports separating a coordinate second-order field from an observable whose observers, initial section and integration domain are defined geometrically. It does not evaluate the registered HDBLAST total response, certify its numerical error, or identify the auxiliary `W_yy` with a force. The methods below are established framework; a justified HDBLAST contribution would be the specific complete shell/initial-data calculation and its verified error bound, subject to comparison with other model-specific work.

## Bounded recent search and relevance filter

Three explicit API queries restricted submission dates to 1 January 2025 through 23 October 2026 and sorted by submission date. Each requested at most 25 records; reported total matching counts were 103, 35 and 119 respectively. Thus this audit inspected only bounded returned records, not all matches or all literature. Exact query strings, result summaries and response hashes are in the retrieval records. The queries cover junction/hypersurface/averaging terms; second-order shell/hypersurface perturbations; and relational/gauge-invariant volume/proper-time terms in gr-qc. Broad 'shell' terms also return atomic/molecular papers, which were excluded by subject relevance.

The 2025/2026 strain paper above was selected for complete-text inspection. The abstract of Han-Wen Hu, Chen Lan and Zong-Kuan Guo, [*Dynamical thin shells in black hole ringdown*, arXiv 2610.03798](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03798), was additionally checked. It describes a radially moving black-hole shell, **linearized** Israel conditions and Floquet/ringdown effects. Its 'second order pole' means a double spectral pole, not a second-parameter-order Einstein–scalar solution. That abstract provides no basis for importing it as the missing HDBLAST second-order certificate. No complete-text result for this paper is claimed.

No surveyed primary result supplies the registered five-mean total response or numerical certificate sought here. This is a statement about the sources and sections inspected; it is not a proof of novelty or a claim that no closer prior work exists. A submission's significance still requires a sharper theorem-level comparison and preferably external specialist review.

## Concrete implications for the current calculation

1. Authenticate and retain the registered physical initial values and velocities; replacing them by zero computes a different quantity. Keep `U=(I-T)^(-1)U_free+W` distinct from `W`.
2. Apply the general moving-embedding pullback formula twice if needed: first to the physical timelike shell, then to its geometrically prescribed proper-time sections. Use the same second-derivative convention throughout.
3. Expand `sqrt(det h)` through second order including the quadratic first spatial-metric perturbation, and retain the lapse, shift and spatial clock-gradient contributions to the slice metric. A spatial trace of the bare second metric alone is incomplete.
4. State a local no-caustic/timelike-foliation premise explicitly. A conditional formal second-order jet and its invariant coefficient do not establish an exact nonlinear family.
5. The retained full physical subsidiary construction, the numerical mean-only error certificate and the unevaluated observable specification are separate claims. Their relationship must be explicit; none promotes the auxiliary coordinate sign to an observational prediction.
