
## Foliation and scope

The uniform short normal tube follows from smooth ODE dependence, inverse-function arguments, compactness of the initial section and a compact sufficiently small parameter interval. The displayed Jacobi estimate follows from `J(t)=I+tB0-integral_0^t(t-v)R_u(v)J(v)dv`. Its sufficient condition prevents focal points; a separate normal-tube injectivity statement is still required to exclude intersections of distant geodesics. The draft correctly distinguishes these two issues and gives no numerically certified finite interval.

For smooth bounded prescribed jets on a neighborhood of the compact anchored section, sufficiently small kinematical parameter realizations can be used to define this second jet functional. They need not solve the nonlinear Einstein–scalar/shell equations. Consequently the correct outcome is a formal invariant coefficient formula with explicit evaluation requirements, not an invariant numerical sign, exact nonlinear family or observational prediction. The retained total C1 shell histories and derivative/time-rescaling error budget remain necessary before numerical interpretation.
