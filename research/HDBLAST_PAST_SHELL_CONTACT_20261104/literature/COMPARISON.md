## Exact operator dictionary to the retained spectra realization

The spectra source uses the physical conformal coordinate z (named x there),
the endpoint mass d_b=1/(ρ_b B_b)=μ_b, and

    s(0)=ρ_b(3a_b/2+p_y,b/p_b)=C_b/2,
    V_L=ρ² Q_bg−5/2.

Indeed substituting p_y/p=h−4a into its Eq. (23) gives

    (3/4)a²+6a(h−4a)+2(h−4a)²
      =2h²−10ah+(35/4)a².

The proposed unscaled static bulk potential and spring are therefore

    V0=V_L+5/2,
    b0_unscaled=s(0)+(5/2)μ_b.

Thus the static operator is the existing Liouville endpoint-mass operator
shifted by (5/2)I wherever the coefficients agree. With r=z/ell, the norm
is rescaled by 1/ell and the new static operator is ell² times that shifted
operator. The proposed auxiliary cutoff beyond r=6 changes the global
auxiliary operator; the identification is not a claim of global equivalence
to the old cone operator after that cutoff. Its stated support bound r≤5
is what makes the difference irrelevant to the finite-past solution.

This exact dictionary is a particularly close internal comparison. It also
explains why the common bulk/boundary temporal shift ω(x)I is natural.

## Defensible incremental statement, subject to the mathematical reviews

The follow-on explicitly reconstructs μ_b and the spring coefficient from the
registered analytic shell intervals, verifies their signs, and uses the exact
common temporal shift to obtain a finite-past first-master evolution through
contact, with an explicit positive estimating energy and a small quantitative
energy-change bound. It then adapts the existing activation argument to the
backward registered velocity. That is a concrete model-specific continuation,
not a new general Wentzell or time-reflection method.

The subsequently proposed higher-graph-energy estimate has a more focused
incremental target: an explicit pointwise upper bound for the **actual first
physical scalar trace**, after using the boundary equation to control the
required normal derivative. Although the early physical-response record
expressly supplied no numerical actual-response amplitude, the later uniform
and inertial waveform records do supply quantitative actual future traces,
including the present width in the latter. The new result is therefore a
specific finite-past bound, not a first amplitude result or a blanket closure
of a previously unresolved response problem. Graph-domain regularity and the operator tools themselves remain
standard; numerical constants and physical reconstruction must be checked
against the final mathematical proof, separately from this literature review.

In particular:

- Positive boundary mass alone is not a proof that an arbitrary spring term is
  positive. Both actual signs and the outward/inward derivative convention must
  be derived. The bulk normal flux must cancel against both boundary summands.
- E_past≤E_present and the almost-constant estimate use the displayed temporal
  coefficient identity and its derivative sign. They are not generic consequences
  of selfadjointness, Wentzell causality, or the damped comparator.
- The estimating energy does not by itself control a pointwise boundary
  acceleration or the physical scalar amplitude. The physical trace uses the
  normal derivative of the longitudinal master.
- The qualitative activation contradiction supplies no numerical lower bound
  on the actual reflected response. An auxiliary whole-line positive value is
  only used under the hypothesis that both boundary value and normal derivative
  vanish.
- No second-order source history, nonlinear constraint completion, physical
  preparation selection, earlier incoming-state reconstruction, radiation
  production, or cosmological origin follows.
