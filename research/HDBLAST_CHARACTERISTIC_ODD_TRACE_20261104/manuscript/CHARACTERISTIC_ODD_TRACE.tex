\begin{abstract}
For one registered packet in a conditional linear Einstein--scalar shell
construction, the past--future difference has zero initial displacement and
a velocity input with small integrated mass. Incoming-characteristic transport, positive
two-variable majorants and a boundary-inertia comparator bound the actual
physical scalar-coefficient difference by $1/40$ on $0\le t\le3\ell$, and by
$1/40000$ on $49\ell/25\le t\le3\ell$, where $\ell=10^{-6}$.
The full dynamic boundary law and the complete previous forcing history
are retained. An explicit normalization join to older actual future
enclosures transfers two disjoint fine zero brackets and a strictly negative
post-pulse coefficient tail to the past. A separate norm/cross-term estimate
also bounds the past first-field quadratic scalar norm. The older analytic
and waveform-certificate premises remain explicit; their full campaigns
are not rerun. These are first-coefficient results in model units, without
an effective nonlinear amplitude interval or a cosmological interpretation.
\end{abstract}

\section{Registered problem and results}
Fix $\ell=10^{-6}$ and $T=3\ell$. Set
\[
Q(x)=\begin{cases}
 \exp\!\left(4-\dfrac1{(x-1)(2-x)}\right),&1<x<2,\\
 0,&\text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
\qquad q(z)=Q(z/\ell),\quad h(z)=e^{-z}q(z).
\]
The registered current master has data $(w,w_t)|_{t=0}=(q,h)$ and solves
the complete reduced problem retained from the contact construction~\cite{contact}:
\begin{align}
 w_{tt}-w_{zz}+W(t,z)w&=0, &
 -w_z+\mu w_{tt}+\beta(t)w&=0\quad(z=0),\label{eq:wave}\\
 W(t,z)&=V(z)-a_0\tanh^2t, &
 \beta(t)&=\beta_0-a_0\mu\tanh^2t,\nonumber\\
 \beta_0&=\gamma+\tfrac52\mu, &a_0&=\tfrac{35}{4}.\nonumber
\end{align}
Its embedding-included first physical scalar coefficient is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
 \Phi_1(\tau,\Omega)&=S(\tau/\rho_b)Y(\Omega),\qquad
 Y=n_1^2-n_2^2,\quad \tau=\rho_bt,\\
 S(t)&=G\cosh^{-3/2}t\,[w_z(t,0)-\gamma w(t,0)],\qquad
 G=\frac3{\rho_b^3p_b}.
\end{aligned}
 \label{eq:physical}
\end{equation}
The background shell scalar is constant, so first clock/label pullbacks add
no amplitude term. The angular mean of $Y$ is zero and $\sup|Y|=1$.
The branch, reconstruction, regularity, full boundary law and smooth linear
development/uniqueness are retained premises. The older actual future
waveform theorem~\cite{inertial} is additionally retained when its intervals
are used below. Exact selected-file checks do not reprove these premises.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:difference}
Under these premises, the registered first scalar amplitude obeys
\begin{align}
 \sup_{0\le t\le3\ell}|S(-t)-S(t)|&<\frac1{40},\label{eq:global}\\
 \sup_{49\ell/25\le t\le3\ell}|S(-t)-S(t)|&<\frac1{40000}.\label{eq:late}
\end{align}
The paired proper times are $-\rho_bt$ and $+\rho_bt$ at one anchor.
The bounds concern absolute differences of first linear coefficients in
model units, not either individual amplitude or a relative error.
\end{theorem}

\section{Zero displacement and complete boundary majorants}
Let $w_+$ and $w_-$ have data $(q,+h)$ and $(q,-h)$ for the same
problem~\eqref{eq:wave}. Evenness of both $W$ and $\beta$, followed by
uniqueness, gives $w_{\rm current}(-t)=w_-(t)$ and
$w_{\rm current}(t)=w_+(t)$. Thus
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \psi&=\tfrac12(w_+-w_-),\qquad (\psi,\psi_t)|_0=(0,h),\\
 S(-t)-S(t)&=-2G\cosh^{-3/2}t\,D_\psi(t),\qquad
 D_\psi=\psi_z(t,0)-\gamma\psi(t,0).
\end{aligned}
\]
The spatial normal does not reverse. The factor two is essential. This is
an actual coupled velocity-only solution, rather than a free approximation
or a second-order preparation difference. Its initial shell jets vanish,
and its shell trace vanishes before $t=\ell$.

The selected contact enclosures give safe bounds on the relevant causal
triangles:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
 \mu&>m=\frac1{1000},& |W|&<M=103000,\\
 0&<\beta_0<B=300,& |\beta|&<B,\quad |\gamma|<\Gamma=300,\\
 |G|&\le G_*:=\frac{25000}{129^3}<\frac3{250}.&&
\end{aligned}
 \label{eq:constants}
\end{equation}
These bounds follow from the admitted shell and background data. Positive
elapsed time is supplied by the complete even problem; the older past-only
interval is not silently enlarged.

Write $y(t)=\psi(t,0)$. The velocity is nonnegative, with
$\int_0^\infty h\dd z\le L:=\ell$. Its free Dirichlet solution $u_D$
has $|u_D|\le L/2\le L$. With the half-line retarded kernel $\mathcal R$,
\begin{equation}
 \psi(t,z)=u_D(t,z)+\mathbf1_{t\ge z}y(t-z)
                    +\mathcal R[-W\psi](t,z).
 \label{eq:dirichlet}
\end{equation}
The kernel is positive and has total mass at most $t^2/2$. On $t+z\le T$,
put $A=\sup|\psi|$ and $V_b=\sup_{[0,T]}|y'|$. Incoming transport is exact:
\begin{equation}
 (\psi_t+\psi_z)(t,0)=h(t)-I(t),\qquad
 I(t)=\int_0^tW(s,t-s)\psi(s,t-s)\dd s,\qquad
 \mu y''+y'=h-I-\beta y.
 \label{eq:incoming}
\end{equation}
Using $|I(t)|\le MtA$, $|y(t)|\le tV_b$, and the positive exponential
kernel of the last equation yields the two inequalities
\[
 (1-r)A-TV_b\le L,\qquad
 -\frac rm A+(1-s)V_b\le\frac Lm,
 \qquad r=\frac{MT^2}{2},\quad s=\frac{BT^2}{2m}.
\]
Their inverse is positive because $d=(1-r)(1-s)-Tr/m>0$. Therefore
\begin{equation}
 A\le A_*:=\frac{L(1-s+T/m)}d,\qquad
 V_b\le V_*:=\frac{L}{md}.
 \label{eq:majorants}
\end{equation}
The triangles and their characteristic subdomains stay inside the retained
collar. No field or coefficient oracle is sampled. The improvement comes
from the velocity mass $L$, not from treating the original displacement
as small.

\section{An actual normal-trace estimate}
On an incoming ray, odd extension of $h$ gives
\[
 u_D(s,t-s)=\tfrac12\int_{t-2s}^t h_{\rm odd}(r)\dd r\ge0,
 \qquad
 \int_0^tu_D(s,t-s)\dd s=\tfrac12\int_0^t r h(r)\dd r
 \le\frac{3\ell^2}{4}.
\]
The boundary lift and the bulk remainder in~\eqref{eq:dirichlet} consequently
give, on the complete interval,
\begin{equation}
 |I(t)|\le I_*:=\frac34M\ell^2+\frac14MV_*T^2
                         +\frac16M^2A_*T^3.
 \label{eq:I}
\end{equation}
In particular, there is no displacement contribution of order $Mtq(t)$.

Let $u$ solve the free bulk equation with the same data $(0,h)$ and constant
complete boundary coefficient $\beta_0$. Its value $b(t)=u(t,0)$ satisfies
$\mu b''+b'+\beta_0b=h$, with zero initial data. For $q_e=y-b$ and
$v_e=q_e'$, subtraction gives
\[
 \mu v_e'+v_e+\beta_0q_e=-I+a_0\mu\tanh^2t\,y.
\]
Keeping the whole earlier forcing history, rather than substituting a tail
estimate into an on-pulse convolution, gives
\begin{equation}
 |v_e|\le E_*:=\frac{TI_*/m+a_0V_*T^4/4}{1-s},\qquad
 |q_e|\le TE_*,\qquad
 |D_\psi-D_u|\le R_*:=I_*+(1+\Gamma T)E_*.
 \label{eq:error}
\end{equation}
Indeed $D_\psi-D_u=-I-v_e-\gamma q_e$ by incoming transport.
This is a characteristic estimate of the actual normal trace; it is not
an inference from an $L^2$ bulk-energy norm.

For the impulse kernel $k$, with
$\mu k''+k'+\beta_0k=0$, $k(0)=0$, $k'(0)=1/\mu$, the identity
\[
 [\mu(k')^2+\beta_0k^2]'=-2(k')^2
\]
gives $|k'|\le1/m$ and $|k(t)|\le t/m$. Thus
\[
 D_u=h-(k'+\gamma k)*h,\qquad
 |(k'+\gamma k)*h|\le C_{\rm mem}:=(1+\Gamma T)\ell/m.
\]
Here $*$ denotes convolution in nonnegative time.
Combining this with~\eqref{eq:error} proves the uniform estimate
\begin{equation}
 |S(-t)-S(t)|\le2G_*[h(t)+C_{\rm mem}+R_*].
 \label{eq:masterbound}
\end{equation}
Exact rational evaluation with $h\le1$ makes the right side less than $1/40$.
For $x\ge49/25$, the bump decreases to its flat endpoint and
\[
 Q(49/25)=e^{-529/24}<e^{-22}<10^{-9}.
\]
The last inequality follows from the positive series bound $e>8/3$ and
the exact integer inequality $(8/3)^{22}>10^9$. Beyond $x=2$, $Q=0$.
Replacing $h$ by $10^{-9}$ in~\eqref{eq:masterbound} makes its bound less
than $1/40000$, proving Theorem~\ref{thm:difference}. An independent route
estimates the incoming trace directly from a complete two-variable majorant;
it obtains the same two safe ceilings without a comparator oracle.

\begin{corollary}
The continuous first scalar amplitude has at least one zero in each of the
two disjoint open past-coordinate intervals
\begin{equation}
 (-1.49999\ell,-1.49998\ell),\qquad
 (-1.97\ell,-1.96\ell).
 \label{eq:zeros}
\end{equation}
Its post-pulse past coefficient satisfies uniformly
\begin{equation}
 -\frac1{1500}<S(-t)<-\frac1{4000},\qquad 2\ell\le t\le3\ell.
 \label{eq:tail}
\end{equation}
\end{corollary}
The zeros follow from the strict signs and continuity. The tail follows
from the retained whole-interval actual future tail enclosure and the
localized bound~\eqref{eq:late}. These are at least two distinct zeros,
without uniqueness, a complete zero count or optimality. The central width
is $\ell/100000$, a factor $3125/64$ narrower than the preceding past
$\ell/2048$ bracket~\cite{pastfuture}. This transfers an older fine future
bracket; it does not improve that future bracket. A negative $S$ is not a
globally negative angular field because $Y$ changes sign.

For completeness a separate norm estimate retains the quadratic cross term.
Define $H_\pm(x)=\ell D_{\rm current}(\pm\ell x)$,
$I_\pm=\int_0^3H_\pm^2\dd x$. From the characteristic bound, the prefront
vanishing and $\Delta H=-2\ell D_\psi(\ell x)$,
\[
 \norm{\Delta H}_{L^2(0,3)}^2
 \le 2[2\ell(1+C_{\rm mem}+R_*)]^2<(3\ell)^2.
\]
The retained actual future interval gives $I_+<9$, hence
\begin{equation}
 |I_--I_+|
 \le2\norm{H_+}_2\norm{\Delta H}_2+\norm{\Delta H}_2^2
 <18\ell+9\ell^2=\frac{18000009}{10^{12}}.
 \label{eq:cross}
\end{equation}
For the first-field squared scalar norm on the past background proper-time
window, the spatial measure is
$\dd V_{0,\tau}=\rho_b^3\cosh^3t\dd\Omega$, $\dd\tau=\rho_b\dd t$,
and $\int_{S^3}Y^2\dd\Omega=\pi^2/3$. The even background measure and
conjugation cancel as in the future:
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C_-=\frac{\mathcal K}{\ell}I_-,\qquad
 \mathcal K=\frac{3\pi^2}{\rho_b^2p_b^2}>0,\qquad
 6.06\,10^{11}<\mathcal C_-<7.44\,10^{11}.
 \label{eq:C}
\end{equation}
The time reversal changes integration limits, not the positive sign of this
integral. The last interval follows by widening the retained actual $I_+$
interval with~\eqref{eq:cross} and multiplying the retained positive
$\mathcal K/\ell$ interval. This is not physical injected or Noether energy.

\section*{Scope and reproducibility}
The new calculations use selected public input copies, continuous proofs,
independent derivations and exact normal/optimized arithmetic. No old waveform
campaign, numerical PDE, background trajectory or field/source/bump oracle
is rerun. Authentication and finite arithmetic do not replace the retained
analytic and actual-waveform theorems. The source capsule includes a separate
manuscript display audit. Internal review is distinct from external peer
review and proof-assistant certification.

Earlier project results already include conditional formal complete future
second Taylor coefficients and conditional exact nonlinear future families,
with qualitative small-amplitude conclusions~\cite{second,nonlinear,intrinsic}.
Those results are preserved. Applying the appropriate hypotheses to a chosen
past completion, quantifying its particular observable and remainder, and
obtaining an effective parameter radius remain distinct tasks. No common
exact past family or finite-amplitude waveform is established in this note.
The bounds provide neither SI time calibration, a physical source mechanism,
radiation/heating nor evidence for a higher-dimensional Big Bang.

The methods have direct characteristic and positive-majorant precedents in
the older project proof~\cite{inertial}; they are not new general principles.
Recent timelike-Einstein boundary work distinguishes reduced existence,
constraint propagation and geometric consistency~\cite{stevens}. Its vacuum,
dimension and boundary hypotheses do not supply this shell problem.
Similarly, damped Wentzell stabilization~\cite{parada} is not imported as a
backward decay theorem. The comparison is focused, not an exhaustive
priority review; broader novelty and significance require specialist assessment.
