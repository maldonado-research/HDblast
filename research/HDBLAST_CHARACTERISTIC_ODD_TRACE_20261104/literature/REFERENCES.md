# Focused primary comparison

4 November 2026, America/Los_Angeles. This is a bounded source comparison,
not an exhaustive novelty search or an assessment of journal significance.

The closest project precedent is the
[inertial waveform proof](https://github.com/maldonado-research/HDblast/blob/74d0455f1446dbb10dd2f63721d966349d092162/research/HDBLAST_INERTIAL_SHELL_WAVEFORM_20261015/INERTIAL_SHELL_WAVEFORM.md),
especially its incoming-characteristic identity, positive two-variable
majorants and complete boundary-error history. The new application isolates
zero displacement and the small odd initial velocity; it does not introduce
a new general wave-equation method. The older actual future waveform theorem
remains a retained premise when its intervals are transported.

The [previous focused comparison](https://github.com/maldonado-research/HDblast/blob/74d0455f1446dbb10dd2f63721d966349d092162/research/HDBLAST_PAST_FUTURE_SCALAR_TRANSFER_20261104/literature/REFERENCES.md)
credits Zahn's product-space and higher-energy construction, Marta's explicit
time-reflection argument, and earlier endpoint-energy estimates. Those
source readings are reused rather than represented as new reviews.

Two additional recent primary sources were checked:

1. Chris Stevens and Juan A. Valiente Kroon, *Wellposedness of the initial
   boundary value problem for the conformal field equations*,
   [arXiv:2604.24931v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.24931v1), 27 April 2026.
   The introduction, Proposition 2 in section 5.2, Proposition 3 in section 6,
   and the opening gauge discussion distinguish reduced-system existence,
   constraint propagation and geometric consistency. These are relevant
   methodological checkpoints. The paper concerns four-dimensional vacuum
   conformal Einstein equations with restricted timelike boundary geometry
   and maximally dissipative data. It does not establish the two-sided
   Einstein–scalar junction problem or the model-specific parameter bounds
   used here.

2. Hugo Parada and Nicolas Vanspranghe, *Optimal stabilization rate for the
   wave equation with hyperbolic boundary condition*,
   [arXiv:2512.19167v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.19167v1), 22 December 2025.
   Section 1.1, its bulk-plus-boundary energy identity, and Theorem 1.2 concern
   dynamic Wentzell boundaries with dissipative feedback under geometric
   control hypotheses. That long-time damping result is different from this
   short-time reversible shell comparison; its decay rate is not imported
   into the past evolution.

Only the indicated passages were read. Neither these external results nor
the bounded search establishes originality, a physical incoming source,
an effective nonlinear radius for this packet, radiation production or a
higher-dimensional origin of the Big Bang. Existing conditional project
nonlinear-family theorems must be distinguished from missing quantitative
past-state and observable estimates.
