rational residual comparison sharpens the retained regular branch's shell

\section{A polynomial defect and its damping barrier}
The supplement lists exact rational degree-four polynomials $Z(\eta,h)$ and
$A(\eta,h)$, beginning with $Z=14/9-(42/5)h+(25/23)\eta+\cdots$ and
$A=1/9+(9/2)h-(7/27)\eta^2+\cdots$. Define
\begin{align}
 B&=2hZ_h-4Z,\qquad R=M-Z^2-\eta Z_\eta Z+AB,\nonumber\\
 D&=h+\eta^2Z^2/12-U/6-A^2.\label{eq:defects}
\end{align}
All coefficients of $R,D$ through total degree four vanish exactly. The
remaining coefficients are bounded, not discarded. On
$|\eta|\leq\epsilon$, $0\leq h\leq h_0$, coefficientwise rational estimates
prove $0<Z<2$, $A>0$, $B<0$, and
\begin{equation}
 4a_-+\inf Z-\sup|\eta Z_\eta|>1.88625\geq\kappa:=37/20.
 \label{eq:damping}
\end{equation}
No invariant manifold or convergence of a formal series is assumed.
For $e=z-Z(\eta,h)$ along the actual trajectory, the exact identities are
\begin{align}
 a-A&=\frac{D+\eta^2(z+Z)e/12}{a+A},\nonumber\\
 e_y&=R+\frac{BD}{a+A}-\mathcal K e,\qquad
 \mathcal K=4a+z+Z+\eta Z_\eta
       -\frac{B\eta^2(z+Z)}{12(a+A)}\geq\kappa.\label{eq:error}
\end{align}
The square-root defect is charged. The final term in $\mathcal K$ is
nonnegative because $B<0$ and $z+Z>0$.

Let $Q=\sup|B|/(a_-+\inf A)$. Absolute coefficients in $R$ and $QD$, with
$|\eta|^i\leq\epsilon^i$ and $h^j\leq h_0^{j-5}h^5$ for $j>5$, give
nonnegative rational $C_j$ satisfying
$|R+BD/(a+A)|\leq\sum_{j=0}^5 C_jh^j$. Use
\begin{equation}
 E(h)=2(h/h_0)^6+\sum_{j=0}^5\frac{C_jh^j}{\kappa-2ja_+}.\label{eq:barrier}
\end{equation}
The denominators have minimum $\kappa-10a_+=7/20$; the memory term has
margin $\kappa-12a_+=1/20$. Since
$(h^j)_y+\kappa h^j=(\kappa-2ja)h^j$, we have
$E_y+\kappa E\geq\sum C_jh^j$. At entry $h=h_0$,
$|z-Z|\leq2\leq E(h_0)$. Scalar comparison yields $|e|\leq E(h)$.
The first term retains all cone histories allowed by the entry interval.
At the shell, the verified bound is $E(h)<3.752255\times10^{-13}$.

The shell laws retain one common parameter:

\end{align}
For $G(\beta)=\beta Z(\beta,h_s(\beta))+W'(\beta)+\delta c/2$,

The full chain derivative $G'=Z+\beta(Z_\eta+Z_hh_s')+W''$ is greater
