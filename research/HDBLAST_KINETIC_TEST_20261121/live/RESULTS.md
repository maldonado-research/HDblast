# Particle heating with a responding wall and bulk

This experiment follows the finite five-dimensional wave collar, the expanding closed wall, the neutral parent and an ensemble of charged daughter particles in one evolution. It asks how long the particles can carry most of the added wall energy in motion relative to their common drift. The particles need no assumed thermal momentum distribution. The higher-mass dedicated interaction is the benchmark. [Live measurements](LIVE.json)

The 512-representative dedicated runs reached a 90% Landau internal-energy index over an uninterrupted radius increase of 2.289 and 2.290 for two random seeds. With collisions disabled, particles born at different times still acquired a spread of speeds, but that interval ended after 1.578-fold growth. The bounded wall-frame dispersion index reached at most 83.34% in the collisionless run. In its first Landau-hot state the longitudinal pressure share was 0.9999992 and its energy-distribution distance from the fitted thermal law was 0.514. Directed motion of different birth cohorts can therefore generate a large internal-energy index while retaining an extremely anisotropic distribution.

The 1,024-particle result shows why pressure and energy shape need separate measurements. At the Landau-hot radius doubling, the longitudinal pressure share was 0.3290, near the isotropic value $1/3$. Yet 25.59% of particles still had $p_\perp/m\le\sqrt{0.1025}$, the narrow range initially available to new daughters. The distance between the Landau-energy CDF and its fitted thermal CDF was 0.2580. For the two native 512 runs the corresponding observed tube fractions were both zero and the CDF distances were 0.0296 and 0.0367. The sampled spectral shape is much more sensitive to particle resolution than the turnaround radius. Both 2,048-representative runs show a sizeable spectral discrepancy at the radius doubling even though the pressure and energy diagnostics look favorable. The two runs place **21.88% and 37.55%** of particles in the narrow transverse birth range. Their energy-CDF distances are **0.2191 and 0.3777**. ## Equations entering the wall

The wall radius $a$, Hubble rate, wall-side bulk mass, electric and magnetic fields, scalar driver and parent all evolve under the preceding finite-collar equations. The parent continuum loses energy only from accepted decays. The accepted rest $D$ and the kinetic threshold $R=M/(2m)$ are reevaluated along the current trajectory. The rest-mass weight $w$ of each numerical representative equals the preceding thermal run's final comoving rest divided by the nominal number of representatives. That choice fixes sampling resolution. A new representative appears when the live accepted rest passes the next half-weight mark. Its direction is sampled from the directional birth cap and its physical birth momentum has magnitude $m\sqrt{R^2-1}$. [Wall equations and inputs](../prior/prior/coupled/joined_forward.cpp) · [Source and evolution](run.py)

Three equal orthogonal electric fields and mirrored charge populations keep the total stress isotropic in this calculation. Each charge population can have a different longitudinal and transverse pressure. The dedicated interaction preserves the species. For each species its distribution is symmetric under rotations about its field axis. A parallel magnetic field rotates the transverse momentum around the axis while preserving its magnitude. The collision sampler averages over that azimuth and exactly conserves pair energy and momentum up to floating-point error. Its vector-pole mass, width and center-of-momentum angular law are the same as in the driven ensemble. Each reflected update represents both signs of a charge-symmetric molecular-chaos ensemble. [Elastic sampler](../driven/ensemble.cpp)

In units of the daughter mass, $p_{\perp i}=q_{\perp i}/a$ and $p_{zi}=(q_{zi}+I)/a$, with $\dot I=QaX$. Set $E_i=\sqrt{1+p_{\perp i}^2+p_{zi}^2}$. The field uses the measured current and gravity uses the summed pressure

$$
J=\frac{Qw}{a^3}\sum_i\frac{p_{zi}}{E_i},\qquad
P=\frac{w}{3a^3}\sum_i\frac{p_{\perp i}^2+p_{zi}^2}{E_i}.
$$

The wall's kinetic gas energy obeys $d(a^3\rho_g)/d\tau=a^3[P_{\rm birth}+J X-3H P]$, and the same current removes the corresponding energy from the field. The value $P_{\rm birth}$ is accepted parent power per volume. The independently summed particle energy $w\sum_i E_i$ checks the integrated energy. These sources enter each Runge–Kutta stage of the gravity, gauge and bulk wave evolution. No previously computed field or expansion history is prescribed. [Particle stress](coupling.hpp)

The collision update uses the native step's midpoint radius and impulse. Newly sampled births have their canonical momentum shifted to their interpolated birth time, with the shell momentum and angular law evaluated on the step's final state. The split collision update can include those new particles for the full step. The half-step run checks sensitivity to both the split and the birth approximation. The tabulated stiffness monitor uses $\omega_p^2=Q^2 w(Ka^3)^{-1}\sum_i(1+p_{\perp i}^2)/E_i^3$, the frozen-ensemble longitudinal response to the electric force. The all-cold upper sets each particle factor to one. The monitor records each accepted endpoint. Across the eight standard runs the largest sampled $\Delta\tau\omega_p$ was 0.0455 and the cold upper was 0.1331. The Friedmann and surface-energy residuals stayed below $4.9\times10^{-9}$ and $1.69\times10^{-8}$ in code units. In the seven particle runs the largest absolute difference between summed particle energy and the conservative gas ledger was at most 0.0208% of peak comoving particle energy. The final energy assigned to discrete births differs from the integrated parent decay source by at most 0.00130%. The largest measured relative pair energy error was below $9\times10^{-16}$. An alternate compiler run allowing fused multiply-add independently recovered the 512-representative native seed-1717 collision count, spectral diagnostics and longest intervals.

## Two ways to quantify random motion

For each sign, the Landau frame is the boost along its axis for which the energy flux vanishes. Let $e_L/n_L$ be energy per particle in that frame in units of its mass, and $e_w$ the total added energy density of the wall. The index $\frac{wN}{a^3}(e_L/n_L-1)/e_w$ measures specific internal energy times the wall-frame rest density. It reduces to the project's fluid hot index for an isotropic rest distribution with common particle and energy flow. The frame choice and its number normalization matter for an anisotropic gas.

A second quantity is the wall-frame energy in excess of the minimum energy of the same particles at their measured total momentum. In each sign's axially symmetric ensemble it is

$$
f_{\rm disp}=\frac{w}{a^3e_w}
\left[\sum_i E_i-\sqrt{N^2+\left(\sum_i p_{zi}\right)^2}\right].
$$

Convexity of the relativistic energy guarantees that its numerator is nonnegative. It is bounded above by the sampled lab-frame gas energy. This index includes energy in a broad distribution of momenta irrespective of the shape of that distribution.

An additional test requires $|P_{zz,L}/(P_{xx,L}+P_{yy,L}+P_{zz,L})-1/3|\le0.05$ for an individual species in its Landau frame. Its conjunction with a 90% energy threshold defines a continuous qualifying interval during expansion. At every native step we intersect the linearly interpolated inequalities and retain the longest interval in radius. Individual collisions can interrupt the interval in a finite sample. The full wall already has isotropic summed stress through the three axes. At selected times we also compare the sampled Landau-frame energy CDF with the Maxwell–Jüttner CDF fitted to its measured mean. The reported Kolmogorov distance measures agreement of this one marginal. [Distribution diagnostic](../driven/spectra.hpp)

## Scope and reproduction

All quantities concern one finite evolution through the onset of contraction. The inherited wall, scalar drive, parent prescription, source width and branching, elastic pole, molecular-chaos collision law and triad symmetry are assumptions. Daughters are represented by finite, equally weighted samples. Collective fluctuations, inverse reactions and a derivation of the source from microscopic quantum dynamics would require extensions. This test concerns heat production and gravitational response within that model. The existing large deficit in ordinary radiation entropy remains a separate cosmological constraint. [Earlier entropy calculation](https://github.com/maldonado-research/HDblast/tree/7fe36012da64e98add3915acd0356c733e717670/research/HDBLAST_DIRECTIONAL_BIRTH_20261121/cosmology)

From the study root run `python -I -B live/run.py --prior prior --output /tmp/LIVE.json --jobs 2`. This reuses the pinned finite-collar source and the local collision sampler. The eight cases include the earlier thermal model as a control, the collisionless kinetic model, two seeds each at 256 and 512 representatives, a halved wall time step and a 1,024-representative run. To reproduce the two 2,048-representative cases, add `--extended` and choose an output path for `EXTENDED.json`. On the reference machine one 2,048 case, including compilation, took roughly 68 CPU seconds.
