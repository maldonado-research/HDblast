                                                 Abstract
          For a retained conditional Einstein–scalar problem with a timelike material shell, we complete
      a component evaluator for the quadratic Ricci, natural Einstein and relative-harmonic reduced
      sources and reconstruct their complete 23-amplitude angular projection. Exact polynomial
      identities retain both transverse-traceless angular orientations. Authenticated differentiated
      first-field error bounds give ordinary metric and scalar two-jet enclosures, and outward interval
      evaluation bounds the actual quadratic forcing on the full causal collar. We also define an
      intrinsic shell scalar whose background and first variations vanish. Its sphere-mean second
      parameter derivative has exactly two simple zeros in each of two certified time brackets. A
      separate quantitative consequence of the retained second-response derivative bound proves
      persistence of two scalar-mean signs at positive times. The universal curvature and gauge formulas
      are established results; the contribution here is their explicit model-specific evaluation and
      reproducible certification. No complete numerical second waveform, finite-amplitude nonlinear
      family, invariant force or cosmological-origin conclusion is established.

     The universal Ricci and gauge identities are already in the literature [2, 3, 4]; the vanishing-
lower-order gauge criterion is also established. This note makes no external novelty or priority
claim. Its concrete contribution is the completed model-specific source calculation, actual interval
enclosures, intrinsic diagnostic and quantitative corollaries. The focused primary comparison is
included with the source.
