# What would it cost to prepare the parent population?

For a number-diagonal unit occupation of a single real scalar on the band $0\le q\le8$, the first wall sample gives $a_i=0.531657$ and $m_i/H_0=46.0931$. Integrating the physical energy $E_q=\sqrt{(q/a_i)^2+m_i^2}$ against $q^2dq/(2\pi^2a_i^3)$ gives an excess energy density of $2735.16\,H_0^4$. Using $(q/a_i)^2/(3 E_q)$ for the pressure gives $54.50\,H_0^4$. The comoving number is $8.64607\,H_0^3$. These are initial free-particle contributions above the chosen physical oscillator vacuum.

At the stored geometry the explicit matter term in the native squared-expansion equation increases by 1.6231% of the recorded $H^2$, and its scalar-force term decreases by 2.3930%. Adding the two changes gives −0.7699%. Those figures evaluate the algebraic source terms at fixed geometry and normal derivatives. The modified matter and scalar junctions would require different normal derivatives and a fresh constrained evolution to obtain a physical expansion rate. Taking the decay coupling small limits emitted pairs while leaving the prescribed initial ensemble energy intact. The pressure and force quoted here apply specifically to the number-diagonal ensemble.
