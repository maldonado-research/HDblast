# Next scientific targets

The present result supplies a conditional geometric bound, rather than a physical
law selecting a preparation. The next model-building task is to derive the initial
slice, scalar-labelled profile or an allowed profile class from an explicit
action, state or generating process.

1. Explain why a blast mechanism would produce the declared canonical data and
   an initial scalar-level area difference inside the specified radius. A radius
   chosen to obtain a stable sign is not itself a physical preparation principle.
2. Connect a justified energy/state condition to the geometric profile norm.
   This may require different estimates: an energy bound alone need not control
   the first-derivative supremum used here. Do not assume that implication.
3. Establish uniform higher-regularity and nonlinear remainder control for a
   physically defined class before claiming a common finite perturbation window.
   Causal coefficient control does not bound exterior data or the class's higher
   derivatives.
4. Extend the response to a registered proper-time history, specify matter
   interactions and a physical scale, and close the appropriate energy ledger
   before cosmological or observational claims.

The working paper needs author and independent specialist assessment, including
the retained analytic premises and contribution relative to the literature.
The unrestricted sign-change example remains valid, and no origin mechanism,
uniform nonlinear stability theorem or journal acceptance is established here.
