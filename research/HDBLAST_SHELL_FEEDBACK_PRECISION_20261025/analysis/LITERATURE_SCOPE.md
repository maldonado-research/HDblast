The contribution supported here is narrower: an authenticated,
model-specific component error comparison, a cancellation-preserving
boundary reconstruction, and finite retarded-feedback evaluation. General
Neumann-series bounds, integration by parts, moment reduction and normal

significance require a fuller theorem-by-theorem comparison and external
specialist assessment. This checkpoint contains no claim of observational
