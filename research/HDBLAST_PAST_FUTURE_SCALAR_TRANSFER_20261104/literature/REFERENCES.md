# Closest primary results and the scope of this note

4 November 2026, America/Los_Angeles. Relevant primary passages were checked;
this is a focused comparison, not an exhaustive priority or novelty review.

The mathematical contribution proposed here is a quantitative application to
one registered linear Einstein–scalar shell packet: an odd-in-time difference
estimate for its physical scalar coefficient, followed by a same-state transfer
of two retained future interval witnesses. Time reflection, higher graph
energies and widening an interval by a uniform absolute error are established
methods. They are not new general principles of this note.

## Direct mathematical precedents

1. Jochen Zahn, *Generalized Wentzell boundary conditions and quantum field
   theory*, [arXiv:1512.05512v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.05512v3),
   Annales Henri Poincaré 19 (2018), 163,
   [DOI:10.1007/s00023-017-0629-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00023-017-0629-3).
   Equation (6), domain (7), Proposition 1, and Eqs. (11)–(15) establish the
   positive bulk-plus-boundary realization, compatible power domains and higher
   derivative energy estimates. Proposition 3 retains the boundary contribution
   in causal energy estimates. These supply close methodological precedents;
   they do not identify this gravitational master norm with physical injected
   energy or give this packet's scalar reconstruction and constants.

2. Alessio Marta, *The nonlinear wave equation with nonlinear Wentzell boundary
   conditions on time-dependent compact Riemannian manifolds*,
   [arXiv:2404.05855v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.05855v2), 6 May 2024.
   Remark 4.3 explicitly uses time-reflection symmetry to extend the autonomous
   well-posedness statement to a two-sided interval. Remarks 4.4–4.5 discuss
   coupled interior/boundary energy exchange and finite propagation; Proposition
   5.1 treats nonautonomous evolution. Its compact geometry and coefficient
   assumptions differ from the present half-line construction. The present
   coefficient identity, sign estimates and physical trace must be proved for
   the registered model rather than imported from that theorem.

3. Abdelhakim Dahmani, Yacine Chitour, Hoai-Minh Nguyen and Christophe Roman,
   *One dimensional wave equation with in-domain localized damping and Wentzell
   boundary conditions*,
   [arXiv:2503.22439v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.22439v1), 28 March 2025.
   Theorem 3.6 and §4, especially Eqs. (4.1)–(4.3), give a product-space wave
   formulation with endpoint kinetic energies and boundary-flux cancellation.
   Its decay mechanism is damping on a finite interval. Neither that decay rate
   nor a backward damping theorem is being transferred to this reversible,
   time-dependent shell problem.

## A recent source checked and its different boundary law

Siwar Ben Said, *On the Wave of Klein-Gordon Type: Propagation Equation at a
Boundary and Interior Observability*,
[arXiv:2607.24839v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24839v1), 24 July 2026.
The introduction and §1.2, Eq. (1.3), use the complex first-order boundary
condition `∂_n u+i∂_t u=0`; Lemmas 1.2–1.3 give a unitary-group framework.
The observability problem, compact geometry and boundary law differ from the
positive endpoint-mass condition here. This recent work is useful context, not
a theorem proving the present scalar bound or its time-reflection identity.

reprove them.

The [inertial waveform capsule](https://github.com/maldonado-research/HDblast/tree/b2a2fce15ca1870fff3e4bc312a65480c9e839cf/research/HDBLAST_INERTIAL_SHELL_WAVEFORM_20261015)

`ell=10^-6` packet with its nonzero registered first velocity. Its `ell_S`
export is `ell*S`, so the scale conversion precedes interval widening. A
free-comparator witness alone, or the distinct `ell=10^-10` packet, cannot
replace those actual-field witnesses. The new comparison is not the first
amplitude calculation for this packet. Its coarse past zero does not transfer
the old narrow zero brackets or small tail sign.

The [earlier full-contact construction](https://github.com/maldonado-research/HDblast/blob/f4001e5d94e6388bfff4f82dee63c174f2dfd5eb/research/HDBLAST_FULL_SECOND_ORDER_CONTACT_20261014/FULL_FINITE_ANGULAR_SECOND_ORDER_CONTACT.md)
also constructs a conditional formal complete second Taylor coefficient through
future contact. This must be distinguished from
the outstanding quantitative particular past preparation, controlled nonlinear
remainder and effective finite-amplitude observable. No blanket absence of all
second-order existence results is claimed.

A bounded search for 2025–2026 boundary-wave and brane-perturbation work found
the recent contrast above, but does not establish absence of closer work,
originality, journal-level significance, radiation production or evidence for
a higher-dimensional origin of the Big Bang. Independent specialist assessment
remains appropriate.
