Raychaudhuri/Hamiltonian identities are standard geometrical averaging
machinery, commonly used in Buchert's formalism. They are derived here
without adopting dust, a matter flow or a four-dimensional equation of
state. The normalized Einstein-Hilbert functional is critical at a round
Einstein metric; its conformal Hessian and diffeomorphism invariance are
standard scalar-curvature variation results. The source-specific advance
is their application to this selected exact-family tangent, complete clock
and actual scalar-driven trace, not a new universal averaging or Yamabe
theorem. Earlier Q-square algebra is not claimed as new.

The observable source itself records the standard varying-embedding and
second-order gauge machinery from Bruni et al., CQG 14 (1997) 2585,
arXiv:gr-qc/9609040, and Mars, CQG 22 (2005) 3325,
arXiv:gr-qc/0507005. Those references concern the registered observable and
gauge conventions; they are not nonlinear shell existence theorems.

This component performs no new external literature retrieval. The analytic
existence, regularity and first-field premises remain those explicitly
retained by the exact-family source. Its finite algebra reports do not
independently prove them.
