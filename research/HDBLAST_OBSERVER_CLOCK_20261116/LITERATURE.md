The actual finite-regularity ODE argument must provide a common state domain and short interval, inward retention at the initial time boundary, and continuous first and second parameter variations. Extending ODE coefficients for that proof does not extend the physical Einstein solution. A longer prescribed proper-time interval needs a separate continuation estimate.

A normal congruence uses the future timelike normal within the shell to its initial spacelike cut. The shell's outward spacelike normal describes a different direction. Under the retained convention K=(sigma/6)h, an intrinsic unit timelike geodesic has ambient acceleration (sigma/6)n; it is generally not ambient free fall. Classical one-sided Israel fields do not provide a globally smooth shell-crossing Christoffel field.

Positive cut metrics, common flow existence and the appropriate global injectivity argument must be established before an observer coordinate cylinder is used. Proper-clock inverses require a common image. Scalar, tensor, velocity and frame derivatives must include moving evaluation. Geometric covariance also requires transporting the declared physical anchor and labels consistently.
