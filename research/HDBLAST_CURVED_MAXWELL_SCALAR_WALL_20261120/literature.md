# Prior work on electromagnetic waves in a closed geometry

## The closest scalar and electromagnetic model

Murata and Kobayashi already constructed three mutually orthogonal Abelian gauge fields in a closed Friedmann universe. Their Eq. (45) chooses invariant one-forms on the three-sphere. The three fields have equal amplitudes and their combined stress is isotropic. Equation (57) couples them to a canonical scalar through a common electromagnetic kinetic coefficient. Setting their parity-odd coupling to zero gives precisely the intrinsic interaction relevant here. Equation (47) contains both the electric term and the curvature-induced magnetic term. Equation (58) shows how their difference drives the scalar. A single oriented Maxwell field generally has anisotropic stress. Choosing three equal, independent fields is a physical assumption about the matter content and preparation. [Murata and Kobayashi, 2021, Sec. III](https://arxiv.org/html/2107.07199#S3.E45)

On the unit round three-sphere, the lowest transverse one-forms have curl eigenvalues $+2$ or $-2$ according to their handedness. Either handedness provides an orthonormal invariant frame and the positive Hodge Laplacian has eigenvalue $4$ on these modes. The sphere has no nonzero harmonic one-form of eigenvalue zero. A 2026 computation extends the one-form spectrum to homogeneous, potentially squashed three-spheres and recovers the round result. These spectral facts fix the coefficient of the angular energy for the selected mode. [Explicit vector spherical harmonics, Eq. (6)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.03426) · [Hodge Laplacian on homogeneous three-spheres](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05406)

## Propagation in five dimensions and the wall interaction

Kodama and Ishibashi derived the vector Maxwell propagation equation on a higher-dimensional warped spherical background. For an uncharged source-free background with three angular dimensions and the lowest transverse vector mode, their Eq. (64) reduces to $D_a(rD^a A)-4A/r=0$, with $r$ the areal radius and $D$ the two-dimensional covariant derivative. This provides a direct check on bulk radial propagation. Dvali, Gabadadze and Shifman give a five-dimensional Maxwell action with an induced four-dimensional tangential Maxwell term on a brane. Their induced coefficient is constant. Testing energy capture here requires specifying the incoming bulk data and their coupling at the boundary, then following the change in the wall scalar energy. [Kodama and Ishibashi, Sec. 4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/hep-th/0308128#S4.E64) · [Dvali, Gabadadze and Shifman, Eq. (9)](https://arxiv.org/html/hep-th/0010071#S2.E9)

Danielsson, Panizo and Van Hemelryck matched self-gravitating AdS wave geometries across a dark bubble and studied the spreading of finite-width beams. Their brane electromagnetic field is sourced by a bulk three-form field strength. Its gravitational backreaction is a useful comparison for an incident wave with transverse structure. The choice of bulk matter, the plane-wave geometry and the boundary conditions differ from a closed wall irradiated by a Maxwell triplet. [Self-gravitating electromagnetic waves in the dark bubble model, June 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16547)

## HDblast's previous tests

The January 20 D-Blast 3 notes proposed a Vaidya mass jump and treated a radiation bath as a separate assumption. The September 19 archive's vector harmonic test concerned a metric perturbation on a de Sitter slice. The November 19 HDblast photon calculation is a closer precedent. It explicitly uses closed-universe Maxwell oscillators with conformal frequencies $L=2,3,\ldots$ and degeneracy $2(L^2-1)$. The rolling wall field produced photons from an assumed vacuum and the follow-up tested dependence on the number of retained modes. That addresses a different initial state and direction of energy flow from a classical wave arriving from the bulk. The November 20 incident-wave study demonstrated a short radiation-dominated expansion using null dust and a specified capture law. The comparison to make here is how much scalar excitation actually follows when the incoming electromagnetic data and the interaction are specified together.
