# Earlier model comparisons and the author portfolio

The 8 October HDBLAST half-space model already gives a four-dimensional replica of a five-dimensional boundary response. Its Robin spectral transform replaces the flat half-space field by four-dimensional fields with a continuous mass label. With matched Gaussian preparation, calibrated probe and measurement law, the resulting smeared boundary statistics agree. That proof uses a stationary flat geometry, nondynamical gravity and a specific quadratic coupling. Its susceptibility and fluctuation spectrum cannot be imported as numerical inputs for the present de Sitter shell. The November time-domain construction instead starts from the registered shell master, its endpoint mass and its proper-time scalar readout. Two other projects clarify what a comparison can establish. The DMDE frozen-source result separates a difference in input profiles from the observable change obtained after transport. Here, independently prescribed identical physical sources would impose a stronger condition than choosing a four-dimensional source after specifying a target trace. The HRF composite-alternative analysis shows why statistical discrimination requires probability laws for the same data. If two alternatives yield the same deterministic response and the readout and conditional noise laws coincide, their output laws coincide too. A noiseless template equality alone leaves the noise assumption open.
