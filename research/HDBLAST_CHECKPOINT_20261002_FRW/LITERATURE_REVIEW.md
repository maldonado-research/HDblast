# Focused literature update

Search and canonical metadata/full-text passage checks used Firecrawl. This is a targeted review of transition regularity, vacuum preparation and curved stress, not an exhaustive web survey or proof of novelty.

1. **Cristian Armendariz-Picon and Alberto Diez-Tejedor, Cosmic Energy Density: Particles, Fields and the Vacuum (2023), [arXiv:2305.16293](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16293).** Section 2.3, Eq. 43, distinguishes the ultraviolet particle and coherent energy terms and requires faster-than-k^-2 Bogoliubov-amplitude decay for a finite excitation-energy contribution. The sharp-transition discussion and Section 3.4 show the k^-4 occupation tail when the scale factor's second derivative jumps. Their smooth examples avoid this artifact. This directly supports the archive-regularity question; their cosmological transition is not the HDBLAST shell and supplies no shell matching constants.

2. **Jose A. R. Cembranos, Luis J. Garay and Álvaro Parra-López, Influence of spatial curvature in cosmological particle production (2026), [arXiv:2602.04970](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04970).** Equations 33–34 state a frequency-adiabaticity diagnostic and finite-time WKB vacuum data; Section 3.1 compares instantaneous and smoother exit prescriptions. Its conformally coupled scalar, spatial-curvature choices and de Sitter/static idealization differ from our minimal scalar and archived spatially flat shell. A finite-time approximate occupation prescription is not, by itself, certification of a Hadamard state or renormalized stress in our model.

3. **Hamzeh Alavirad, Time-resolved digital quantum simulation of cosmological particle creation in a de Sitter-radiation transition (2026), [arXiv:2605.04099](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04099), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/467g-1dk1).** Section 2, Eqs. 2–8, explicitly matches a and a' while a''/a jumps and derives n_k=1/[4(k eta_e)^4]. The authors identify this as a sudden-transition benchmark. Section 5 limits the quantum-circuit result to a controlled truncated model; the hardware run has an approximately 1e-2 noise floor and is not quantitative verification of cosmological particle creation. Its reproduced per-mode occupation does not remove the ultraviolet energy issue or validate an HDBLAST radiation era.

The 2026 papers are useful controls and methodological comparisons. They do not bypass the background, state and common-action matching requirements. The earlier checkpoint's references on renormalization and adiabatic-state regularity remain relevant; no historical claim equating all finite-order adiabatic states with Hadamard states is adopted.

The current leading-jump and initial-state calculations were derived independently and compared with the literature. The results are project-specific diagnostics based on known quantum-field methods; no external mathematical priority is asserted.
