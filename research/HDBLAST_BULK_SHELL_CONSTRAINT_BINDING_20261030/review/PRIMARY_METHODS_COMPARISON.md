initial-constraint and harmonic-boundary review. It does not establish

## Established geometric and scalar-shell identities

T. Shiromizu, K. Maeda and M. Sasaki, *The Einstein equations on the 3-brane
world*, [arXiv:gr-qc/9910076](https://arxiv.org/html/gr-qc/9910076).
The primary body was read around the Codazzi projection, junction condition
(15), and conservation/Bianchi discussion (21)-(22). The geometric relation
between normal bulk flux and intrinsic brane divergence is established
literature. The stated cosmological-constant bulk and brane stress assumptions
do not replace HDBLAST's scalar-dependent tension or its particular quadratic
source maps. The Codazzi relation itself is not a new theorem here.

K. Maeda and D. Wands, *Dilaton-gravity on the brane*,
[arXiv:hep-th/0008188](https://arxiv.org/html/hep-th/0008188).
The primary body was read in section II, including the bulk scalar equation,
Codazzi equation (11), and scalar/metric junction conditions. It treats a bulk
scalar with arbitrary brane tension and potential functions, with possible
conformal matter coupling. Its introduction explicitly notes that induced
four-dimensional equations generally do not close on their own. This is a
close conceptual comparison for the scalar contribution to shell conservation.
Its normal orientation, gravitational coupling and jump conventions must be
converted before importing any sign or factor into this capsule.

In the present conventions the established off-shell relation is

```text
E_na = div_gamma(R)_a - D_a tr_gamma(R) - P D_a phi,
R = K_out - sigma gamma/6, P = n_out phi + sigma_prime/2.
```

On the fixed shell, with zero background and first physical metric residual,
the scalar condition has a visible quadratic role: before setting the first
scalar residual `P1` to zero, the five exported natural Einstein source rows
retain `-2 P1 D_a eta`. That finite source-map identity is the registration
check in this capsule. A proof or successful computation of it is not a
derivation of the universe's initial conditions.

## Recent related work and its limits

*Second-order slow rotation of wormholes in Einstein-scalar-Gauss-Bonnet
gravity*, [arXiv:2609.18475v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.18475v1), September
2026. The primary abstract and introduction were read, including the discussion
of the throat source, junction conditions and internal regular singular point.
This work includes monopolar and quadrupolar corrections and compares shooting
with collocation. It reinforces the methodological need to include the source
response at a junction. Its higher-curvature equations and stationary rotating
wormhole setting differ from this Einstein-scalar expanding-shell problem.
Its Frobenius construction or solutions cannot be imported as an existence
result for HDBLAST. No result from that model is used in the exact checks.

*General Relativity with Finite Boundaries*,
[arXiv:2609.31928v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.31928v1), September 2026.
The primary abstract, opening formulation discussion and stated conformal
boundary data were inspected. The thesis fixes the induced conformal class
and the trace of extrinsic curvature and studies linearized gravity about
Minkowski, de Sitter and anti-de Sitter backgrounds. Those boundary data differ
from the physical scalar/Israel junction laws here. No material-shell
well-posedness theorem is obtained merely by citing a finite-boundary result.
