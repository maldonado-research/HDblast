# Quantities still needed for an effective nonlinear window

The new interfaces expose dependencies; their constants are not evaluated.

1. Fix the exact compatible preparation and affine amplitude parameter. Verify
   its reflected past-domain application, state, inverse, subsidiary and
   regularity margins on a common parameter interval.
2. Quantify the retained uniform first/second variation estimates. Obtain
   component and mixed spatial/parameter norms on the necessary compact tube.
3. Bound the augmented proper-time observer/Jacobi flow and its first/second
   parameter variations, including initial normal data, finite-atlas comparison
   constants and the common noncrossing domain.
4. Compute full cut-metric `P,Q,lambda,Lambda` and scalar `E1,E2` budgets.
   Alternatively exploit justified angular-oscillation or odd-residual moduli.
   A derivative at zero alone is insufficient for any uniform input budget.
5. Insert outward-rounded or exact bounds into a sufficient remainder guard.
   The guard `<106` belongs to the retained coarse central cell with margins
   `63/53`; a different time bracket needs its own endpoint margins.

Only after all these joins are certified would the inequalities give a
nonzero numerical amplitude regime for this particular moment and family.
They do not predict the scalar mean, shell volume, every harmonic, full-field
zeros, a physical source, radiation or a higher-dimensional cosmological origin.
