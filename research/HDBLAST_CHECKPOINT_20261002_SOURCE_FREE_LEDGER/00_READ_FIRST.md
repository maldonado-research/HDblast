# Registered source-free ledger diagnosis

This checkpoint investigates the conservation failures preserved in the three
earlier metric-response experiments. It is a post hoc diagnosis of known failed
data, with its implementation and numerical criteria published before this
diagnostic is evaluated. It does not retrospectively preregister the original
experiment or change any earlier scientific FAIL.

The fixed question is whether Simpson integration of the saved stress history
accounts for a gate-scale conservation defect on the single interval
`eta in [-2.5, -1.5]`, after the compact source has ended. An exact free-flow
antiderivative provides a comparison constructed from the retained initial
modes, independently of the measured final stress.

All twelve cases are required: two source histories, two saved resolutions and
three fixed comoving cutoffs. Every saved profile sample inside the selected
interval is compared. The actual unprojected complex modes, momentum nodes,
weights and histories are preserved in small input capsules with their original
NPY member bytes and public source hashes.

Read `PROTOCOL.md`, `EXPERIMENT.json`, the two implementation notes and
`FULL_REGISTRATION.json` for the frozen definitions. The old analytic proposal
is retained in `theory/`; this protocol makes its sampling, normalization,
serialization and Simpson arithmetic choices explicit before evaluation.

At preparation time the diagnostic outcome is **UNCOMPUTED**. A positive ledger
attribution would address only the chosen source-free interval. It would not
establish the active-source integral, inherited mode accuracy, accepted metric
calibration, coupled dynamics, a higher-dimensional blast or a hot Big Bang.

The numerical implementation and internal reviews are AI-assisted. All
scientific claims require the recorded calculations and their stated scope;
no external peer review or fundamental mathematical novelty is claimed.

This read-first document is frozen at registration. Subsequent outcomes are
recorded in `reports/RESULTS.md` and `outputs/`; consult those for the result.
