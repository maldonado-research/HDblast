The proposed experiment is a prospective registration of a post hoc saved-data diagnosis of the known failed metric trial. Its outcomes are uncomputed. The original three failures, 59 conservation failures, gates and stopped validators remain unchanged. This diagnostic can attribute a source-free Simpson error; it cannot grant the old calibration PASS or establish an active-source, coupled or physical cosmology result.

The unrestricted free-mode formulas, independent oscillatory ledger, signed flow-density projection and decomposition `D_S = D_cont + E_Q` are mathematically consistent with the producer's free ODE. Keep `Re(c)` and the raw `u,w` components; normalization projection would change the question. The antiderivative uses the initial raw modes and background, rather than measured final stress. Numerical closure of the decomposition checks arithmetic, not independent physical accuracy.

Root resolved two ambiguities before freeze:

- Compare R and P at **every retained sample in the inclusive interval [-2.5,-1.5]**: 129 coarse and 257 fine samples. The interval begins at retained observation index 4 and ends at index 5. Do not silently include earlier samples between -3 and -2.5 or select a smaller mask after results.

Compute analytic `L=-1/eta` at diagnostic precision from exact saved time. Differences from rounded stored geometry belong to the comparison error; substituting that rounded L into the continuum identity would define another formula.

The scheduled grids are feasible in memory with selective member loading and bounded processing. Coarse uses 8192 nodes and fine 16384. Across both sources, a single profile pass requires 10,534,912 node/sample pairs at maximum cutoff; two arithmetic precisions require 21,069,824. Dense Python arbitrary-precision objects or a complex exponential at every pair could violate the original 900 s/262144 KiB limits. A fixed uniform phase recurrence and bounded blocks can reduce the cost, but full scheduled-shape synthetic timing/memory evidence is needed before freeze. Partial or resource-failed results cannot satisfy an all-twelve-case criterion.
