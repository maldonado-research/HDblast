# Primary sources, comparison and scope

This focused audit was performed on 6 November 2026. It compares the
quantitative regularity step with the closest inspected primary sources;
it is not an exhaustive priority search or an external novelty review.

1. **E. Delay, Smooth compactly supported solutions of some underdetermined
   elliptic PDE, with gluing applications**,
   [arXiv:1003.0535v4](https://arxiv.org/html/1003.0535v4).
   Theorem 3.8 provides the weighted projected normal isomorphism under
   its adjoint hypotheses. Lemma 10.2 gives the qualitative high-order
   estimate with a potential L2 term; Appendix 10 tracks scaled-cover
   assumptions. Proposition 4.1 and Corollary 4.3 address boundary decay.
   Those numerical constants are not supplied for this Einstein–scalar
   representative. The complete-line proof here evaluates a finite-mode
   regularity estimate explicitly while retaining the earlier inverse
   as a premise. It does not replace the general gluing theory.

2. **B. Le Floch and P. G. LeFloch, Harmonic, radial, and shell stability
   of the weighted Einstein constraints on the sphere at infinity**,
   [arXiv:2607.28599v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.28599v1), July 2026.
   Theorem 2.4 and Appendix A give relevant precedent for weighted
   fourth-order Hamiltonian and second-order momentum stability using
   Poincare, Korn and Hardy estimates. Their conical localization and
   infinity regime differ from the compact physical annulus and its
   exponential boundary weight. No numerical constant from that setting
   is substituted into this calculation.

3. **B. Le Floch and P. G. LeFloch, Optimal localization for the Einstein
   constraints**, [arXiv:2312.17706v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.17706v2),
   revised August 2026. This supplies a broader variational projection
   and weighted-regularity comparison in asymptotically Euclidean vacuum
   geometry. Matter prescription, dimension and localization domain are
   different here. The actual canonical Hessian must be derived separately.

4. **B. Le Floch and P. G. LeFloch, The seeds and silhouettes of optimal
   gravitational shielding**,
   [arXiv:2610.39262v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39262v1), October 2026.
   This is current primary context for exterior vacuum seed constructions
   and the companion projection theory. It supplies no coefficient or
   extension constant for the present compact Einstein–scalar annulus;
   no theorem from it is needed for the elementary estimate here.

5. **P. Isett, Y. Mao, S.-J. Oh and Z. Tao, Integral formulas for
   under/overdetermined differential operators via recovery on curves
   and the finite-dimensional cokernel condition I: General theory**,
   [arXiv:2509.04617v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.04617v1).
   Support-controlled right inverses via adjoint recovery, duality and
   finite-rank obstruction corrections have established general antecedents.
   The specialized compact comparison and weighted minimization used here
   are not claimed as a new general inverse principle. The applicable
   domain and symbol conditions still require their own verification.

6. **J. Isenberg and D. Maxwell, A Phase Space Approach to the Conformal
   Construction of Non-Vacuum Initial Data Sets in General Relativity**,
   [arXiv:2106.15027v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2106.15027v2).
   Canonical matter fields and conjugate densities provide established
   context for the distinction between affine scalar density and physical
   normal scalar momentum. The present compact weighted construction is
   different from the conformal method.
