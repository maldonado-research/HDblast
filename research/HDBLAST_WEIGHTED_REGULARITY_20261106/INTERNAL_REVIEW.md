majorants, scalar and momentum coercivity, complete-line transformation,
normal operators, distribution-to-Sobolev bootstrap, physical covariant
conversion and canonical ADM formulas. This is internal review, not
external peer review or observational evidence.

Specific checks addressed the radial volume factor rho^(3/2), the tensor

sector's absent angular-gradient row, fixed-density momentum normalization,
and probability-sphere versus full geometric versus reflected-copy norms.
The Sobolev estimates use explicitly defined derivative-square-sum norms;
standard Fourier Bessel conventions have their separate conversion factors.

The global bootstrap first obtains regularity from the distribution
equation and the low norm. It does not assume the desired high norm in
order to prove it. Potentials use the retained dotted domain. Compact
approximation and bounded physical reconstruction establish zero extension,
without adding ordinary endpoint traces or a different boundary realization.

Independent symbolic normal-operator and ADM expansions and signed Taylor
probes supplemented the continuous audit. Those finite probes are checks,
not a substitute for the global Bell-polynomial and coupled-ODE majorant
proofs. Exact public helpers recompute the majorants and coefficient
envelopes; they do not import private report values as an inverse oracle.
Repeated normal/optimized runs test reproducibility rather than supply
independent evidence for the physical hypothesis.

The closed replay authenticates the pinned public source/code inventory
and checks finite algebra and rational inequalities. Its corruption
controls cover the trust protocol and a genuinely false coefficient

or implicit-family theorem, external originality, or nonlinear evolution.
