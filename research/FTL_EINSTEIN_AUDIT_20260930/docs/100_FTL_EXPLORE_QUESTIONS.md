This is the FTL-only question set recovered from the September 8 handoff, rather than the mixed-program article collection prepared earlier.

The technical prompts identify calculations to explain or review; their presence does not certify a completed proof, executable release, or empirical result. Read the equation/status documentation before presenting an answer. Workflow/source-selection questions such as 073, 083, and 084 describe source-era planning, which later dependence/source-lock reports may supersede. Editorial changes: question 053 removes an unsupported breakthrough presupposition, and question 075 specifies the two-posterior total-variation identity.
