# Reviewing the FTL working hypothesis

Critiques and corrections are welcome. This program is intended to make assumptions and failure conditions inspectable.

When opening an issue or proposing a change, include the document/version, equation identifier, precise claim being challenged, assumptions used, and a reproducible derivation or counterexample. For computational issues, provide the actual input identity, environment, parameters, output, and minimal reproduction procedure. Do not post credentials or restricted datasets.

Separate these questions:

- Does an equation follow from its stated assumptions?
- Does the numerical method evaluate that equation accurately?
- Does the proposed diagnostic have a justified statistical interpretation?
- Does a physical model derive the assumptions and observational bridge?
- Do observations distinguish that model from alternatives?

A successful software test answers only its defined methods question. Synthetic output cannot be promoted as physical observation. Review by an AI assistant must be described as internal assistance, not external peer review or independent scientific replication.

Preserve historical errors and superseded definitions in the correction history. Keep Q occupancy distinct from first positive severity. Do not silently alter cosmology normalization, input priors, ringdown parameter conventions, or frozen decision rules.

This folder covers the Einstein Audit for FTL Hidden Sectors only. Other research programs sharing gravitational-wave terminology do not validate it. Cite the folder-specific metadata instead of the repository's HDBLAST root citation when discussing the FTL documentation.
