## Why the stress and scalar source must share one action

With x=M² and the conventions in the action document,

    T_munu = -2 δGamma/(sqrt(-g) δg^munu),
    S = -δGamma/(sqrt(-g) δx) = Q/2,
    rhodot + 3H(rho+p) = xdot S.

The finite local action includes -V(x)+F(x)R+alpha R². Changing F(x) changes both the metric source and the scalar source: Q shifts by -2F_x R. A pressure prescription chosen separately from the mass source can fail this relation. Constant curvature coefficients and the reference matching conditions are explicit choices; conservation does not determine them.

In the static future, energy can be written in terms of |beta_k|², but pressure and Q also contain alpha_k beta_k* interference. Their follow-up differences from occupation-only estimates reach approximately 0.05055 and 0.02348. Those contributions were retained. A particle spectrum cannot by itself establish the source that the gravitational and scalar junction equations require. Nor does Gaussian particle production by itself give a thermal fluid.

The nonnegative mass profile x=r(2B-1)² touches zero without becoming negative. The exact mode equation and positive-reference subtraction remain regular there. A low-momentum canonical frequency squared can still become negative because of the separate curvature term -a''/a; the numerical evolution does not replace such modes with an instantaneous particle vacuum.

## Next calculation: derive and evolve the source in the existing 5D model

The next target should be a separately registered, smooth coupled evolution derived from the model action. Its first scientific question is whether the quantum source can be included while maintaining the bulk constraints, all junction conditions and an energy ledger over a resolved interval. A radiation-era claim requires further physics and a substantially longer validated evolution.

1. **Fix the actual action and matching data.** Pin the existing bulk potential, brane tension, orientation and normalization, together with the scalar coupling x(phi_b)=m0²+G²(phi_b-phi_star)², field multiplicity, physical parameter range and finite gravitational couplings. The benchmark's finite conditions can define an explicit model choice but are not empirical measurements of those couplings. Specify how the same finite terms appear in the brane action and quantum expectation value so they are counted once.

2. **Derive all three sourced junction equations before evolving.** With the inherited orientation and normalization, the matter contributions enter the established junction structure

       n·A = [sigma+kappa5² rho_tot]/6,
       n·B = [sigma-kappa5²(2rho_tot+3p_tot)]/6,
       n·phi = -[sigma_phi+kappa5² J_phi]/2.

   Re-derive the signs and factors directly from that pinned action, including local curvature variations. The quantum scalar source is

       J_phi = x_phi Q/2 = G²(phi_b-phi_star)Q.

   Supply rho, p and J_phi from the same state and finite prescription. A tension-only replacement omits the independent pressure and scalar responses. If geometric counterterms are moved to the junction's left side, remove the identical contribution from the right side.

3. **Prepare smooth initial data and evolve the modes with the geometry.** Construct bulk and shell initial data that satisfy the constraints and specify a smooth incoming history or a demonstrably admissible ultraviolet state. The artificial exactly static preparation of the present control is not automatically available on the actual shell. Evolve the physical mode functions, phi_b and the shell/bulk geometry together, with source evaluation using the evolving fields. Do not replay the prescribed a=exp(A B) history or interpolate the nonsmooth archive into a claim of self-consistency.

4. **Specify the treatment of higher derivatives within the EFT.** R² and derivative-dependent local stress can raise the differential order of the coupled equations. Register whether a justified order-reduced semiclassical system or a larger constrained initial-value system is used, its domain of validity and its initial data. Compare the selected method in its controlled weak-source limit. Numerical stability obtained by omitting terms would not establish the original action's evolution.

5. **Close the work and bulk-gravity ledgers.** Measure the quantum exchange

       rhodot_Q+3H(rho_Q+p_Q) = J_phi phidot_b,

   together with the compensating scalar/bulk energy flux, tension contribution, signed junction budget and Weyl balance. Require bulk constraints, junction residuals and this ledger to improve under independent bulk-grid, mode-band, momentum-quadrature, time-step and source-update refinement. Include zero-source and wrong-sign/omitted-source diagnostics. Start with a controlled weak-source regime and extend only when these gates pass; stop and report constraint loss, an unresolved turning point or cutoff/EFT violation.

6. **Treat decay and thermalization as an additional model calculation.** The present free Gaussian field has no decay channel. A later interacting matter action must supply any decay current and its equal opposite energy transfer to daughter fields. Thermalization requires interaction rates or distribution evolution, an energy ledger, a suitable equation of state and sustained expansion. An inserted radiation fluid, an assumed decay rate or a transient positive particle energy cannot by itself establish a hot epoch.

The earlier derived-particle-source 5D work remains relevant negative evidence: below its stated gravity cutoff it did not reproduce the phenomenological-friction radiation outcome, while the strong-backreaction runs lost reliability. A better quantum source calculation does not erase those observations. Any new parameters or matching conditions that change the physical model must be declared before the new evolution, with old outcomes retained.

A useful next deliverable is therefore a registered action-and-junction specification, a constrained smooth initial state, and a short coupled weak-source evolution with resolved energy transfer. Only a passing result would justify extending to appreciable backreaction, repeated mass-zero passages and eventual decay/thermalization. No such coupled experiment has been executed in this checkpoint.
