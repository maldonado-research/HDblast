This computational methods checkpoint tests a minimally coupled scalar quantum field on a prescribed smooth Friedmann background, using a common local-action convention for renormalized energy density, pressure and paired scalar current. The mass squared remains nonnegative and touches zero once; the incoming state is the exact static vacuum. Exact symbolic exchange/finite-cutoff trace identities, numerical mode evolution, independent Radau spot checks, static controls and preserved preregistrations are included.

The original registered matrix remains FAIL: its flat control passed, but the curved pressure changed by 1.0089% between momentum cutoffs 96 and 192 and exceeded the fixed allowance. A separately registered cutoff-384 follow-up passes all 13 acceptance groups. Its pressure changes by 0.2291% of the global comparison scale, within the unchanged absolute-plus-relative tolerance. This is finite-cutoff numerical evidence, not a certified infinite-cutoff result.

The package contains equations, source code, compact executed numerical curves, original failures, repair history, independently audited results, figures, literature notes and complete reproduction instructions. Large raw complex-mode arrays are regenerated by the replay; their retained local hashes are recorded. AI systems assisted implementation, derivation and internal review; this is not external peer review.

This prescribed-background control does not evolve the five-dimensional shell or backreaction and does not establish decay, thermalization, a sustained radiation era, the proposed higher-dimensional blast mechanism, or a new cosmological solution. The finite local matching coefficients are a declared scheme, not measured model couplings. The heavy-regulator argument remains conditional and has no certified uniform remainder bound. This is a separate scoped methods record referencing earlier HDBLAST studies; it does not revise their claims.
