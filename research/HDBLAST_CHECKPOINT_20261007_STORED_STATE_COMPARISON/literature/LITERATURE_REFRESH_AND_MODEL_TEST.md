## The most useful new concrete test example

Rao et al., [*Correlated parity violation in gravity and electromagnetism from five-dimensional spacetime*, `2608.09299v3`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09299v3), submitted 10 August and revised 3 September 2026, provides a specific example of a restricted joint prediction. Sec.II.A assumes a compact spacelike circle and a low-energy KK zero-mode sector. Sec.V constructs a particular parity-odd teleparallel parent action, Eq.(39), whose reduction Eq.(41) fixes the relative coefficients of gravitational Nieh–Yan and electromagnetic Chern–Simons operators. The common parent action supplies the restriction; arbitrary independent 4D couplings would not.

In the spatially flat FRW electromagnetic-vacuum background, Eqs.(44) and (48) give, in the declared variables,

`omega_GW,A^2 = k^2 + c p_A k (ln phi)'`,

`omega_EM,A^2 = k^2 + 6 c p_A k (ln phi)'`.

Thus Eq.(49) fixes `Delta omega_EM,A^2 = 6 Delta omega_GW,A^2`. This is a relation between linear propagation coefficients, described explicitly in the text as a leading-WKB dispersion relation. It is not a factor-six relation between arbitrary measured CMB and GW quantities. The physical branch has `phi>0`; the scalar evolution also changes common GW friction.

Sec.VI distinguishes three quantities that must stay separate: the photon polarization rotation, a fixed-k GW eikonal helicity phase, and the Fourier-domain CBC waveform phase. For the same propagation endpoints only, the first two obey `Delta alpha = 3 Delta Phi_GW,WKB` at leading order. CMB photons from last scattering and low-redshift binary GWs do not have the same endpoints. The source explicitly requires full-waveform reanalysis including radion friction for quantitative CBC limits, and leaves a complete cosmological evolution/data analysis for future work. Existing quoted CMB indications are described as foreground-sensitive, not conclusive evidence. No source observational number is imported into HDBLAST.

The transferable idea is to derive a correlated observable restriction from a specified action before fitting data. These parity couplings are not implied by HDBLAST, and adding them would define a different model. Even the source's relation could be reproduced by a specially constrained 4D effective action; it would test this model family against specified rivals, not logically prove a fifth dimension against every possible 4D theory.

## A recent white-hole/brane result with explicit missing bulk closure

Sengupta–Singha, [*Bouncing Dust Collapse and Black-to-White Hole Transition on the Brane*, `2610.04353v1`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04353v1), submitted 3 October 2026, is closer in language to a bulk-origin hypothesis but has a very specific physical construction. Sec.2 uses the Shtanov–Sahni model with a second timelike direction, negative brane tension, Z2 symmetry, a tuned effective cosmological constant and a high-energy limit neglecting induced gravity. Sec.3 sets the interior Weyl/dark-radiation contribution to zero and assumes exactly homogeneous, spherical closed dust. Its negative density-squared correction permits a classical bounce. A spacelike extra dimension would give a different sign; the timelike signature is a model choice, not an interchangeable label.

The important limitation is stated in the paper itself. Conservation and tracelessness of the projected Weyl stress do not show that a regular five-dimensional bulk realizes it. Sec.4.4 says flux orientation is a bulk boundary condition that the brane equations do not fix. Sec.5.3 chooses the exterior patching and does not show a regular inducing bulk; stability beyond the Cauchy horizon is separate. Sec.2.2 and the discussion leave the actual timelike KK spectrum and inhomogeneous/nonspherical stability unexamined. It supplies neither quantum state preparation nor particle production/thermalization. Its primary relevance is the need to distinguish a solved effective brane model from a completed bulk geometry and energy-flow calculation.

## A foundational origin proposal, now read beyond its old snippet

Pourhasan–Afshordi–Mann, [*Out of the White Hole: A Holographic Origin for the Big Bang*, `1309.1487v2`](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.1487v2), first submitted 5 September 2013, starts from a DGP action with 5D gravity and a 4D induced-gravity brane, Eq.(1.1). Its collapse/Schwarzschild-bulk discussion is an existing predecessor, not new evidence for HDBLAST.

The useful quantitative bridge is Sec.4: assume a Z2 brane, a thermal massless scalar atmosphere in a 4+1-dimensional locally flat treatment, linear scalar gravity, low momentum compared with temperature, and static equilibrium between brane and atmosphere. Eqs.(4.4)–(4.13) map thermal density correlations to a brane curvature spectrum with amplitude proportional to `(Tb/M5)^6`. The thermal atmosphere and equilibrium are premises, not consequences of a nonzero Bogoliubov coefficient. The next page explicitly says the observed red tilt is not predicted by the simple free-thermal-field model and postpones its explanation. Its old cosmological numbers are historical, not current adopted constraints.

This predecessor illustrates what a bulk-origin claim must add to an effective pulse: a specified source state, correlation function, brane matching rule and observable prediction. It does not supply HDBLAST's current state enclosure, action, flux normalization or reheating history.

## QFT methods that sharpen the state/source contract

Ghoshal–Pal–Strumia, [*Particle production from bubble collisions*, `2607.15279v1`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15279v1), submitted 16 July 2026, distinguishes the exact vacuum-persistence effective action from a quadratic off-shell truncation evaluated on an approximate self-interacting wall collision. Sec.2 argues that this combined approximation can produce gauge/field-coordinate dependent hard-particle rates, even a spurious free-theory rate. The authors propose an on-shell partonic approximation for ultra-relativistic, nearly freely passing walls, with its own high-energy limits. Sec.8 keeps hard production, later soft coherent evolution and thermalization separate. This is a recent preprint argument about a particular approximation, not an independently settled review of every production calculation. Its 4D scalar phase-transition walls are not a 5D brane collision. For HDBLAST, an externally prescribed pulse remains a valid declared test-field experiment, while interpreting it as a physical collision requires the source dynamics, full correlated contacts and appropriate invariant production observables.

Li, [*Stochastic inflation as an open quantum system II: open effective field theory and stochastic matching*, `2605.21929v3`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21929v3), revised 28 September 2026, derives rather than freely assigns the stochastic sector. Sec.3 assumes exact de Sitter, flat slices, a BD bath, weak coupling and temporal/spatial scale separation. The environment is the short-mode sector of the same inflaton field, not unseen bulk matter. Sec.5 matches non-Gaussian, history-dependent influence-functional kernels to full-theory SK correlators/form factors; Gaussian Markovianity does not extend automatically beyond that order. The discussion leaves general backreacting/continuum completion open. The transfer is to preserve response, noise/correlations, preparation state and memory as jointly derived data of a chosen effective model. A freely selected white-noise strength or damping term would not establish bulk energy origin or a thermal state.

Strohmaier, [*An analytic approach to the stress energy tensor in quantum field theory*, `2508.00487v4`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.00487v4), revised 21 August 2026, gives a rigorous analytic formulation of stress as a connection over metrics. Theorems25–26 use globally hyperbolic spacetimes, smooth **spacetime-compact** metric differences and a fixed pure quasifree Hadamard Klein–Gordon state, obtaining unitary implementation and smoothness on a dense finite-particle domain. Spatially homogeneous FLRW forcing on noncompact space does not automatically satisfy the compact-support premise. This mathematical regularity result does not choose the state, bound its distance from rounded data, validate a numerical trajectory or complete the finite momentum band. Spatial localization or compactification to invoke it would require a separately stated model and comparison.

Costeri–Dappiaggi–Juárez-Aubry, [*The Hadamard parametrix on globally hyperbolic spacetimes with Robin boundary conditions: Fundamental solutions and Hadamard states*, `2609.24465v1`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24465v1), submitted 21 September 2026, supplies a boundary-sensitive admissibility framework. Its Robin-Hadamard singular structure includes reflected null rays. Theorem4.35 and Proposition5.8 retain boundary convexity/no glancing, appropriate reflected-branch geometry, local finite reflection counts, admissible reflected sums and suitable auxiliary ground-state hypotheses. These are not consequences of normalization or node agreement. A timelike boundary is different from a second timelike extra dimension. A moving Israel junction must first be shown to satisfy the required operator, boundary and geometry assumptions. The theorem supplies no selected physical vacuum or interval bound on HDBLAST's actual state.

## Current observational method, without importing a dimensional detection

Chen–Zhang, [*Searching for Extra Dimensions with Gravitational Waves: Dark-Siren Constraints from GWTC-4*, `2606.14549v1`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14549v1), submitted 12 June 2026, was already a snippet citation in September. Sec.II now confirms the phenomenological amplitude law Eq.(2.16), involving `D`, crossover scale `Rc` and smoothing index `n`; the redshift factor multiplying `Rc` is part of its convention. The analysis fixes `n=1` and a background matter-density parameter, uses population/selection modeling and incomplete galaxy-catalog information, and studies crossover-scale and Hubble-prior dependence. The source reports consistency with GR, no dimensional-leakage evidence, and weak sensitivity when `Rc` exceeds observed source distances. Its numerical analysis was not rerun here.

An amplitude-damping fit is not a Big Bang-origin test. HDBLAST would need to derive the relevant propagation law and its source/distance validity range from its own action before this family of likelihoods becomes applicable. A large extra-dimensional crossover scale can be observationally indistinguishable from GR in the accessible distance range; a fit alone cannot identify dimensional origin.

## The specific next falsifiable higher-dimensional question

Write the current linear experiment schematically as `y = R[J, state]`. If a higher-dimensional model induces `J_bulk` and a free 4D source may be chosen as `J_4D=J_bulk`, this experiment predicts the same `y` in both descriptions. This is an identifiability statement about an unconstrained effective source, not a new physical theorem. Better arithmetic and a passing Ward identity cannot remove that degeneracy.

A useful physical continuation therefore needs a restricted bulk model:

1. Declare the bulk/brane action, dimension/signature, source trajectory, junction/boundary conditions and incoming bulk/brane state. Derive the effective forcing, its normalization and the energy/flux ledger from these declarations.
2. Derive at least one additional observable or functional restriction from the **same** parameters: for example a KK spectral density controlling a response kernel and fluctuation correlations, a scalar/tensor spectral relation, a restricted leakage-distance law, or an independently correlated channel. Identify its domain, approximation errors and competing explanations before fitting.
3. Solve the brane/bulk matching, excitation abundance, backreaction and thermalization problem appropriate to that model. A prescribed pulse, conserved projected stress or coherent excitation does not fill these steps.
4. Compare the restricted predictions with declared 4D rivals, state freedom, nuisance parameters and a predeclared likelihood/holdout. An arbitrary 4D EFT can still reproduce many bulk-model predictions; success would support a restricted model relative to its tested rivals, not uniquely establish dimensional origin.

The current numerical state comparison is a prerequisite for the selected quantum experiment. It is a separate claim from completing this physical model test. Higher-dimensional origin and Big Bang causation remain **NOT_ESTABLISHED**; external novelty remains **NOT_ASSESSED**.
