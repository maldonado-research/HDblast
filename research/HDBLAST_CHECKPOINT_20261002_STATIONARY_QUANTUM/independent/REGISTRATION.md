# Prospective independent stationary review registration

This protocol is frozen before any new root or quantum source evaluation. Execute only after the root coordinator confirms that the complete experiment registration and source hashes have been publicly frozen. The primary family is a mathematical benchmark at delta=.001, c=.5975949350280132, N=1, b in {-1,0,+1}, gamma=kappa5^2/L^3 in {0,.01,1,100,1e4,1e6}. High-amplitude cases carry no claim of controlled physical EFT. Gamma=0 is the classical control.

The independent root subset is b in {-1,+1}, gamma in {0,100,1e6}, six parameter points at each of two fixed integration resolutions (12 runs total). Each slope/resolution starts at the inherited classical shooting coordinates and continues only through its independent increasing-gamma subset. Producer roots are not initial guesses. Freeze eta_ref=-0.00008405268308670538, z_ref=0.00005924014794328956, r_ref=0.00011848029588657912 from FIXED_MODEL.json. Evaluate f=1+b*(eta_b-eta_ref)/2, require f>0, and use x=r_ref*f^2 directly in shifted eta; phi_ref=1+eta_ref is symbolic and is not used for subtracting nearly equal doubles. Never redefine references during root search. Retain x_phi=b*r_ref*f and x_phiphi=b^2*r_ref/2. This is the original generic quadratic interaction: for b=±1, m0=0, Ghat=sqrt(r_ref)/2, phi_chi=phi_ref-2/b; for b=0, m0^2=r_ref and Ghat=0. The global zero of the b=±1 mass lies outside the registered positive-factor domain and is excluded. The root registration is passed as a file and verified against its frozen SHA256 before any evaluation.

The separate acceleration-system method and direct metric/resolvent source derivation are in DESIGN.md. The code uses scipy DOP853 with the fixed tuples (rtol,y0,max_step)=(1e-11,1e-3,.1),(3e-14,1e-4,.05), component absolute tolerances [1e-14,1e-14,1e-100,1e-100]. scipy root(method=hybr) uses xtol=5e-11,maxfev=120,eps=1e-8 on residuals divided by delta. All attempted objective evaluations and all failures are retained. Root success flags and verified residual gates are both reported; optimizer flags alone do not establish a root.

Acceptance: |E_i|/delta<=2e-12 for both junctions; max|P²-1-R²(w²/12-U/6)|/max(1,P²)<=2e-10 along the integrated interior; independent-resolution and producer-comparison agreements <=2e-9 absolute in phi, <=2e-8 relative in positive H², and <=2e-7 absolute in each shooting coordinate. These are empirical numerical gates, not rigorous global existence/error certificates. The Hamiltonian constraint is independently monitored, not algebraically imposed by the acceleration integrator.

The proper-time endpoint subset is the four nonzero b=±1,gamma={100,1e6} roots at the finer independent resolution. The frozen legacy independent source code (proper_time_source.py) imports no producer. It derives rho from the metric integrand directly and uses distinct scalar and stress integrals. Two fixed numerical settings are (dps,join,upper,UVorder)=(40,.01,60,10),(55,.005,80,12) in units of the constant r. Normalize density discrepancies by z² and current discrepancies by z. Require absolute tolerance 1e-9 plus relative tolerance 1e-7 against the refined proper-time value, both for refinement and cross-method agreement. Bounded spectral and IR tails are retained; UV truncation, quadrature and arithmetic are not claimed as interval-certified. The trace equation is checked after both sources have been calculated and explicitly gated at each setting: divide its absolute residual by z^2 and use 1e-9+1e-7*max(abs(4rho/z^2),abs(xQ/z^2)). For each W,rho,Q, explicitly gate the sum of its reported analytic spectral and IR tail bounds, normalized by z^2,z^2,z respectively, against 1e-9+1e-7*abs(the corresponding normalized source). This bounds only those two error contributions, not UV truncation or quadrature. All these gates enter aggregate status; none is merely stored.

Negative controls: reverse J; omit J; assign p=+rho in the temporal junction; vary r with x in the scalar variation; replace rho by W; and reverse the curvature term in the metric variation. The first four are endpoint defects at the independently calculated root, assessed against the normalized residual floor. At gamma=0 controls that vanish identically are reported not applicable. W/rho and metric-sign source controls are assessed in the proper-time normalized source units against the source comparison tolerance. Four source derivatives rho_z,rho_phi,J_z,J_phi and common-action pairing rho_x-Q/2+zQ_z/4 are independently computed and retained. No current-free source is substituted for b=±1.

Failures remain recorded. No post-result changes to the grid, action, reference, sign, matching, integration resolution or acceptance gate are allowed. Corrections to implementation errors require retained failed sources/logs and explicit mechanical provenance. Static roots do not establish time evolution, dynamical stability, radiation production, thermalization, or a measured physical HDBLAST parameter choice.
