## Most useful recent neighbor

**Ulf Danielsson and Suvendu Giri, “Weak gravity at micron scales from dark bubble cosmology and its cosmological consequences.”** First submitted 26 November 2025; full text read as **arXiv:2511.21362v2, revised 22 June 2026**. Journal metadata: *Physical Review D* **113** (2026) 126010, [DOI 10.1103/819l-m95m](https://doi.org/10.1103/819l-m95m). [Versioned full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.21362v2); [canonical metadata/history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.21362).

- Verified passages: §II, equations (1)–(5), introduce two AdS–Schwarzschild regions and a tensionful moving shell whose proper-time induced metric is closed FLRW. §IV.1, equations (70)–(73), derives homogeneous radiation cosmology with pure AdS interior and AdS–Schwarzschild exterior. §VI identifies the role of non-normalizable modes and mixed conditions at asymptotic AdS infinity.
- Material limitation, directly acknowledged near the end of §V: satisfying the junction conditions does not establish dynamical stability; fluctuations of the coupled bulk–brane system are left to future work.
- Applicability (review inference): useful as a recent physically neighboring example with an explicit proper-time shell and testable predictions. Its junction/Friedmann calculation gives no finite-Sobolev, coupled scalar–metric evolution bound for the retained HDblast system. Its asymptotic AdS conditions, matter content and cosmological background differ. The paper's proposed micron-scale and curvature signatures are predictions of that model, not evidence for HDblast.
- Actionable comparison: retain a clear separation between a consistent shell background, coupled nonlinear evolution, and observational viability. This recent paper itself keeps the second step open; it cannot replace the missing complete lower maps, first-wave quadratic forcing, commutators, normal recovery, or actual-family amplitude bootstrap.

## Model-specific viability caution

**Ivano Basile, Alessandro Borys and Joaquin Masias, “Dynamical dark energy in 0’B braneworlds.”** Published **14 September 2025**, *European Physical Journal C* **85** (2025). The published version of record was read. [Full text and DOI 10.1140/epjc/s10052-025-14706-9](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjc/s10052-025-14706-9).

- Verified passages: §§2–4 construct a ten-dimensional non-supersymmetric string/D3-brane realization of dark-bubble cosmology. §4.3 and §4.3.1, equations (4.20)–(4.30), compare its increasing Hubble rate with homogeneous four-dimensional two-derivative scalar descriptions and obtain a scalar-potential obstruction. §5.2 evaluates the primordial spectral tilt; §5.2.1 derives late-time equation-of-state and string-scale constraints. §6 concludes that this particular 0’B realization fails its early- and late-time cosmology checks.
- Applicability (review inference): a helpful example of an apparently attractive higher-dimensional bubble construction meeting model-specific phenomenological obstacles. Its no-go concerns an effective four-dimensional scalar realization of that super-exponential expansion. It neither refutes nor validates the specified five-dimensional Einstein–scalar shell, and does not supply its boundary PDE estimates.
- Actionable comparison: after mathematical closure, HDblast will need its own map to measured cosmological observables and a consistency analysis of matter/exit/reheating assumptions. Importing a conclusion from a different dark-bubble construction would be unjustified.

## Nearby scalar–brane stability technique with different action

**José Euclides G. Silva, Leandro A. Lessa and Roberto V. Maluf, “Braneworlds in Einstein-Scalar-Gauss–Bonnet gravity.”** Published **27 February 2026**, *European Physical Journal C* **86**, article 199 (2026). The published version of record was read. [Full text and DOI 10.1140/epjc/s10052-026-15438-0](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjc/s10052-026-15438-0).

- Verified passages: §2, equation (1), adds the nonminimal scalar-dependent term `αχ(φ)𝒢` to a five-dimensional Einstein–scalar action. §§3–4 study a static warped thick brane with asymptotically AdS geometry, rather than a finite thin shell. §5 explicitly restricts the stability calculation to tensor modes. §5.1, equations (53)–(60), uses a Kaluza–Klein separation and positivity requirements on the effective propagation coefficients; §5.2 studies massless/massive tensor modes.
- Applicability (review inference): illustrates why positivity/coercivity conditions must be checked for the actual coefficients. Its result is first-order tensor-sector stability under stated conditions; it is not coupled nonlinear Einstein–scalar stability. Different higher-curvature action, thick-brane geometry, perturbation sector and boundary structure prevent direct theorem transfer to HDblast.
- Actionable comparison: useful only as a contrast in a related-work section. The retained model already needs its own actual-family metric-patch and energy-coercivity proof; a KK tensor spectrum cannot supply that proof.
