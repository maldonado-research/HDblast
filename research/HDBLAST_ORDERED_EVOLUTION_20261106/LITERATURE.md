## Concrete use in the next proof

1. **Preserve the ordered operator interface.** Evaluate the full fixed-bundle maps L_t, L_w and L_0 from the exact transformed equation, including reference/frame/ordering terms. The current literature supplies no replacement numeric value for these maps.
2. **Keep the full second variation.** Bound both D²F(0)[(V1,DV1),(V1,DV1)] and −2 P_V(0)[V1]V1. The latter contains V1 D²V1. Pointwise coefficient analyticity or first-order energy alone does not close the required source norm. This requirement comes from the retained equation, not from a claim that a new paper proved it.
3. **Use the homogeneous straightened rows honestly.** Commute with boundary-preserving tangential/time derivations, then recover normal derivatives from the actual equation. Do not assume normal differentiation preserves homogeneous rows. The external boundary papers reinforce the need to account for flux, boundary input regularity and corners; they do not evaluate these model-specific constants.
4. **Maintain a separate actual-family bootstrap.** A neighborhood in coefficient/state space and a large initial jet ceiling do not prove that an evolved exact family stays in the metric patch. Establish this before any amplitude radius, common observer-clock domain, area denominator or actual-area quadrupole remainder.
5. **Treat the new boundary formulation as a research alternative, not a proof shortcut.** Stevens–Valiente Kroon offers a structural comparison for constraint propagation. Replacing the retained formulation would cost a fresh analysis and would not advance the immediate quantitative interface automatically.
