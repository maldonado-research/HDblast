# Prospective publication record

The complete new experiment and implementations are frozen in a public
commit before any new response or mode run. FULL_REGISTRATION.json pins
the payload; its hash is supplied to the primary and validator. The
independent producer receives its own publicly frozen manifest hash.
Actual remote verification is retained in outputs/PUBLIC_FREEZE_EVIDENCE.json
after publication. No outcome selects the sources, grids, gates or budgets.
Earlier causal outputs remain inherited context, not new stress execution.
Pure symbolic development failures are retained explicitly.
