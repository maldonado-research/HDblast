# Claims and next steps

The registered experiment calibrates linear homogeneous scalar-to-minimal-
stress/current response at the fixed reference `H=1,r=2,xi=0`, with fixed
incoming BD state and prescribed geometry. Two independent implementations
pass the original frozen comparisons. Raw mode operators, complete W2/W4
subtraction, finite-cutoff trace contacts, separate Ward integration and
constructive omitted-band bounds make this a reviewable component result.

The 20 controls comprise five alternate operator/subtraction evaluations
on saved modes, ten algebraic sign/contact/normalization diagnostics, and
five synthetic state/reference/format/Wronskian guard checks. They are not
20 additional physical simulations or alternate-state evolutions. Replacing
the minimal operator with an improved operator on the same modes does not
simulate a fully consistent different-curvature-coupling theory. Finite-cutoff
reference-variance sensitivity is diagnosed algebraically; it need not exceed
every main numerical gate.

The after-pulse negative relative density describes linear coherence and
vacuum polarization. It does not establish negative total energy, positive
particle yield, radiation temperature, dissipation or thermalization. A
positive second-order canonical energy is a distinct observable. Its isolated
occupation contribution cannot generally replace the full second-order
minimal stress: infrared coherence and background terms matter.

The next bounded task should publicly freeze an independent spectral,
Bogoliubov-mode and time-domain work calibration of that canonical energy,
with explicit infrared/ultraviolet error bounds and subtraction of the
known reference/background drift from the matched work. It will remain a
fixed-geometry component calculation. Actual shifted-root propagators,
metric-scalar and metric-metric retarded kernels, bulk/shell matching,
boundary conditions and quantum initial data are still needed before
coupled evolution or quantum stability. No new cosmological observation,
extra-dimensional evidence or hot-Big-Bang mechanism is claimed.
