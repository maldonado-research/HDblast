## Concrete implementation route

1. Isolate the old arithmetic and verified-inclusion design into a new
   reviewed module. Keep exact input parameters, explicit interval rejection
   at zero denominators, cellwise inclusions and saved full-cell tubes.
   Do not reuse the old `step()` logic blindly: components with index `>=4`
   receive a special one-way Riccati contraction that is unsuitable for a
   general augmented background or coupled boundary state.
2. Propagate the actual registered background in inward `z`, starting from
   independently justified shell enclosures. In addition to
   `(rho,eta,p,a)`, carry `h=U_eta/p` using the exact identity

   \[
   h_z=-\rho[U_{\eta\eta}-h^2+4ah].
   \]

   The old core uses the corresponding outward-coordinate auxiliary
   quantity to reduce interval dependency. This avoids dividing wide
   independently propagated field boxes at every later cell. Initialize
   `h` with a valid quotient enclosure and verify its defining identity;
   it supplies no new freedom or background solution.
3. Use the new background tube to evaluate the current algebraic formulas
   for `C`, `W`, `mu`, boundary `beta`, and physical normalization. Construct
   the characteristic bulk trial solution and positive-inertia boundary ODE
   together, including the returned reflected wave.
4. Export the actual continuous trial solution and its bulk, boundary,
   initial and coefficient defects. Integrate polynomial products with
   outward bounds; transport inherited errors cell to cell. Apply the
   current physical derivative-trace estimator to
   `w_z-(C_b/2)w`, including errors in that output map. A small grid residual
   or agreement under refinement alone does not certify the continuous
   physical output.
