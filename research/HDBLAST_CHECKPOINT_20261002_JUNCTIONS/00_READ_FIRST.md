# From the prescribed quantum-source control to the actual shell

This checkpoint derives and checks the boundary equations needed to connect the
common-action quantum source to HDBLAST's inherited five-dimensional model.
It also develops a stationary de Sitter initialization bridge using the archived
regular-cone branch. It contains algebra and reanalysis of existing data, not a
new coupled cosmological simulation or a computed de Sitter quantum expectation.

The main result is an explicit pairing of energy density, pressure and scalar
current with all three junction conditions. A lapse-retaining action variation
fixes the pressure independently of conservation. The independent review shows
why conservation alone misses a local action counted twice and a pressure error
at an instant with zero expansion. The current particle-source interface needs
additional geometry and an explicit treatment of higher derivatives before it
can accept the common quantum source.

Read these files in order:

1. `ACTION_AND_JUNCTION_SPECIFICATION.md`: inherited action, conventions,
   local variations, junctions, mode clock and signed energy ledgers.
2. `review/INDEPENDENT_ACTION_AND_JUNCTION_REVIEW.md`: independent variation,
   adverse controls and limits of the existing solver interface.
3. `static_bridge/STATIC_DESITTER_SOURCE_BRIDGE.md`: conditional stationary
   response and the remaining quantum-source calculation.
4. `PROSPECTIVE_WEAK_SOURCE_PROTOCOL.md`: requirements for a future registered
   physical experiment. It is a design, not a completed registration.
5. `REPRODUCE.md`: run the checks in a fresh output directory, retaining the
   frozen source and historical evidence.

The earlier original smooth-FRW matrix remains FAIL because its curved pressure
did not pass the K192 cutoff gate. The separately registered K384 follow-up
remains PASS under all thirteen gate groups. Neither finite-cutoff result proves
a continuum limit, a coupled shell solution, thermalization or a hot Big Bang.

These calculations and the internal independent review used AI assistance,
including GPT-6.1 Sol with Ultra reasoning for this round. They are not external
peer review and do not establish historical novelty. The highest-priority next
calculation is a jointly renormalized massive de Sitter stress and variance in
the same fixed-reference convention, followed by constrained initialization.
No new DOI is assigned to this checkpoint.
