Suggested title: **HDBLAST: cohort energy bounds, cancellation-resistant radiation diagnostics, and exact particle-mode controls**

Author: Ricardo Maldonado
Date: 1 October 2026
Suggested type: software/data with methods report
Publication status: GitHub checkpoint; no Zenodo deposit created in this session

This reproducibility checkpoint develops an upper energy budget for one Gaussian particle cohort on an expanding history, including both undecayed particles and decay radiation. An exploratory application to a fixed archived tuned-model trajectory gives conditional endpoint radiation budgets well below the target in the two crossing cases that pass the local screen. Two additional formal examples fail the production screen and are retained with explicit warnings.

A post hoc diagnostic measures the linear radiation contribution against a sum of absolute vacuum, Weyl, quadratic-radiation and scalar/source terms. The full saved-trajectory audit finds an earlier accepted sampled interval of approximately 0.31446 e-fold in the finer Y=2, dc=0.01 run. At its later saved plateau the scalar-free upper fraction is approximately 0.76545. The interval is retrospective and sampled; shared restart ancestry limits the independence of resolution comparisons. This diagnostic preserves the original September registered verdicts and avoids interpreting negative-vacuum cancellation as physical radiation dominance.

The checkpoint also supplies a standalone particle-mode integrator tested against an archived exactly soluble positive mass pulse. Free and reflectionless cases, nonzero occupation values, time-window changes, step refinement and Wronskian normalization are included. These are controls on prescribed backgrounds, not a coupled five-dimensional reheating calculation.

The package contains analytical derivations, executable source, curated input excerpts, computed outputs, the eleven saved numeric timeseries needed for the nine-history audit, an internal audit, a dated five-paper targeted literature review and a next-test plan. The selected input files were hash-matched to their executed-run provenance; they are archived data, not new five-dimensional evolution. It does not establish a hot Big Bang mechanism, observational support, stability of a coupled matter evolution, or external mathematical novelty. Internal computational reviews with AI assistance are not external peer review.
