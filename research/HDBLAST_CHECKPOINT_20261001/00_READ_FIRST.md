C1 is an upper budget for a specified cohort on a fixed expanding history. It is not a universal impossibility theorem. Two additional formal rows fail the archived local-production screen and cannot be treated as validated production predictions. Gaussian tails and a peak-mass screen do not certify ultraviolet control.

The new trajectory gate does not include the original B3 stationarity and half-e-fold plateau requirements; it is a separate retrospective diagnostic.

C2 uses linear radiation in its numerator and counts quadratic radiation and absolute scalar, Weyl and vacuum contributions in its denominator. A saved point cannot determine an entire radiation era. The original September B1 **INCONCLUSIVE**, B3 registered **PASS-combined** (where applicable), and B4 **NOT VALIDATED** labels are unchanged.

C3 validates an implementation on prescribed pulses, not particle production in the coupled five-dimensional model. No new full five-dimensional evolution was performed in this round.
