where primes in this paragraph denote proper-time derivatives.

               - (3/2)dH/dtau - (9/4)H²] X_k=0.

With conformal time d eta=d tau/a and u_k=a chi_k,

where a'' now means conformal-time derivatives. X_k=sqrt(a) u_k relates them and preserves canonical Wronskian normalization. Consistency requires the same physical state, not separately guessed vacuum states in the two clocks.
