## Why the actual correction decay is essential

The potential norm with decaying weight is not a same-weight algebra.
Near an endpoint, a radial function `u=w^(-3/4)` has integrable weighted
square `w^(1/2)`, including each finite q-scaled derivative. Its square
has nonintegrable weighted square `w^(-1)`. Compact smooth truncations
approximate u in the dotted potential norm. Therefore ordinary unweighted
Sobolev multiplication alone cannot establish the claimed nonlinear map.

The proof instead estimates the physical images of the correction maps
in the graded E_d norms, retaining the extra decay, every derivative of
the source multiplier w^-2, and the residual bounded factor w in the
products. The [embedding and product proof](WEIGHTED_EMBEDDING_PRODUCTS.md)
evaluates these constants on the complete annulus, every angular mode
and the required tensor slots.

The canonical density form also simplifies the momentum constraint:

```
(M/rho0)_i = -2 gamma_ik nabla0_j tau^jk
             -2 tau^jk nabla0_j gamma_ki
             +tau^jk nabla0_i gamma_jk + omega dphi_i.
```

This expression is bilinear and has a state-independent Hessian. The
fixed rho0, original scalar normalization and nonzero-velocity seed
remain in the calculation. The formula is derived directly from the
registered constraint, rather than imported from another convention.

exponent typo for physical P on p.6. Fixed `Pi_psi=2P dV_h` and
`dV_h*=phi^q dV_h` require the negative exponent; its p.4 formula and
p.5 density argument agree with that deduction. This is a version-specific
reading, not a claimed published erratum. That sentence supplies no sign
or factor to the present derivation. Its exact PDF has an internal
24 September 2024 date, while indexed HTML carried a later internal date;
the primary version record controls the citation.
