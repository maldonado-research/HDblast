# Primary comparison and contribution

Checked 5 November 2026. This is a focused comparison, not an exhaustive search
or a certification of originality. Relevant passages in six versioned primary
papers were inspected. The two recent papers below supply useful context, not
new physical evidence for HDBLAST.

The construction uses established finite propagation, wave energy estimates,
Volterra contraction and characteristic integration. Its contribution is the
explicit application to the registered packet: a directly bounded past
potential, near-equal principal weights before possible past shell contact,
and two compatible past face traces with a negative common corner and positive
midpoints. These are first-order, model-specific results. Broader novelty and
significance require specialist assessment.
