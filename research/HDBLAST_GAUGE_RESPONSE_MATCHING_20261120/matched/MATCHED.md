# Matching the short-wave response during a closed-universe descent

The previous calculation changed the number of photon harmonics while keeping the bare scalar action fixed. Here each enlarged finite system is calibrated to the same slow electromagnetic response at reference cutoff $K_0=48$. This lets us ask how much the photon energy and the rolling field still move when shorter waves participate. The comparison uses the two archived round closed launches, the same positive $I^2(\Theta)F^2$ interaction coefficient, both photon polarizations and a photon vacuum on the initial constant-$I$ plateau. The covariant gauge action carries the usual minus sign, $-I^2 F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}/4$.

## Which system is being held fixed?

Use $x=H_0t$, $a=H_0a_{\rm physical}$, $h=a'/a$, $v=\Theta'$, $u=V/(3M_{\rm Pl}^2H_0^2)$ and $\epsilon=(H_0/M_{\rm Pl})^2$. $M_{\rm Pl}$ is the reduced Planck mass. A prime denotes $d/dx$. Let $g=d\log I/d\Theta$. The original compact smooth profile changes $\log I$ by $\delta$ over the field interval between 20% and 80% of the directed distance to the first minimum. At the endpoints of this interval all its derivatives vanish. The ultraviolet regulator retains both polarizations through fixed three-sphere harmonic $K$. Its proper frequency varies as $H_0K/a$ during the descent.

For a vacuum mode, the slow-response term in its instantaneous occupation is $n_L\simeq (agv)^2/(4L^2)$. Its physical excess in the band added above $K_0$, with both polarizations, is therefore $c\,g^2v^2/(6a^2)$ in our units, where

$$ c=\frac{\epsilon}{2\pi^2}\sum_{L=K_0+1}^K\frac{L^2-1}{L}. $$

The reference system includes the response of its own first 48 shells. To hold that reference fixed we put a term with the opposite coefficient in the reduced scalar action. Define $Z(q,a)=1-cg(q)^2/a^2$ and the scalar Lagrangian proportional to $a^3(Zv^2/6-u)$. The sum and its sign are fixed before following the roll. The calibration is at zero probe frequency. It removes one local contribution. The highest-frequency modes can still be produced as real outgoing photons, and the retarded response at finite frequency still differs between cutoffs.

The factor $a^{-2}$ follows from using a fixed comoving cutoff. The background pressure comes from varying the reduced action with respect to the spatial metric's scale parameter at fixed field velocity and lapse. In particular,

$$\rho_\phi=u+Zv^2/6,\qquad p_\phi=(Z+aZ_a/3)v^2/6-u.$$

The subtracted term itself has $p_{\rm sub}=\rho_{\rm sub}/3$. This makes its stress consistent with the leading photon energy it compensates on the round background. A generally covariant completion valid for other metrics would require additional work.

## Equations and conservation

Write $B=\epsilon/(3\pi^2a^4)$, $w_L=L(L^2-1)$, $m_L=\langle b_L b_L\rangle$ and $n_L=\langle b_L^\dagger b_L\rangle$. The photon excitation density and the scalar source per unit velocity are

$$ r_\gamma=B\sum w_Ln_L,\qquad S=2Bg\sum w_L\operatorname{Re}m_L. $$

With the instantaneous half quantum subtracted, the mode evolution is $m'_L=-2i(L/a)m_L+gv(2n_L+1)$ and $n'_L=2gv\operatorname{Re}m_L$. We launch all modes with $m_L=n_L=0$. Evolution of scalar momentum and curvature is governed by

$$
(a^3Zv)'=a^3[-3u_q+Z_qv^2/2-3S],\qquad
h'=a^{-2}-(Z/2+aZ_a/12)v^2-2r_\gamma .
$$

These equations imply $r'_\gamma+4hr_\gamma=vS$, $\rho'_\phi+3h(\rho_\phi+p_\phi)=-vS$, and preservation of the closed Friedmann constraint $h^2+a^{-2}=\rho_\phi+r_\gamma$. The uniform canonical field has a positive kinetic term while $Z>0$. The default integration stops at the first potential minimum, first maximum radius, or first point with $Z=0.05$. Fractions at that positive reserve are diagnostics inside the active coupling region. They do not give an outgoing photon yield.

The initial frequencies of harmonics 192 and 512 are roughly $1.69M_{\rm Pl}$ and $4.51M_{\rm Pl}$. Those extensions deliberately stress the regulator and provide no independent evidence for new physical photon degrees of freedom at those energies.

## How long did the resolved strong majority last?

On the constant outgoing plateau the matching term and its spatial and field derivatives vanish, $Z=1$ and $g=0$. The photons conserve $a^4r_\gamma$. We used the historical [source-free evolution](../prior/feedback/coast.py) to follow every consecutive resolved strong case, beginning at switch-off and ending at the first maximum radius or first return to the active field interval. It evolves the parent, radiation and closed scale factor independently and checks its first field minimum against the oscillator solution. The duration is expressed as $\Delta N=\log(a_{\rm end}/a_{\rm begin})$ over the part of that interval with $F_\gamma\ge1/2$.

On the positive branch the photons remain above half up to the first maximum radius on each resolved trajectory. The $K=50$ and $51$ outgoing states remain below half throughout their respective off-switch intervals. There is no collision or thermalization mechanism in these mode equations.

## Checks

The runs include $\delta=0.01,0.1,0.25,0.5,1,3$ and both archived parent branches. The calculation independently evolves $v,a,a'$ and the shifted canonical position–momentum quadratures in selected weak, strong and stopped cases. It compares the no-added-band reference with the previous numerical record, checks both integrated energy balances, and checks the purity identity $|m|^2=n(n+1)$. The minimum reported $Z$ is found by polishing locally bracketed minima from dense output. Small positive reserves and tighter independent evolutions check sensitivity at the two strong boundaries.
