hyperboloid R²-T²=r_b² with outward unit normal n_eta. Its exact laws are

    n_eta psi-c Box_Sigma,eta psi+d_e psi=0, c=1/(Omega_b B_b)>0,
    n_eta psi+d_o psi=0.

Here d_e=Omega_b gamma_e+r_b L_b, d_o=Omega_b gamma_o+r_b L_b,
gamma_e=4/rho_b-4/(rho_b²B_b), gamma_o=4/rho_b+p_b²/(3a_b).
Neither shifted d sign is assumed. V_c=3L-2sL_s+sL²+(8/3)s(Phi_s)² is smooth
on the chosen actual background interval. Positive auxiliary boundary inertia
is not the physical gravitational shell kinetic form. Scalar existence does
not by itself prove the reconstructed junctions or gauge claims.

## Precise2026 Robin mapping

The source uses inward n_in and (n_in·nabla+kappa)u=0. Our n_out=-n_in
and n_out psi+d_o psi=0 imply (n_in·nabla-d_o)psi=0, so kappa=-d_o.
The paper permits any real constant kappa. Its ground-state positivity
restrictions belong to a separate question and are not finite-slab existence
hypotheses.

compactness for the Green construction by localization. Infinitesimal

results, not these existence/causal-support propositions. They should not be
imposed solely for our smooth Robin evolution.

use smoothness and finite potential bounds, a trace estimate and added L²
