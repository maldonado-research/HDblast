# Next calculations

The new result closes a finite-slab contact gap for a specified linear family.
It should now be reviewed by a qualified external mathematical physicist,
with the model assumptions, closest boundary-theory literature and the full
junction/current derivation supplied together. This is the next publication
quality gate; internal agent checks do not replace it.

1. **Second-order consistency.** Derive the second-order bulk and shell
   constraint equations for these first-order states. Determine whether
   global constraints restrict which linear solutions integrate to actual
   nearby nonlinear solutions. This is an open calculation, not a claimed
   obstruction or a solved existence theorem.
2. **Physical energy and stability.** Derive a well-defined physical
   Hamiltonian for a specified evolution and gauge/boundary domain. The
   positive auxiliary wave estimate and positive diagonal of K are
   insufficient to infer ghost freedom or nonlinear stability.
3. **Connect the two constructed families.** Establish an actual-background
   domain long enough to follow the preceding near-cone flux pair to the
   shell, or construct overlap data with both verified properties. The new
   collar family does not supply that connection automatically.
4. **Angular and gauge completion.** Obtain uniform angular estimates,
   compatibility domains and gauge/charge control before claiming a complete
   physical phase space or unrestricted initial-boundary wellposedness.
5. **Numerical illustration, after theory review.** Evolve a pinned
   background and smooth collar pair, report convergence and full boundary/
   current residuals, and distinguish solution data from the geometric
   support schematic. The present exact checks do not simulate a waveform.

A cosmological mechanism would additionally require energy transfer,
thermalization, appropriate cosmological evolution and discriminating
observational predictions. The present linear theorem does not establish any
of those steps. No new APS submission is implied by this checkpoint.
