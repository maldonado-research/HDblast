**Title:** HDBLAST: matched quantum stress and scalar source through a smooth mass-zero crossing

**Author:** Ricardo Maldonado. **Date:** 2 October 2026.

We calculate a consistently matched one-loop stress tensor and scalar source for one real minimally coupled field on a smooth prescribed Minkowski background, with M²(t)=4tanh²(t). Exact scattering states define the in-vacuum. A covariant Pauli–Villars prescription fixes potential and curvature matching at positive reference mass squared r=4.

The benchmark isolates a pressure contribution that an energy-exchange test cannot determine on flat spacetime. Omitting the matched curvature term leaves logarithmic regulator dependence in pressure. Including it, eight registered dynamic variants pass exact occupation, normalization, scalar-work and independent pressure-trace controls. A separate physical-mode representation uses positive-reference convergent integrands. Numerical refinement and finite regulator effects are reported separately; residual regulator effects remain several percent of the source near the crossing.

The package contains prospective protocols, code, high-precision analytic checks, full result summaries, selected curves, figures, independent internal reviews and targeted literature notes. An action-level FRW bridge is derived, but no curved-shell or bulk evolution is performed.

This is a controlled quantum-source benchmark, not evidence for a higher-dimensional Big Bang. It does not establish coupled backreaction, particle decay, thermalization, a radiation era or observed extra dimensions. Matching conditions are explicit choices for this benchmark, not cosmological measurements. AI tools assisted derivations, implementation and internal checks; these are not external peer review. No external mathematical novelty is claimed.
