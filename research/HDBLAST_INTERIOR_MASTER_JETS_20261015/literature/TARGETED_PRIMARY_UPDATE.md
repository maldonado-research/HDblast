## New or newly verified primary developments

**An–Anderson III, current revision.** Zhongshan An and Michael T. Anderson, [arXiv:2503.12599v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.12599v3), revised 18 June 2026. The canonical record identifies a gap in the former main linearized existence proof and a weakened theorem. Primary §1, Theorem 1.1 and the discussion after Corollary 1.3 give injectivity and dense range on a restricted dense class; they do not provide a general continuous inverse. The text says general energy estimates fail, including at a flat round timelike cylinder. **Consequence:** do not cite an earlier broad well-posedness claim for conformal-class/mean-curvature boundary data as a shell certificate. This vacuum boundary law also differs from the shell junction law.

**A concrete geometric-boundary obstruction.** Xiaoyi Liu, Harvey S. Reall, Jorge E. Santos and Toby Wiseman, [arXiv:2505.20410v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20410v2), revised 3 July 2025. Primary §§4.2–4.4 construct smooth cavity perturbations with common boundary and corner data, initial data tending to zero and a diverging gauge-invariant curvature observation at positive time. The mechanism uses increasingly fast growth at large angular frequency for conformal/generalized-conformal boundary conditions. **Consequence:** finite-mode convergence is insufficient to prove a uniform estimate if an unrestricted tangential sector is retained. This is not a proof of ill-posedness for the present scalar shell or for a symmetry-restricted reduction; its boundary operator must be examined separately.

**A valid nearby vacuum result, with different data.** An and Anderson, [arXiv:2507.15567v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.15567v3), revised 30 May 2026. Primary Theorem 1.1 establishes a local smooth tame inverse in harmonic gauge for smoothly corner-compatible vacuum data, with prescribed boundary conformal class and a particular volume density. Theorem scope includes higher dimensions. Primary Remark 2.9 checks gauge propagation through the vacuum constraints. **Consequence:** the gauge/constraint/corner architecture is a useful template. The prescribed density, vacuum equations and its energy estimates cannot be substituted for a field-dependent umbilic Einstein–scalar junction.

**Higher regularity of a dynamic boundary is extra work.** Delio Mugnolo and Enzo Vitillaro, [The wave equation with acoustic boundary conditions on non-locally reacting surfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.09219v2), Memoirs AMS 303 (2024), no. 1526; inspected arXiv revision 11 June 2022. Primary Theorem 1.2.3 gives time derivative order i in spatial H^(n−i), 0≤i≤n≤r, under coefficient hypotheses (A0–3), H^n/H^(n−1) data and explicit corner compatibility. Primary §3.3.2 separates classical normal traces from distributional normal derivatives. **Consequence:** include boundary states, differentiated boundary equations and corner jets in the norm. This fixed-domain linear acoustic system's positive boundary coefficients and estimates do not establish the shell's metric–scalar or pointwise-output bounds.

**A recent wave-equation residual estimator.** Zhaonan Dong, Emmanuil H. Georgoulis, Lorenzo Mascotto and Zuodong Wang, [arXiv:2509.08537v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08537v2), revised 23 April 2026. Primary §2 specifies a linear homogeneous-Dirichlet wave problem on a convex polytopic domain; §5 assumes Δu in L¹(time;L²(space)). Theorem 5.3 bounds displacement error uniformly in time in spatial L², using initial-data, source, time, space and mesh-change estimators. Reconstructions supply computable constants. **Consequence:** useful design for a continuous-defect ledger. It does not bound boundary derivative observations, validate floating-point arithmetic, or furnish the actual variable-coefficient shell estimator; proposed extensions are not imported as proved shell results.

## Direct foundational comparisons

**Coupled matter really matters.** Gustav H. Holzegel and Claude M. Warnick, [The Einstein–Klein–Gordon–AdS system for general boundary conditions](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.5332v2), 18 December 2013, revised 28 July 2014. Primary Theorem 4.1 proves a local renormalized weak solution with constraint propagation for 0<κ<2/3 and prescribed smooth β,γ boundary data; Theorem 4.2 requires extra regularity for a classical solution. The four-dimensional system is spherically symmetric, with the boundary at AdS conformal infinity. Primary Remark 4 notes a contraction obstruction at energy regularity for one geometry-dependent boundary defining function. **Consequence:** derive matter/boundary estimates together; the result does not cover a finite five-dimensional shell, arbitrary scalar potential, or the requested derivative observable.

**A broad wave theorem still has a boundary-symbol hypothesis.** Heinz-Otto Kreiss, Oscar Reula, Olivier Sarbach and Jeffrey Winicour, [arXiv:0807.3207v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.3207v1), 21 July 2008. Primary equations (3)–(4), Theorem 1 and Lemma 1 require a strictly outgoing oblique derivative and tangential derivative couplings made arbitrarily small by a field transformation; a strict triangular hierarchy is sufficient. Their constrained Einstein application is vacuum. **Consequence:** the theorem is a hypothesis checklist, not an automatic reflecting-Robin shell theorem. An energy proof or an explicit valid reduction is still required for the shell boundary matrix. The HTML's separate generated title-page date is not treated as a revision date.

Daniel Tataru's [1998 primary trace paper](https://www.numdam.org/item/ASNSP_1998_4_26_1_185_0.pdf) was opened, but equation extraction was incomplete and the screenshot route failed.
