# Focused primary comparison: incoming bulk state, shell exchange and radiation

4 November 2026. Relevant passages were checked in the full texts of five primary papers.
This is a focused comparison, not an exhaustive literature review. None proves the
current five-dimensional, nonspherical, finite-shell past preparation problem.

## 1. A bulk scalar leaves genuinely nonlocal preparation data

K. Maeda and D. Wands, *Dilaton-gravity on the brane*, Phys. Rev. D **62** (2000)
124009, [hep-th/0008188v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0008188v2),
DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.62.124009.

Equations (2.11), (2.15), (2.17)–(2.19) give Codazzi, the scalar jump, Israel
matching and the split `S=-lambda*g+tau`. The discussion after (2.25) identifies
the normal second scalar coefficient `Phi2` as bulk information not determined
locally on the brane; arbitrary choices need not have global solutions. Equation
(2.26) and the unnumbered equation immediately after it give a trace-proportional
matter-exchange law for their conformal coupling. The sign of a conformal source
must be derived with an explicit matter-metric convention, measure and stress
definition, as in the supporting note; no printed coefficient is transferred
without that check. Their normal orientation
and gravitational normalization must also be converted. The result supports specifying a bulk
state/history and matter coupling; it does not supply them.

## 2. Null equations, junctions and an explicit flux law

D. Langlois and M. Rodriguez-Martinez, *Brane cosmology with a bulk scalar field*,
Phys. Rev. D **64** (2001) 123507,
[hep-th/0106245v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0106245v1),
DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.64.123507.

The homogeneous/isotropic ansatz uses null coordinates in equations (5)–(9).
Its metric junctions are (10)–(13); the scalar junction depends on the specified
brane Lagrangian, discussed in (14)–(23). Equations (73)–(75) give the modified
brane continuity law with flux from `T05=phi_dot*phi_prime`. The text after (9)
notes scattering by a nonzero potential prevents the special exactly one-null-
coordinate solutions of the potential-free ansatz. This is not a prohibition on
prescribing incoming perturbation data. Their separated solutions and prescribed
matter couplings do not construct HDBLAST's quadrupole packet or its retarded
second preparation. Their discussion near the end of section IV D also notes
that conformally coupled radiation has vanishing trace/coupling parameter.

## 3. A genuine characteristic plus timelike-boundary existence model

G. H. Holzegel and C. M. Warnick, *The Einstein-Klein-Gordon-AdS system for general
boundary conditions*, [1312.5332v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.5332v2).

Section 3.1, Definition 2, specifies free null radius/scalar data and their
integrability conditions. Constraints (29)–(32) construct the remaining initial
variables rather than setting them arbitrarily to zero. Boundary data include
the Robin condition (39). Theorem 4.1 gives local unique weak solutions for the
specified mass range `0<kappa<2/3` and data; the constraint propagation result is
part of the proof. Theorem 4.2 requires additional regularity for a classical
solution. The discussion after it carefully limits geometric uniqueness for
Robin/inhomogeneous conditions. This is spherical **3+1-dimensional asymptotically
AdS** Einstein–Klein–Gordon with conformal boundary. It is a rigorous template for
what must be specified, not an existence theorem for a five-dimensional Israel
shell, a nonspherical packet, or a global retarded inverse.

## 4. Projected geometry is not a thermal matter sector

T. Shiromizu, K. Maeda and M. Sasaki, *The Einstein Equations on the 3-Brane World*,
Phys. Rev. D **62** (2000) 024012,
[gr-qc/9910076v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9910076v3),
DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.62.024012.

Equations (14)–(17) distinguish brane tension, actual matter stress `tau`, its
quadratic correction and projected Weyl data `E`. Equations (21)–(22) impose
conservation/Bianchi relations in their bulk-vacuum setting. The Weyl term carries
bulk gravitational information and is not freely specified. A radiation-like term
in an induced Friedmann equation is not, by itself, a populated thermal particle
sector. Scalar-bulk exchange changes the conservation law and requires the scalar
junction analysis of the first two sources. No radiation temperature, entropy,
particle production or Big Bang origin follows from a shell area response.

## 5. One explicit interaction/state example, requiring a new model

L. Kofman, A. Linde and A. Starobinsky, *Towards the Theory of Reheating After
Inflation*, Phys. Rev. D **56** (1997) 3258–3295,
[hep-ph/9704452v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9704452v2),
DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.56.3258.

Equation (6) supplies an explicit interaction `-g^2*phi^2*chi^2/2`, among other
terms. Equations (38)–(40) give the mode oscillator/frequency and occupation
number. Equations (42)–(44) specify the Bogoliubov description, initial positive-
frequency state and particle number. These results require a specified pump
history and quantum state and treat backreaction; preheating is distinguished
from subsequent thermalization. The present HDBLAST shell has neither this matter
field nor an established pump/state satisfying those hypotheses. Adding the
interaction changes both the scalar boundary law and the shell stress. A zero
classical chi field with zero classical data remains zero under the homogeneous
chi equation; it is not a calculation of quantum vacuum production.

## Consequence for the next calculation

A candidate retarded prescription must specify an actual past domain, compatible
initial/characteristic geometry and scalar data, boundary/corner conditions and a
physical clock. It must generate the fixed first packet before its quadratic
source can explain any second response. The induced brane equations alone leave
bulk data undetermined. A pure-tension shell exchanges vacuum/tension energy but
contains no independent radiation stress. For a physical reheating proposal,
specify additional fields, interactions and a state, derive the changed exchange
and junction laws, and then compute the resulting preparation and energy budget.
None of those missing inputs is furnished by the five sources or by the current
H1 estimate.
