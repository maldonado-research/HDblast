# Next target: evolution through shell contact

The exact-family theorem settles a sufficiently short protected interval.
It leaves a concrete coupled boundary problem when the perturbation reaches
the shell. The preceding formal and material supplements supply starting
equations, rather than a completed contact evolution.

1. Compute the full quadratic geometric and scalar bulk source for the
   concrete commuting state `A = B = diag(1,−1,0,0)`. In particular,
   calculate the total tensor source: the known matter-only transverse
   trace-free projection does not establish that the total source vanishes.
2. Convert the material outward-Gaussian shell equations to the chosen
   generalized harmonic evolution gauge, including displacement, normal
   normalization and pullback terms. Keep the two-copy junction equations
   until any exact copy symmetry has been proved.
3. Derive compatible constraint and gauge boundary conditions together with
   the physical Israel/scalar conditions. Prove the needed energy estimates
   and boundary well-posedness for this five-dimensional scalar-coupled model;
   vacuum umbilic or four-dimensional boundary results alone do not supply it.
4. Construct and independently verify the full second-order contact solution
   before claiming nonlinear continuation across contact. Numerical evolution
   can check the construction but does not replace the analytic compatibility
   and well-posedness arguments.

The registered two-copy quadratic scalar junction source is `−4 ψ₁²`;
the exact even-master reflection class has a necessary rescaled tensor
Neumann condition. Those statements, their assumptions and their verifier
are in the [material junction supplement](../HDBLAST_MATERIAL_JUNCTIONS_20261014/README.md).
