# Questions for a specialist reviewing this draft

The aim is to assess whether the detailed physical-shell application is correct and useful enough for a focused mathematical-relativity paper. These questions are a review brief, not a claim that a specialist has been contacted or has endorsed the work.

1. Does the exact ADM/mixed-Israel identity correctly account for the cut, matching fields and boost angles for arbitrary permitted variations? Are the matching-chart and regularity assumptions sufficient for the stated second differentiation?
2. Does the first-derivative cancellation cover every matching second-order correction, without relying on support of that correction? Does the explicit master reconstruction produce a genuine solution of all linearized equations on the specified precontact slab?
3. Does the explicit background with its fixed potential and affine tension correctly realize all hypotheses? Is the nonzero radial and angular pairing normalized consistently with the action and two-copy Hessian convention?
4. In view of Harlow–Wu, De Vuyst et al., Khavkine, and Hájíček–Kijowski, what useful content does this interface calculation add? Is it a publishable worked application, a shorter technical note, or material better retained as supporting research documentation?
5. Does the finite commuting-matrix material help the gravitational argument enough to justify its length? The paper proves its elementary statements directly and makes no priority or PDE sufficiency claim for them.

The source ledger identifies complete-primary gaps, particularly several classical original papers and the shell-Hamiltonian erratum. Resolve any gap that bears on a claimed extension before making a stronger novelty claim. The current draft avoids importing their unverified sufficiency theorems or corrected component formulas.

The further research problem remains the full second-order constraint and interface system for a charge-zero state. A regular finite matrix seed removes a finite degeneracy but does not solve that PDE problem. Additional arithmetic alone does not resolve the present publication-significance question.
