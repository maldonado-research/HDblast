Planning-only shape feasibility at ell = 10^-6
============================================

Scope: this fresh rational calculation checks consequences of a proposed
uniform error majorant. It does not produce a new waveform, run an archived
producer, or certify the PDE majorant. Nothing outside this planning directory
was written or executed for this calculation.

Input from parent calculation:

  |H(x)-Hhat(x)| <= eta_exact globally,
  eta_exact = 2007523995553919/1999981973040500000
            < eta = 251/250000 = 0.001004,
  Hhat(x) = Q'(x) + ell exp(-ell x)Q(x),
  ell = 1/1000000,
  Q(x) = exp(4-1/((x-1)(2-x))) on (1,2), zero elsewhere.

Here H must be continuous. The exact rational rounding of eta is checked;
the source majorant remains an assumption. The parent's separate tail bound
is not needed for the following global-error consequences.

Analytic geometry used
----------------------

For z=2x-3, d=1-z^2,

  Q=exp(-4z^2/d),
  Q'=-16z Q/d^2,
  Q''=-32Q(1-6z^2-3z^4)/d^4.

With a=sqrt(2/sqrt(3)-1), Q' has its unique positive global maximum
at x_L=(3-a)/2 and unique negative global minimum at x_R=(3+a)/2.
Its positive maximum and negative minimum are respectively M and -M, where

  M=6(2+sqrt(3)) a exp(1-sqrt(3)),
  4.235640422134888218 < M < 4.235640422134888219.

The code certifies x_L in (1.29,1.32) and x_R in (1.68,1.71).
The sign of Q'' proves monotonicity on either side of these peaks.

Sign and amplitude gates
------------------------

Since 0<=Q<=1 and its support is positive,

  Q'-eta <= H <= Q'+ell+eta.

The rational sample magnitude is

  Q'(5/4)=-Q'(7/4)=(128/9)exp(-4/3)>3.7.

Thus eta+ell<3.7 suffices for the two opposite sample signs. With the
specified eta, outward rational enclosures are

  H(5/4) in [3.747933075423669618, 3.749942075423669619],
  H(7/4) in [-3.749941075423669619, -3.747932075423669618].

There is consequently at least one actual zero between 5/4 and 7/4.
The global peak heights satisfy

  max H in [4.234636422134888218, 4.236645422134888219],
  min H in [-4.236644422134888219, -4.234635422134888218].

The first interval also bounds ||H||_infinity. Convenient coarse gates are
4.2346<max H<4.2367 and -4.2367<min H<-4.2346.

Conservative peak-window gate
------------------------------

The exact rational endpoint expressions are

  Q'(1.29) = (42000000/4239481) exp(-1764/2059),
  Q'(1.32) = (140625/18496) exp(-81/136).

Fresh rational evaluation gives

  M-Q'(1.29) in [0.029681983025445975, 0.029681983025445976],
  M-Q'(1.32) in [0.044578913596622392, 0.044578913596622393].

Both exceed 1/50. Let G be the smaller endpoint gap. Outside
I_L=(1.29,1.32), monotonicity gives Q'<=M-G, and therefore

  H(outside I_L) <= M-G+ell+eta < M-eta <= H(x_L)

provided G>2eta+ell. A particularly simple sufficient gate is

  1/50 > 2eta+ell,
  equivalently eta < 19999/2000000 = 0.0099995.

The proposed rounded error passes because

  2eta+ell = 2009/1000000 = 0.002009 < 1/50.

Every global positive maximizer therefore lies in (1.29,1.32).
By reflection symmetry of Q', every global negative minimizer lies in
(1.68,1.71). Endpoints are excluded. Continuity and strict exclusion
outside these compact closures also prove the global extrema exist.

The absolute global maximum lies in the union of these two windows.
A uniform C^0 error bound does not determine which lobe attains that
absolute maximum, or establish uniqueness of actual extrema or zeros.
No such uniqueness or lobe selection is claimed here.

Reproducibility
---------------

Run shape_gate.py in this directory. It uses only standard-library exact
Fraction arithmetic, integer square-root enclosures, and alternating Taylor
brackets S49(t)<=exp(-t)<=S48(t) for 0<=t<=2. All checks use explicit
exceptions, so python -O preserves them. Three negative controls reject
a missing derivative factor 16, a reversed derivative sign, and an
excessive error accepted for the proposed windows.

Normal and python -O runs produced byte-identical JSON in shape_gate.json
and shape_gate_optimized.json. SHA256:
