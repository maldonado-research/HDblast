# HDBLAST: current research and publication status

Updated **2 October 2026, Pacific time**. Frozen archive names and registrations retain their UTC dates, including 3 October. This page distinguishes the latest completed research on GitHub from the versions actually published on Zenodo.

HDBLAST asks whether an event in a higher-dimensional space could have supplied the hot initial state of our universe. That cause remains **unestablished**. The work has no external peer review or observational confirmation. Internal independent implementations and AI-assisted reviews are documented computational checks.

## Latest completed result

Two independently implemented calculations identify a sampling error in the retained conservation ledger during the registered active-source interval. All twelve consistency cases pass, and eight satisfy the fixed attribution threshold. A fresh standalone archive replay reproduces every registered scientific value and stable output frame exactly.

This is a numerical diagnosis on saved data and fixed geometry. Small signed continuity residuals involve cancellation; they are **not certified total-error bounds**. Earlier metric calibration and refinement remain **FAIL**. Continuum accuracy, the inherited quantum state, full metric response, coupled five-dimensional evolution and a sustained hot radiation era remain unresolved.

## Next scientific step

The [next-study proposal](../research/HDBLAST_ACTIVE_SOURCE_LEDGER_PACKAGE_20261002/post_result_design_review/NEXT_REGISTERED_STUDY.md) targets certified oscillatory integration and convergence checks before another unchanged metric calibration.
