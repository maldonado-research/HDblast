# HDBLAST: current research and publication status

Updated **3 October 2026, Pacific time**. Frozen archive names and registrations retain their UTC dates, including 3 October. This page distinguishes the latest completed research on GitHub from the versions actually published on Zenodo.

HDBLAST asks whether an event in a higher-dimensional space could have supplied the hot initial state of our universe. That cause remains **unestablished**. The work has no external peer review or observational confirmation. Internal independent implementations and AI-assisted reviews are documented computational checks.

## Latest completed result: conditional source-operator certificate

Two independently implemented enclosure methods pass the separately registered integration prerequisite for two declared smooth source functions. Each route returns all **1,170 moment rows and 130 source-primitive rows**, covering 64 panels and nine exact rational momenta on the fixed interval [−4.5,−3.5]. The largest conservative whole-interval enclosure radius across both implementations is below **1.373 × 10⁻²⁸**, within the unchanged **10⁻²⁶** gate. The archived analytic bounds apply to all 64 panels and real momentum k ∈ [0,256]; computed complete arithmetic enclosure widths are certified at the nine registered probes. A fresh standalone archive replay reproduces both numerical payloads and scientific frames exactly, preserving the frozen files byte for byte.

This certificate is conditional on the archived analytic proofs, source and geometry identities, arithmetic contracts and verified public-freeze chronology. It concerns source/operator integrals on prescribed geometry. The separate J_Lg channel encloses ∫ Lg; action-derived pressure/contact work requires the later full certificate. It does not certify the inherited quantum state, momentum quadrature, full action-derived pressure/contact conservation integral or coupled gravity. The full twelve-case certificate and inherited 2 × 10⁻⁸ integral gate remain **UNRESOLVED**. Earlier metric endpoint and refinement checks remain **FAIL**. A higher-dimensional cause of the Big Bang and a hot radiation era remain **NOT_ESTABLISHED**; external mathematical novelty is **NOT_ASSESSED**. Internal independent review is not external peer review or proof-assistant formalization.

Two independently implemented calculations identify a sampling error in the retained conservation ledger during the registered active-source interval. All twelve consistency cases pass, and eight satisfy the fixed attribution threshold. A fresh standalone archive replay reproduces every registered scientific value and stable output frame exactly.

This is a numerical diagnosis on saved data and fixed geometry. Small signed continuity residuals involve cancellation; they are **not certified total-error bounds**. Earlier metric calibration and refinement remain **FAIL**. Continuum accuracy, the inherited quantum state, full metric response, coupled five-dimensional evolution and a sustained hot radiation era remain unresolved.
