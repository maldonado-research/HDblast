## What changes the immediate research priorities

The most useful finding is that **the absence of an oscillating scalar does not exclude reheating after a matter sector is added**. Instant preheating supplies a concrete, established mechanism for testing a single passage through a particle-production threshold. That does not rescue the existing matter-free calculation: adding the fields and coupling changes the model, and their energy extraction must backreact on the scalar junction condition and geometry.

The immediate numerical priority remains the registered detuning and the coordinate breakdown. Neither can be resolved by borrowing a reheating formula. Conversely, no apparent expansion-rate plateau, radiation-like term, or large curvature alone establishes a visible thermal bath.

## Directly applicable established methods

### 1. Nonlinear brane evolution and invariant diagnostics

Martin, Felder, Frolov, Peloso and Kofman, [Braneworld dynamics with the BraneCode](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0309001), submitted **1 September 2003**, *Physical Review D* **69**, 084017 (2004). Primary PDF reviewed, especially sections 2, 4–6 and appendix A.

This is close prior art: a self-consistent five-dimensional Einstein–scalar evolution with two orbifold branes. It finds unstable de Sitter configurations relaxing to lower-curvature configurations or approaching collisions with Kasner-type geometry. Its brane observables use proper time and the induced expansion rate. Appendix A explicitly describes residual conformal-coordinate freedom. Sections 4–5 also distinguish Weyl curvature generated by the bulk from local matter.

**Use:** benchmark geometric diagnostics and coordinate changes against exact solutions. **Limit:** the two-brane topology and boundary data differ from the present single shell plus regular cone. Its outcomes do not prove the HDBLAST endpoint or uniqueness of the two observed branches.

Related linear prior art is Frolov and Kofman, [Can Inflating Braneworlds be Stabilized?](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0309002), **1 September 2003**, PRD **69**, 044021: de Sitter curvature can destabilize a radion in a scalar-stabilized two-brane system. It is useful context, not a derivation of the project’s particular tachyon eigenvalue.

### 2. A consistent bulk/brane matter extension

Maeda and Wands, [Dilaton-gravity on the brane](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0008188), submitted **24 August 2000**, revised **11 October 2000**, PRD **62**, 124009. The primary abstract confirms the derivation of induced metric and scalar equations with arbitrary bulk potential and scalar-dependent brane tension, including bulk scalar/gravitational terms and coupling to brane matter.

**Use:** derive the modified junction and conservation equations from one action before adding a radiation source. A four-dimensional effective equation does not independently close the bulk Weyl contribution. Do not replace the full dynamical five-dimensional calculation with an unexplained friction term and describe the result as the unchanged model.

### 3. Nonoscillatory production is a viable test mechanism, not a completed solution

Felder, Kofman and Linde, [Instant Preheating](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9812289), submitted **9 December 1998**, revised **12 December 1998**, PRD **59**, 123523. Primary PDF equations (3), (4), (10) and section III checked. A rapid crossing of a scalar-dependent mass minimum creates particles; their mass can subsequently increase and their decay can transfer energy to lighter fields. Repeated oscillations are unnecessary.

For a canonically normalized four-dimensional driver \(\varphi\), a local linear crossing and \(m_\chi^2=m_0^2+g^2(\varphi-\varphi_*)^2\), the established approximation is

\[
n_k=\exp[-\pi(k_{\rm phys}^2+m_0^2)/q],\qquad
n_\chi=\frac{q^{3/2}}{8\pi^3}e^{-\pi m_0^2/q},\qquad
q=g|\dot\varphi_*|.
\]

**Use:** compute a prospective production threshold from the actual shell history. **Limit:** this flat, short-event approximation requires slow geometric change during production and controlled backreaction. Neither a dimensionless simulation scalar nor its coordinate-time derivative may be inserted without the physical normalization. A produced population is not automatically thermalized or radiation dominated.

The related [Inflation and Preheating in NO models](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9903350), **16 March 1999**, explicitly treats nonoscillating potentials. It strengthens the distinction between “no oscillation” and “no matter coupling.”

## Recent results screened for relevance

### 4. Higher-curvature bulk inflation: new journal publication, different theory

Sueto, Yoshimoto and Cano, [Cosmic inflation from regular black holes](https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/3cy6-p27c), PRD **114**, 064023, published **8 September 2026**; [arXiv:2604.04601](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04601), first submitted **6 April 2026**. Primary journal abstract and arXiv full text checked.

The authors derive brane junction conditions in a gravity theory with an infinite tower of higher-curvature terms. Regular bulk black holes yield an early de Sitter regime controlled by the new curvature scale. The full-text conclusion explicitly leaves cosmological perturbations and a concrete reheating completion for further investigation.

**Use:** a separate branch for asking whether controlled modifications could replace a singular endpoint. **Limit:** these are not the Einstein–scalar field equations used in Chat 13; their junction conditions, perturbative degrees of freedom and cutoff questions would have to be rederived. This paper does not provide a ready-made radiation source or observational validation. Its September date is a journal-publication date, not the first appearance of the theory.

### 5. Warm brane inflation: useful consistency requirements, not a cold-start prescription

Yeasmin and Deshamukhya, [Warm inflation in a braneworld scenario](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjc/s10052-026-16156-3), EPJC **86**, 1013, published **27 August 2026**; [arXiv:2512.09389](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.09389), first submitted **10 December 2025**. Primary article checked, particularly sections 2 and 3 and the validity discussion surrounding equation (42).

It studies a natural-inflation potential with specified dissipation laws and compares its predictions to Planck/BICEP/BAO constraints. The numerical treatment assumes slow roll and a nearly thermal, quasi-stable radiation bath. The published fitted fluctuation function has a stated parameter range and model dependence.

**Use:** require thermalization, dissipation and background conditions to be checked together if a warm extension is built. **Limit:** importing its fitted fluctuation enhancement or a temperature-dependent friction law into an initially empty, rapidly rolling HDBLAST shell is not justified. It cannot establish how the first bath forms.

### 6. A September bounce proposal changes the signature of the extra dimension

Sengupta, [Cycles Without End: An Ekpyrotic Braneworld Bounce with a Kinetically Coupled Entropic Mechanism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06485), submitted **6 September 2026**. Primary abstract and model introduction checked; an unrefereed preprint in the accessed record.

The proposal combines a timelike extra dimension with a tailored potential and an additional entropy-field mechanism. These are material changes to the present spacelike-bulk, single-scalar setup. Its claimed bounce and perturbation predictions cannot be transferred to Chat 13. A model with an extra time direction would require its own assessment of causal evolution, spectrum and stability before consideration here.

**Disposition:** comparison only; do not use it to relabel the unresolved collapse as an established bounce. Its presence also demonstrates why a title or abstract promising a cyclic universe is insufficient evidence for this project.

### 7. Bulk leakage can make hidden matter, not necessarily dark radiation

Megías, Prieto and Quirós, [Massive Graviton Dark Matter from a Gapped Continuum](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07295), **8 July 2026**, revised **3 August 2026**. Primary full text equations (21), (22), (92)–(94) checked.

In its linear-dilaton warped background, a gapped graviton continuum acts as a holographic component that can redshift like matter. The brane-to-bulk energy-loss rate scales as \(T^8/M_5^3\) in the stated thermal regime. Its abundance is highly sensitive to the reheating temperature.

**Use:** include a separate bulk-loss ledger when constructing brane reheating, and derive the late equation of state of any hidden sector. **Limit:** neither its coefficient nor its gapped spectrum follows from the current bulk geometry. Do not automatically translate every bulk excitation into an extra relativistic-species bound.

### A. A coordinate test that isolates the clock problem

For the present metric

\[
ds^2=e^{2B(t,z)}(-dt^2+dz^2)+e^{2A(t,z)}d\mathbf{x}^2,
\]

set \(u=t-z\), \(v=t+z\), \(U=F(u)\), \(V=F(v)\), with \(F'>0\). The transformed conformal coordinates
\(T=(U+V)/2\), \(Z=(V-U)/2\) retain the single shell at \(Z=0\). Direct substitution gives

\[
\widetilde B=B-\tfrac12\log[F'(u)F'(v)],\qquad
\widetilde A=A,\qquad \widetilde\phi=\phi.
\]

On the shell choose \(F'(t)=e^{B_b(t)}\). Then \(\widetilde B_b=0\) and \(T=\tau_b+\text{constant}\). This is a local proper-clock gauge construction wherever the Jacobian is positive. Unlike a two-fixed-brane problem, there is no second-brane periodicity condition here.

This identity supplies a **test**, not a finished evolution algorithm. It cannot recover missing spacetime from the corrupted late data, and merely replacing the horizontal plot axis by proper time does not fix the PDE. Regrid or restart before the lapse degenerates, transform the initial hypersurface and derivative data consistently, and compare invariant curves \(H_b(\tau_b)\), \(\phi_b(\tau_b)\), curvature and constraint residuals over a shared reliable interval. A dynamically enforced clock gauge still needs a well-posed bulk formulation and boundary treatment.

The companion `PROPER_CLOCK_PROOF.md`, `verify_proper_clock.py` and `PROPER_CLOCK_EXACT_CHECKS.json` now give an exact local algebra check: the metric transformation, shell position, induced Hubble rate, proper derivatives, normal derivatives and the registered pure-tension junction residuals. All 54 checks pass using only the Python standard library. This is a symbolic coordinate check, not a numerical chart repair.

### B. A dimensionally explicit brane production test

An optional new brane interaction could be

\[
S_\chi=-\frac12\int_{\rm brane}\sqrt{-h}\,[h^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\chi\partial_\nu\chi+
(m_0^2+g_b^2(\Phi_b-\Phi_*)^2)\chi^2]\,d^4x.
\]

Here \(\Phi\) denotes a **dimensionful canonical bulk scalar**, not an automatic identification with the registered simulation variable \(\phi\). The exact local convention is stated in the header of [Chat 13's supplied equation derivation](</Users/ricardomaldonado/Documents/D-Blast 3/untitled folder 150/HDBLAST_CHAT13_NONLINEAR_ROLLOFF_20260921/derive_evolution_equations.py>): \(R_{AB}=\partial_A\phi\partial_B\phi+(2/3)U g_{AB}\). This is consistent with the normalization \(S_{\rm bulk}=(2\kappa_5^2)^{-1}\int\sqrt{-g}[R-(\partial\phi)^2-2U]d^5x\). In physical coordinates, \(\phi\) is dimensionless and \(\Phi=\phi/\kappa_5\) is canonical; \([\kappa_5]=\mathrm{mass}^{-3/2}\), so \([\Phi]=\mathrm{mass}^{3/2}\), \([g_b]=\mathrm{mass}^{-1/2}\), and \([\chi]=\mathrm{mass}\). This restoration is conditional on the specified action normalization; it fixes no numerical value of \(\kappa_5\) or the simulation's physical length unit. See the companion proof for the source hash and dimensional checks. Locally define \(q=g_b|d\Phi_b/d\tau_b|\), which has dimension mass squared. Necessary approximation checks include

\[
H_b/\sqrt q\ll1,\quad
\left|\frac{d^2\Phi_b/d\tau_b^2}{(d\Phi_b/d\tau_b)\sqrt q}\right|\ll1,\quad
\sqrt q,m_\chi<\Lambda_{\rm EFT}.
\]

The existing code’s scalar normalization and length unit must first be mapped to \(\Phi_b\) and proper time. That mapping is not supplied by a plot of \(\phi_b\) alone. Production must then modify the scalar boundary equation through the variation of this same interaction, and its renormalized stress tensor must enter the gravitational junction conditions. A light decay channel and interactions sufficient to redistribute momenta are further independent requirements. This is a well-defined extension target, not a successful reheating calculation.

### C. Keep three energy categories separate

Measure or evolve separately (i) energy remaining in the scalar and brane vacuum, (ii) energy in actual brane matter, and (iii) bulk scalar/Weyl excitations or outgoing flux. An \(a^{-4}\)-like correction to \(H^2\) does not alone identify photons or a thermal bath. The final positive vacuum energy also matters: even a successfully produced bath must dominate over it for an adequate radiation era. A four-dimensional low-energy Friedmann approximation may be used for this comparison only after its range is demonstrated for the evolving shell.
